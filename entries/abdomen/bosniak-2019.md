# Bosniak classification of cystic renal masses, version 2019

> Risk stratification of **cystic** renal masses seen at **CT or MRI**, in five classes
> (I, II, IIF, III, IV), from almost certainly benign to likely malignant. Version 2019 adds
> MRI criteria, defines previously vague terms (thin, minimally thickened, thick, nodule) and
> moves more masses into lower-risk classes. The authors present it as an **update proposal**
> that still needs validation. [bosniak-2019, abstract]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** cystic renal masses in adults at CT or MRI performed with a renal mass
  protocol (unenhanced + enhanced phases), including masses found incidentally. [verify]
- **Cystic mass:** less than ~25% enhancing tissue. A mass with more enhancing tissue is a
  **solid** mass and is not classified by Bosniak. [verify the exact threshold wording]
- **Does not apply to** (as stated by the authors): infectious, inflammatory or vascular
  lesions, and neoplasms in patients with hereditary renal cancer syndromes. [verify list]
- **Ultrasound:** not formally incorporated in v2019. B-mode cannot show enhancement, so it
  cannot assign IIF/III/IV. Contrast-enhanced US is discussed as a possible future
  addition. [verify]

| Enhancement (MRI) | Unequivocal enhancement on subtraction images (qualitative) | [verify] |
| Simple fluid (CT) | −9 to 20 HU | [verify] |
| Simple fluid (MRI) | Signal similar to cerebrospinal fluid | [verify] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Class | CT | MRI | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Well-defined, thin (≤ 2 mm) smooth wall; homogeneous simple fluid (−9 to 20 HU); no septa or calcification; the wall may enhance | Same morphology; fluid similar to CSF | [verify] |
| **II** | Any of: (a) well-defined, thin smooth wall with **few (1–3) thin** septa (wall/septa may enhance; calcification of any type allowed); (b) **homogeneous hyperattenuating (≥ 70 HU)** at unenhanced CT; (c) homogeneous **non-enhancing** mass > 20 HU at renal-mass-protocol CT (calcification allowed); (d) homogeneous −9 to 20 HU at unenhanced CT; (e) homogeneous **21–30 HU at portal venous phase**; (f) homogeneous low-attenuation mass **too small to characterize** | Any of: (a) few thin septa as at CT; (b) homogeneous, markedly **T2-hyperintense** (similar to CSF) at unenhanced MRI; (c) homogeneous, markedly **T1-hyperintense** (about ≥ 2.5× normal parenchyma) at unenhanced fat-suppressed MRI | [verify each subtype] |
| **IIF** | Smooth **minimally thickened (3 mm)** enhancing wall, **or** smooth minimal thickening of one or more enhancing septa, **or** **many (≥ 4)** smooth thin enhancing septa | Same; also cystic masses **heterogeneously** hyperintense at unenhanced fat-suppressed T1 | [verify] |
| **III** | One or more enhancing **thick (≥ 4 mm)** or enhancing **irregular** (≤ 3 mm obtuse protrusion) walls or septa | Same | [verify] |
| **IV** | One or more enhancing **nodules** (≥ 4 mm obtuse, or any size with acute margins) | Same | [verify] |

Calcification by itself does not change the class in v2019; heavy calcification can hide
enhancement, and MRI may then help. [verify]

## 4. Management

| Class | What the source suggests | Source |
|---|---|---|
| I, II | No follow-up | [verify] |
| IIF | Imaging follow-up, typically at 6 and 12 months, then yearly for a total of 5 years; upgrade if new or increased enhancing features | [verify intervals] |
| III | Treatment or active surveillance, shared decision with the patient and urology | [verify] |
| IV | Treatment, or active surveillance in selected patients | [verify] |

Expected malignancy rates per class are discussed in the paper (low for I–IIF, around half
for III, high for IV). **Do not quote percentages until checked** against the full text. [verify]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Applying Bosniak to a **solid** mass (≥ ~25% enhancing tissue). [verify]
- Assigning IIF/III/IV from **B-mode ultrasound** (no enhancement information).
- Measuring enhancement with a single small ROI or in a thin septum (partial volume,
  pseudoenhancement) — compare with the incidental renal mass white paper's ROI rules.
  [acr-renal-2018]
- Treating a "too small to characterize" lesion as indeterminate when it is homogeneous and
  low attenuation (class II). [verify]

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does **not** classify masses at B-mode US.
- It is a **proposal requiring validation**, not a validated final system. [abstract]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- The ACR incidental renal mass white paper (2018) predates v2019 and uses the original
  classification; where they differ, v2019 is the newer definition of the classes.
  [acr-renal-2018]
- Contrast-enhanced ultrasound proposals (CEUS Bosniak) exist; not part of v2019. [verify]
