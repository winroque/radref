# LI-RADS Ultrasound Surveillance v2024

> Standardized reporting of **surveillance** ultrasound for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) in
> patients at high risk. Two whole-liver assessments: a **US category** (US-1 negative, US-2
> subthreshold, US-3 positive) and a **visualization score** (VIS-A, VIS-B, VIS-C). Aligned
> with AASLD 2023 guidance. [acr-us-lirads-2024]

## 1. Scope

- **Population** (aligned with AASLD 2023): adults with Child-Turcotte-Pugh A or B cirrhosis of
  any cause; subsets of adults with chronic hepatitis B without cirrhosis (men > 40 or women
  > 50 from an endemic country, those from Africa at an earlier age, family history of HCC,
  PAGE-B ≥ 10 in Western patients on antivirals). Other regions may include further HBV, HCV or
  MASH subsets. [acr-us-lirads-2024]
- **Not recommended:** Child-Turcotte-Pugh C cirrhosis not eligible for transplant, or life
  expectancy < 1–2 years from comorbidities. [acr-us-lirads-2024]
- **Setting:** outpatients whenever possible; in emergency or inpatients only after acute
  conditions resolve, and then with caution. [acr-us-lirads-2024]
- The surveillance population is not identical to the CT/MRI/CEUS diagnostic population (e.g.
  cirrhosis from vascular disorders may be surveilled, but CT/MRI LI-RADS does not apply).
  [acr-us-lirads-2024]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AFP positive | AFP **≥ 20 ng/mL**, or doubling (or more), or increase on two consecutive tests (aligned with AASLD) | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| Tiebreak | If unsure between two categories, choose the **more suspicious** one (surveillance favours sensitivity) | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### US category

| Category | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **US-1** Negative | No observation, or only definitely benign observation(s) | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| **US-2** Subthreshold | Observation(s) **< 10 mm**, not definitely benign | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| **US-3** Positive | Observation(s) **≥ 10 mm**, not definitely benign, including area(s) of parenchymal distortion; **or** new thrombus in a portal or hepatic vein | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |

### Visualization score

| Score | Definition | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VIS-A** | No or minimal limitations, unlikely to affect sensitivity | Liver homogeneous or mildly heterogeneous; minimal attenuation/shadowing; liver seen in near entirety | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| **VIS-B** | Moderate limitations that may obscure small (< 10 mm) observations | Heterogeneity that may affect detection; moderate attenuation/shadowing; some liver or diaphragm not seen | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| **VIS-C** | Severe limitations that significantly lower sensitivity | Severely heterogeneous liver; severe attenuation/shadowing; **> 50%** of right or left lobe, or of the diaphragm, not seen | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |

## 4. Management

Applied in order: [acr-us-lirads-2024, management page]

| Result | Management | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US-3 | Diagnostic MRI, CT or CEUS | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| AFP positive (not US-3) | Diagnostic MRI or CT (CEUS unlikely to help) | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| VIS-C | Repeat US within 3 months once, **or** alternative surveillance modality (e.g. abbreviated MRI, multiphase CT) — favoured with MASH or alcohol-related cirrhosis, CTP B/C, or BMI ≥ 35; if still VIS-C, change strategy | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| US-2 (VIS-A/B) | Repeat US in 3–6 months, up to 2 times; if no longer seen or still < 10 mm → recategorize as US-1 and return to 6-month surveillance | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |
| US-1 (VIS-A/B) | Repeat US in 6 months | [acr-us-lirads-2024] |

Alternative surveillance is **not** recommended after VIS-A or VIS-B (sensitivity > 75%).
[acr-us-lirads-2024]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Reporting a category per observation: both assessments refer to the **whole liver**.
- Scoring an inpatient exam done during an acute illness as VIS-C and labelling the patient a
  poor US candidate.
- Recommending CEUS for a positive AFP without a US-3 finding.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not apply the CT/MRI diagnostic categories (LR-1 to LR-5) to surveillance US.
- It does not choose between full MRI, abbreviated MRI and multiphase CT as the alternative
  after VIS-C (no evidence yet).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- **Changes in v2024** (vs v2017): name "LI-RADS Ultrasound Surveillance"; scores renamed
  VIS-A/B/C; VIS-C → repeat within 3 months or alternative modality; US-2 recategorized to US-1
  after two follow-ups; positive AFP → diagnostic imaging; outpatient emphasis; MASLD/MASH
  nomenclature. [acr-us-lirads-2024]
- The 2018 article describing US LI-RADS v2017 is superseded for the algorithm.
