# Incidental splenic and nodal findings (ACR white paper, 2013)

> General approach, with little literature behind it, to incidental splenic lesions and
> abdominal/pelvic lymph nodes on CT or MRI; one paragraph on sclerosing mesenteritis.
> Guidance, not a standard of care. [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]

## 1. Scope

- **Spleen:** splenic lesion in an **asymptomatic** patient. A symptomatic mass, or a
  metastatic lesion found at staging, is not incidental. [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]
- **Nodes:** abdominal and pelvic lymph nodes found incidentally. [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]

## 2. Definitions

### Spleen

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cyst | Imperceptible wall, near water (**< 10 HU**), no enhancement | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Haemangioma | Peripheral, discontinuous, centripetal enhancement (rare in spleen; often only delayed enhancement) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Benign features | Homogeneous, low attenuation (**< 20 HU**), no enhancement, smooth margins | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious features | Heterogeneous, enhancing, irregular margins, necrosis, parenchymal or vascular invasion, substantial enlargement | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Indeterminate features | Heterogeneous, intermediate attenuation (**> 20 HU**), enhancing, smooth margins | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Dense thick wall calcification (**> 5 mm**) in near-water lesion | Old haematoma or post-traumatic cyst; benign | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| "Evaluate" | PET, MRI or biopsy (biopsy seldom indicated; surgical back-up first) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |

### Lymph nodes

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Benign | Short axis **< 1 cm in the retroperitoneum**, normal architecture (oval, fatty hilum), normal enhancement, normal number | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Abnormal | Short axis **≥ 1 cm in the retroperitoneum**; abnormal architecture (round, indistinct hilum); abnormal enhancement (necrosis or hypervascularity); increased number (**≥ 3 clustered nodes in one station**, or **≥ 2 clustered in ≥ 2 regions** — gastrohepatic ligament, retroperitoneum, mesentery) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |

The 1 cm threshold is safest in the retroperitoneum; the paper acknowledges that normal nodes
are smaller in the **retrocrural** space and **porta hepatis**. Normal-size nodes can be
diseased (low-grade lymphoma). [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Spleen

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic benign features (cyst, typical haemangioma, benign features) | No follow-up | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Non-diagnostic, prior imaging, stable **≥ 1 year** | No follow-up | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Non-diagnostic, prior imaging, not stable | Evaluate (PET, MRI or biopsy) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| No prior, **no cancer**, suspicious | Evaluate | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| No prior, **no cancer**, indeterminate | **MRI at 6 and 12 months** | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| No prior, **cancer**, **< 1 cm** | Imaging follow-up (MRI at 6 and 12 months) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013, Fig. 1 (checked on the rendered flowchart)] |
| No prior, **cancer**, **≥ 1 cm** | Evaluate (suspect metastasis if > 1 cm) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |

### Lymph nodes

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Benign features | No follow-up | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious, prior imaging, stable **≥ 1 year** | Benign, no follow-up | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious, prior imaging, not stable | Biopsy or other evaluation (PET-CT, scintigraphy, EUS) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious, no prior, **known neoplasm** | Biopsy or other evaluation (if the tumour drains there, it is not incidental) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious, no prior, no neoplasm, clinical/lab pointing to **lymphoproliferative** disease | Biopsy of a representative accessible node | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Suspicious, no prior, no neoplasm, no clinical/lab features suggesting a lymphoproliferative disorder | **CT or MRI in ~3 months** (with a known non-neoplastic condition: re-image after treatment) | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |
| Underlying inflammatory/infectious disease (e.g. pancreatitis) | Follow-up after treatment; probably secondary | [acr-spleen-nodes-2013] |

## 5. Pitfalls

- Lymphangiomatosis mimics metastases; MRI helps (high T2, no enhancement, subcapsular,
  multiloculated). [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]
- Sclerosing mesenteritis is often asymptomatic; the "fat halo sign" helps to distinguish it
  from metastases, lymphoma and desmoid. [acr-spleen-nodes-2013]

## 6. What the source does not say

- It gives no station-by-station normal node sizes; it cites Dorfman 1991 and lists
  the small-node stations without giving the values (see
  `abdominal-lymph-node-size-ct-dorfman-1991`).
