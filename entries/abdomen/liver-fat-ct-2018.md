# Liver fat fraction from unenhanced CT (Pickhardt 2018)

> Linear conversion of mean liver attenuation on **unenhanced** CT (HU) into an estimated
> MRI proton density fat fraction (PDFF), derived from phantom and clinical correlation.
> [pickhardt-liver-fat-2018]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** unenhanced CT. [pickhardt-liver-fat-2018]
- **Does not apply to:** contrast-enhanced phases. [verify any other exclusions, e.g. kVp,
  iron overload, amiodarone]

## 2. Definitions

- **PDFF:** MRI proton density fat fraction.

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Formula | Source |
|---|---|
| Estimated PDFF (%) ≈ −0.58 × HU + 38.2 | [pickhardt-liver-fat-2018] [verify coefficients and ROI method in the full text] |

Worked values (arithmetic from the formula): 57 HU ≈ 5%; 40 HU ≈ 15%; 20 HU ≈ 27%.
