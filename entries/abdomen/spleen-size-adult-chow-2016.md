# Adult spleen size (Chow 2016)

> In 1,230 healthy stem-cell donors, spleen length and volume depended on **sex and height**
> (and weight). The classic 12-cm limit was exceeded by **26% of men** and 6% of women.
> Height-adjusted upper limits (95th percentile) for length: **women 0.0282 × height (cm) +
> 7.5526**; **men 0.0544 × height (cm) + 3.6693** (cm). [chow-spleen-size-2016]

## 1. Scope

- **Valid for:** women 155–179 cm and men 165–199 cm tall (German, predominantly white, healthy
  donors). [chow-spleen-size-2016]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Maximum length on a longitudinal intercostal view through the hilum (posterior axillary line, about the 10th rib), supine | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |
| Volume | 0.52 × length × width × anteroposterior dimension | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Measure | Women | Men | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median length (5th–95th) | 10.0 cm (8.1–12.1) | 11.3 cm (9.0–13.5) | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |
| Median volume (5th–95th) | 134 cm³ (64–231) | 179 cm³ (90–334) | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |
| Upper limit, length (cm) | 0.0282 × H + 7.5526 | 0.0544 × H + 3.6693 | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |
| Upper limit, volume (cm³) | 7.0996 × H − 939.5 | 4.3803 × H − 457.15 | [chow-spleen-size-2016] |

H = body height in cm. Example (computed from the formula): a 180-cm man → about 13.5 cm.

- The formulae classified 95–96% of these healthy volunteers as not enlarged; repeat
  measurements 6 months or more apart were stable (bias about 0.04 cm). [chow-spleen-size-2016]
- 13- and 14-cm cut-offs are above the limit for all women < 180 cm and men < 170 cm (13 cm) or
  < 190 cm (14 cm), so they cause false negatives. [chow-spleen-size-2016]

## 4. Management

- Not addressed. A free app (SplenoCalc) computes percentiles. [chow-spleen-size-2016]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Calling splenomegaly at 12 cm in a tall man.
- Using the formulae outside the validated height range.

## 6. What the source does not say

- Not validated in other ethnicities or in Brazil; BMI effect noted but not included in the
  formulae.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Internal inconsistency: the abstract reports 234 of 906 men (26%) above 12 cm, while one
  results sentence prints "23 of 906 men (26%)" (typo).
