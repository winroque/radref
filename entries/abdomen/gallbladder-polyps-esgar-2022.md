# Gallbladder polyps — European guideline 2022

> **≥ 10 mm → cholecystectomy** (if fit and willing). **6–9 mm with ≥ 1 risk factor →
> cholecystectomy.** **6–9 mm without risk factors, or ≤ 5 mm with risk factors → US at 6 months,
> 1 year and 2 years.** **≤ 5 mm without risk factors → no follow-up.** Risk factors: age > 60,
> primary sclerosing cholangitis, Asian ethnicity, sessile polyp (including focal wall thickening
> > 4 mm). [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** polypoid lesions of the gallbladder found at transabdominal US in adults; the
  recommendations are based on transabdominal US. [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]
- **Does not apply to:** pseudopolyps with reverberation/comet-tail artefact (adenomyomatosis,
  cholesterolosis); large or infiltrative masses, which are managed as cancer.
  [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]
- Monitoring is reserved for patients who are potentially fit for surgery.
  [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Polyp | Elevation of the gallbladder wall that protrudes into the lumen, is not mobile and has no acoustic shadow | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| Multiple polyps | Use the size of the largest | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| Risk factors for malignancy | Age > 60 years, PSC, Asian ethnicity, sessile polypoid lesion (including focal wall thickening > 4 mm). A solitary polyp adds to the risk when ≥ 1 factor is present, but was not put on the list | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Polyp | Risk factors | Recommendation | Strength / evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 10 mm | Any | Cholecystectomy (MDT discussion may help) | Strong / low | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| Any size with attributable symptoms | — | Cholecystectomy suggested if no other cause | Strong / low | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| 6–9 mm | ≥ 1 | Cholecystectomy | Strong / low–moderate | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| 6–9 mm | None | US at 6 months, 1 and 2 years; stop after 2 years without growth | Moderate / moderate | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| ≤ 5 mm | ≥ 1 | US at 6 months, 1 and 2 years | Moderate / moderate | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |
| ≤ 5 mm | None | No follow-up | Strong / moderate | [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022] |

- During follow-up: growth to 10 mm → cholecystectomy; growth ≥ 2 mm within 2 years → weigh
  current size and risk factors (MDT); disappearance → stop. [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]
- Primary imaging is abdominal US; contrast-enhanced or endoscopic US may help in difficult cases
  at expert centres. [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]

## 4. Management

- As above; surgery only if the patient is fit and accepts it.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Mixing European (≥ 10 mm surgery) and SRU 2022 (risk tiers by morphology, longer thresholds)
  recommendations in one report; state which guideline is used.
- Applying the guideline to comet-tail pseudopolyps. [esgar-gallbladder-polyps-2022]

## 6. What the source does not say

- No polyp-morphology tiers comparable to SRU's "extremely low risk" pedunculated ball-on-the-wall
  category.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- SRU 2022 (`sru-gallbladder-polyps-2022`) is more conservative about surgery for small polyps;
  the two documents disagree and neither cites a resolution.
