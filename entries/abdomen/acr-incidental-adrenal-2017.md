# Incidental adrenal mass (ACR white paper, 2017)

> Consensus algorithm for adrenal masses found incidentally on CT or MRI in asymptomatic
> adults: which findings are diagnostic of benignity, when to perform adrenal-protocol CT,
> and when to consider resection, biopsy or PET-CT. Guidance, not a standard of care.
> [acr-adrenal-2017]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adults (≥ 18 years), **asymptomatic**, imaged for an unrelated reason.
  Bilateral lesions: assess each one. With or without cancer history (it changes the branch).
  [acr-adrenal-2017]
- Masses **< 1 cm in short axis** (including subcentimetric "thickening" or "nodularity")
  need no work-up. [acr-adrenal-2017]
- Multiple metastases elsewhere: adrenal work-up rarely changes management. Patients who are
  not treatment candidates or have limited life expectancy may not need work-up.
  [acr-adrenal-2017]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S, P, T | Attenuation (HU) unenhanced, portal venous (60–90 s), 15-min delayed | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Absolute washout | (P − T) / (P − S) × 100; **≥ 60%** = adenoma | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Relative washout | (P − T) / P × 100 (no unenhanced phase); **≥ 40%** = adenoma | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Adrenal-protocol CT | Unenhanced (read at once; stop if ≤ 10 HU or fat) → portal 60–90 s → 15-min delayed; 3 mm axial and coronal; unenhanced at low dose with fixed **120 kVp** | [acr-adrenal-2017] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Diagnostic of benignity → no follow-up

| Finding | Diagnosis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Macroscopic fat | Myelolipoma | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| No enhancement (< 10 HU change; needs both phases) | Cyst or haemorrhage | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Benign calcified mass (old haematoma, granulomatous disease) | Benign | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| **≤ 10 HU unenhanced CT**, or signal drop on opposed-phase chemical-shift MRI (vs spleen) | Lipid-rich adenoma, **any size** | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Dual-energy CT virtual unenhanced ≤ 10 HU | May be used for adenoma (VNC may overestimate; vendor-dependent) | [acr-adrenal-2017] |

## 4. Management

### Indeterminate mass ≥ 4 cm

| Cancer history | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| No | **Surgical resection (without biopsy) is recommended** — possible adrenocortical carcinoma | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Yes | Proceed to PET-CT or biopsy (text: "should proceed") | [acr-adrenal-2017, PET/CT section] |

### Indeterminate mass 1 to < 4 cm

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Prior imaging, stable ≥ 1 year | Benign, no follow-up | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Prior imaging, new/enlarging, no cancer | Biochemical evaluation and, depending on growth rate, resection without biopsy (text); the flowchart, an image, could not be checked for "consider adrenal CT" | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| Prior imaging, new/enlarging, cancer | Consider biopsy or PET-CT | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| No prior, no cancer, 1–2 cm | Probably benign; consider adrenal CT at **12 months** | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| No prior, no cancer, > 2 and < 4 cm | Adrenal-protocol CT now | [acr-adrenal-2017] |
| No prior, cancer, isolated adrenal mass | Adrenal-protocol CT now; central necrosis raises suspicion of metastasis (consider biopsy or PET-CT) | [acr-adrenal-2017] |

### After adrenal-protocol CT

- Washout above thresholds → adenoma.
- Below thresholds → imaging follow-up, biopsy, PET-CT or resection depending on the scenario;
  without cancer and no benign diagnosis: CT in 6–12 months or resection. [acr-adrenal-2017]
- Very avid enhancement (> 110–120 HU) → consider pheochromocytoma; catecholamines /
  metanephrines. [acr-adrenal-2017]

### Biochemical evaluation

- Consider functional evaluation for **most** incidental adrenal masses (previously only with
  hypertension or clinical signs), and **before biopsy or resection** to exclude
  pheochromocytoma. Test choice belongs to the clinician. [acr-adrenal-2017]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Unenhanced kVp other than 120 changes HU.
- Chemical-shift MRI may leave lipid-poor adenomas (> 20–30 HU unenhanced) indeterminate;
  the paper considers washout CT superior. [acr-adrenal-2017]
- No growth-rate threshold separates benign from malignant. [acr-adrenal-2017]

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not set a size above which a lipid-rich adenoma (≤ 10 HU) needs follow-up.
- It does not mandate a test panel (detailed review is out of scope); it cites one
  endocrinologist approach: dexamethasone suppression test and plasma metanephrines for all, 24-h
  urine if equivocal, renin/aldosterone if hypertensive; plasma fractionated metanephrines
  before biopsy if phaeochromocytoma is possible. [acr-adrenal-2017, Endocrine Evaluation]
