# Incidental renal mass on CT (ACR white paper, 2018)

> Flowcharts for renal masses found incidentally on CT in asymptomatic adults: unenhanced CT,
> contrast-enhanced CT, characterized cystic masses (Bosniak, pre-2019 version), solid or
> too-small-to-characterize masses, and fat-containing masses. Published online 2017, in print
> 2018. Guidance, not a standard of care. [acr-renal-2018]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adults (≥ 18 years), asymptomatic, imaged for reasons other than urinary
  disease. [acr-renal-2018]
- **Does not apply to:** syndromes predisposing to renal neoplasia; primary cancers likely to
  metastasize to the kidney (lung, lymphoma, melanoma); infiltrative renal processes; masses
  with adenopathy/metastases or so typical of RCC that they go straight to management.
  [acr-renal-2018]
- Before applying: confirm renal (not perirenal) origin; exclude pseudomass (column of Bertin,
  scar), renal artery aneurysm, calyceal diverticulum, infection/abscess. [acr-renal-2018]
- Limited life expectancy, comorbidity or high surgical risk: treatment may become
  surveillance, or no work-up (shared decision). [acr-renal-2018]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ROI | Several ROIs in different parts of the mass; never a single pixel | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Fat | ROI **< −10 HU** → fat flowchart | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Heterogeneous | Wall thickening, ≥ 1 septum, mural nodule, measurable or visible attenuation difference, calcification | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Too small to characterize (TSTC) | Smaller than 2× the reconstructed slice thickness | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Enhancement, CT | **≥ 20 HU** definite; **> 10 and < 20 HU** equivocal (pseudoenhancement; use 15 HU in exophytic or larger lesions); **≤ 10 HU** none | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Enhancement, MRI | Signal increase **≥ 15%**, or visible on subtraction | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Stable | No morphologic change **and** mean growth **≤ 3 mm/year for ≥ 5 years** | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Growth (solid mass) | **≥ 4 mm/year** on average | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Morphologic change | Any increase in heterogeneity (contour, attenuation, number of septa) | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Size | Maximum diameter in any plane (TNM 7) | [acr-renal-2018] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Flowchart 1 — unenhanced CT

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Homogeneous, **−10 to 20 HU** | Probable cyst, no work-up (some members prefer 15 HU as upper limit) | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Homogeneous, **21–69 HU** | Indeterminate → MRI or unenhanced + enhanced CT | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Homogeneous, **≥ 70 HU** | Haemorrhagic/proteinaceous cyst, no work-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Heterogeneous | Indeterminate → MRI or unenhanced + enhanced CT | [acr-renal-2018] |
| TSTC, much lower or higher than parenchyma, well-defined, homogeneous | Probable cyst, no work-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| TSTC, subjectively inconclusive | MRI (preferred) or CT in **6–12 months** | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Homogeneous **−20 to −10 HU** | Consider MRI or US to exclude cyst | [acr-renal-2018] |

### Flowchart 2 — contrast-enhanced CT

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Homogeneous, **−10 to 20 HU** | Simple cyst, no work-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Homogeneous, **> 20 HU** | Indeterminate (hyperdense cyst vs solid) → MRI or unenhanced + enhanced CT | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Heterogeneous | MRI or unenhanced + enhanced CT | [acr-renal-2018] |
| TSTC, much lower than enhanced parenchyma | Probable cyst, no work-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| TSTC, inconclusive | MRI (preferred) or CT in 6–12 months | [acr-renal-2018] |

MRI is preferred for masses **< 1.5 cm** and for detecting enhancement in hypovascular
lesions; US can characterize a homogeneous mass as haemorrhagic/proteinaceous cyst.

### Flowchart 3 — characterized cystic mass (Bosniak, pre-2019 version)

| Bosniak | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| I, II | Benign, no follow-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| IIF | Surveillance with CT or MRI "first semiannually then annually, for a minimum of 5 years"; no change in imaging features for ≥ 5 years = stable. Growth without morphologic change does **not** indicate malignancy; morphologic change (more septa, thicker wall/septa, solid nodular component, reclassification as III/IV) → refer | [acr-renal-2018] |
| III, IV | Refer for treatment (malignancy ~54% in III); surveillance of III acceptable with limited life expectancy or high surgical risk | [acr-renal-2018] |

### Flowchart 4 — solid mass or TSTC at unenhanced + enhanced CT/MRI

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Solid **< 1.0 cm** | Active surveillance with semiannual then annual MRI or CT until growth to > 1 cm, then refer (~60% malignant at pathology but likely indolent; some masses stay on surveillance to 2–3 cm) | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Solid **1.0–4.0 cm** | Refer; consider biopsy (~80% malignant) | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Solid **> 4.0 cm** | Refer (~90% malignant) | [acr-renal-2018] |

Biopsy especially if the mass enhances and is hyperattenuating unenhanced or T2-hypointense
(fat-poor angiomyolipoma), notably in young women. Biopsy is of little use in cystic masses.

### Flowchart 5 — fat-containing mass (ROI < −10 HU)

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fat **without** calcification, solitary, no documented growth, **< 4 cm**, asymptomatic | Sporadic AML, no work-up | [acr-renal-2018] |
| AML **≥ 4 cm** or aneurysm **> 5 mm** | Refer for prophylactic treatment | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Multiple AMLs or growth | Refer; multiple may warrant evaluation for tuberous sclerosis | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Symptomatic AML | Urology, any size | [acr-renal-2018] |
| Fat **with** calcification | Suspect RCC → unenhanced + enhanced CT or MRI | [acr-renal-2018] |

## 5. Pitfalls

- Pseudoenhancement in small intrarenal lesions (10–20 HU change).
- Measuring a single pixel.
- Applying Bosniak to B-mode ultrasound (enhancement cannot be assessed).

## 6. What the source does not say

- It uses the **pre-2019** Bosniak classification; it does not include v2019 definitions or MRI
  criteria.
- It does not give a growth threshold that indicates malignancy in IIF cysts.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- **Bosniak v2019** (`bosniak-2019`) redefined cystic mass categories for CT and MRI.
- In the paper, calcification has less weight and size does not enter the cystic categories.
