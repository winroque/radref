# Incidental pancreatic cyst (ACR white paper, 2017)

> Size- and age-based imaging surveillance for asymptomatic incidental pancreatic cysts, with
> worrisome features and high-risk stigmata that trigger EUS/FNA and surgical evaluation.
> Guidance, not a standard of care. [acr-pancreas-2017]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adults (≥ 18 years), asymptomatic, single or multiple cysts.
  [acr-pancreas-2017]
- **Does not apply** with possibly related signs/symptoms (jaundice, anorexia, weight loss,
  palpable mass, steatorrhoea) or relevant laboratory abnormalities (e.g. raised amylase).
  Abdominal pain: radiologist's judgement. A patient who becomes symptomatic leaves the
  algorithm (surgical evaluation). Not a surgical candidate: do not follow (explicit for
  ≥ 80 years). [acr-pancreas-2017]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Presumed mucinous | Every incidental cyst is presumed mucinous (e.g. IPMN) unless imaging or aspiration proves another histology | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| Size | One measurement: longest axis on axial or coronal, with series/image | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| Worrisome features | Cyst **≥ 3 cm**; thickened/enhancing wall; non-enhancing mural nodule; MPD **≥ 7 mm** (ACR simplifies Fukuoka's 5–9 mm to a single 7 mm cut-off) | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| High-risk stigmata | Obstructive jaundice with head cyst; enhancing solid component; MPD **≥ 10 mm** without other cause | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| Growth (longest axis) | Cyst **< 5 mm** → **100%** increase; **5 to < 15 mm** → **50%**; **≥ 15 mm** → **20%** | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| Aggressive growth rate | > 2 mm/year suggests aggressive behaviour | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| High risk by imaging (> 2.5 cm chart) | Mural nodule, wall thickening, MPD ≥ 7 mm, peripheral calcification | [acr-pancreas-2017] |

General trigger for immediate EUS/FNA and surgical evaluation (figure legends): mural nodule,
wall thickening, MPD ≥ 7 mm, or extrahepatic biliary obstruction/jaundice. Exception: a cyst
≥ 3 cm with no other feature may be followed. [acr-pancreas-2017]

## 3. Criteria / categories

Total follow-up is typically **9–10 years**, ending at **80 years**. A growing cyst changes
chart but usually does **not** restart the 9–10-year count. [acr-pancreas-2017]

### Cyst < 1.5 cm

| Age at detection | Schedule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| < 65 years | Yearly × 5; if stable, every 2 years × 2; stable ≥ 9 years → stop | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| 65–79 years | Every 2 years × 5; stable 10 years → stop | [acr-pancreas-2017] |

Growth but still < 1.5 cm → yearly imaging or EUS/FNA; stop if < 1.5 cm for ≥ 10 years.
Growth to ≥ 1.5 cm → next chart or EUS/FNA. T2 "white dot" (< 5 mm): one stable control at
2 years is enough; some do not report them above 75–80 years. [acr-pancreas-2017]

### Cyst 1.5–2.5 cm

| Situation | Schedule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MPD communication (branch-duct IPMN), 1.5–1.9 cm | Yearly × 5, then every 2 years × 2; stable 9 years → stop. Growth ≤ 2.5 cm → every 6 months × 4, yearly × 2, then every 2 years (or EUS/FNA); stop if ≤ 2.5 cm for 10 years. Growth > 2.5 cm → EUS/FNA | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| MPD communication, 2.0–2.5 cm | Every 6 months × 4, yearly × 2, every 2 years × 3; stable 10 years → stop. Growth → EUS/FNA | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| No/indeterminate communication | Every 6 months × 4, yearly × 2, every 2 years × 3; stable 10 years → stop. Growth ≤ 2.5 cm → every 6 months × 2, yearly × 5, then every 2 years (or EUS/FNA). Growth > 2.5 cm → EUS/FNA. Or EUS/FNA at once: serous, cystic NET, pseudocyst → manage by diagnosis; mucinous/indeterminate → same imaging schedule, surgery if growth or worrisome features | [acr-pancreas-2017] |

EUS/FNA at detection is an alternative to follow-up.

### Cyst > 2.5 cm

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Serous cystadenoma | According to symptoms; **> 4 cm** → surgical evaluation | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| Low risk by imaging | Every 6 months × 4; stable → yearly × 2, then every 2 years × 3; stable 10 years → stop. Growth → EUS/FNA + surgical evaluation | [acr-pancreas-2017] |
| High risk by imaging | EUS/FNA + surgical evaluation now | [acr-pancreas-2017] |

The paper recommends EUS/FNA before any resection.

### Age ≥ 80 years at detection

Follow only surgical candidates who wish it. ≤ 2.5 cm → every 2 years × 2; stable → stop;
growth ≤ 2.5 cm → yearly; > 2.5 cm → EUS/FNA. > 2.5 cm: serous (> 4 cm surgical evaluation);
low risk → every 2 years × 2; high risk → EUS/FNA + surgical evaluation. [acr-pancreas-2017]

## 4. Management

Imaging method for follow-up: contrast MRI with MRCP or pancreatic-protocol CT (pancreatic
phase ~50 s and portal ~80 s, thin slices). MRI avoids cumulative radiation; abbreviated MRI
(T2 + contrast) is acceptable for follow-up. [acr-pancreas-2017]

What to report: morphology and location; size; MPD communication and largest MPD calibre;
worrisome features/high-risk stigmata; growth (and rate); multiplicity (largest is the index,
all are assessed). Central calcification suggests serous; peripheral, mucinous cystic
neoplasm. [acr-pancreas-2017]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Restarting the 10-year count after growth (the paper says usually not).
- Applying the algorithm to symptomatic patients.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not incorporate later consensus documents (revised Fukuoka, AGA, European).
