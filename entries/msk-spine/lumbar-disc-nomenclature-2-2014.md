# Lumbar disc nomenclature 2.0 (2014)

> **Herniation:** localized (< 25% of the circumference in the axial plane) displacement of disc
> material beyond the disc space. **Protrusion:** in every plane, the displaced material is
> narrower than its base. **Extrusion:** in at least one plane it is wider than its base, or has
> lost continuity. **Sequestration:** extrusion with complete loss of continuity. **Bulge**
> (> 25% of the circumference, usually < 3 mm) is **not** a herniation. Use **annular fissure**,
> not "annular tear". [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** reporting lumbar disc morphology on CT and MRI (radiologists and clinicians).

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Disc space | Bounded craniocaudally by the end plates and peripherally by the outer edges of the ring apophyses, excluding osteophytes | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Localized / focal | < 25% (90°) of the disc periphery in the axial plane | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Bulge (symmetric) | Disc tissue beyond the apophyses throughout the circumference, usually < 3 mm; a contour description, not a diagnosis | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Asymmetric bulge | > 25% of the circumference, more on one side, not focal enough for protrusion; not a herniation | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Protrusion | Greatest distance between edges of the displaced material < distance between the edges of its base, in any plane | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Extrusion | In at least one plane, displaced material wider than its base; or no continuity with the disc | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Sequestration | Extruded material with complete loss of continuity with the parent disc | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Migration | Displacement of extruded material away from the extrusion site | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Intravertebral herniation | Herniation through the end plate (Schmorl node) | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |
| Annular fissure | Separation between annular fibers or from their attachments; preferred to "annular tear", which wrongly implies injury | [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

- Herniations may be further described as contained (covered by outer annulus) or uncontained.
  [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014]

## 4. Management

- Not a management document.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Calling a broad symmetric bulge a "herniation".
- Using "annular tear" in medicolegal contexts (implies trauma). [lumbar-nomenclature-2-2014]

## 6. What the source does not say

- No size cut-offs for stenosis grading.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Replaces the 2001 nomenclature (Spine 2001).
