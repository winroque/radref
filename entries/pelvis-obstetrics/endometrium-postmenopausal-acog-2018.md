# Postmenopausal endometrium (ACOG CO 734, 2018)

> With postmenopausal bleeding, a thin, distinct endometrial echo **≤ 4 mm** on transvaginal US
> has a negative predictive value > 99% for endometrial cancer and is an acceptable first test.
> Persistent or recurrent bleeding needs histology whatever the thickness. A thickness > 4 mm
> found incidentally **without bleeding** need not routinely trigger evaluation; TVUS is not a
> screening test. [acog-co734-2018]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** postmenopausal women, with or without bleeding, examined by transvaginal US.
- **Does not cover:** premenopausal bleeding, tamoxifen users or hormone therapy specifically
  (not addressed in this opinion).

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Endometrial thickness | Maximum anteroposterior thickness of the endometrial echo on a long-axis (midsagittal) transvaginal view, at its thickest part, between the echogenic borders, perpendicular to the longitudinal plane | [acog-co734-2018, Fig. 1] |
| Fluid | Endometrial fluid is **not** included in the measurement | [acog-co734-2018] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Situation | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bleeding, thin echo ≤ 4 mm | NPV > 99% for endometrial cancer; TVUS appropriate as initial evaluation | [acog-co734-2018] |
| Bleeding, echo not adequately seen | Failure to identify a thin, distinct echo → sonohysterography, office hysteroscopy or endometrial sampling | [acog-co734-2018] |
| No bleeding, echo > 4 mm (incidental) | Need not routinely trigger evaluation; individualized assessment by risk factors | [acog-co734-2018] |

- Evidence summarized in the opinion (Table 1): NPV 99.1–100% for cut-offs of 3–5 mm in four
  cohorts; pooled missed cancers 1 in 383 (≤ 3 mm), 1 in 339 (≤ 4 mm), 1 in 239 (≤ 5 mm)
  (Table 2). [acog-co734-2018, Tables 1–2]
- Without bleeding, one cited study found NPV 99.94% for ≤ 6 mm (1 cancer in 1,750 women);
  polyps removed from asymptomatic women carried a cancer incidence of about 1 in 288.
  [acog-co734-2018]

## 4. Management

- Bleeding: TVUS or office endometrial sampling are both reasonable first tests; both are not
  required. TVUS should be offered first only when a thin echo would end the work-up. Higher
  clinical risk → sampling first. [acog-co734-2018]
- Persistent or recurrent bleeding → histology regardless of thickness, because rare cancers
  (especially type II) present with < 3 mm. [acog-co734-2018]
- Insufficient office biopsy with bleeding that has stopped and a thin echo at TVUS → no further
  evaluation needed. [acog-co734-2018]
- Abnormal endometrium identified → sampling. [acog-co734-2018]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Axial uterus, obesity, myomas, adenomyosis or prior uterine surgery can prevent a reliable
  measurement; report "not adequately visualized" rather than a number. [acog-co734-2018]
- Including intracavitary fluid in the measurement.
- Applying the ≤ 4 mm bleeding rule to asymptomatic women. [acog-co734-2018]
- A thin echo does not reliably exclude type II cancer. [acog-co734-2018]

## 6. What the source does not say

- No numeric threshold that triggers evaluation in asymptomatic women (it mentions the 6-mm
  study only as evidence).
- No guidance for women on tamoxifen or hormone therapy.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Digitado-IA also cites Smith-Bindman 2004 (11-mm decision threshold without bleeding), SRU
  2001, SOGC 451 (2024), Chee 2025 and Doll 2021/2024 (lower performance of the 4-mm rule in
  Black patients). None has been read into radref yet; check ACOG for reaffirmation status.
