# Postmenopausal endometrium on transvaginal US

> **With bleeding:** a thin, distinct echo **≤ 4 mm** (ACOG) — or not above 5 mm (SRU 2001) —
> makes endometrial cancer very unlikely (NPV > 99%). Persistent or recurrent bleeding needs
> histology whatever the thickness. **Without bleeding:** the ≤ 4–5 mm rule does not apply; a
> decision analysis puts the biopsy threshold at **> 11 mm** (cancer risk ≈ 6.7%), and ACOG says
> an incidental > 4 mm need not routinely trigger evaluation.
> [acog-co734-2018; sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001; smith-bindman-endometrium-2004]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** postmenopausal women examined by transvaginal US, with or without bleeding.
- **Postmenopausal bleeding (SRU):** any vaginal bleeding other than the expected cyclic bleeding
  of sequential hormone therapy. [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Endometrial thickness | Maximum anteroposterior **double-layer** thickness on a long-axis (sagittal) transvaginal view, at the thickest part, between the echogenic borders, excluding the hypoechoic inner myometrium | [acog-co734-2018; sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |
| Fluid | Not included; with intracavitary fluid, measure each layer and add them | [acog-co734-2018; sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |
| Technique (SRU) | Transvaginal 5–10 MHz, empty bladder, sagittal and transverse views including cervix, fundus and cornua; transabdominal alone is insufficient | [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |
| Non-diagnostic study | Endometrium not seen in its entirety or margins indistinct (about 5–10% of patients) — state technical adequacy in the report | [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### With bleeding

| Statement | Source |
|---|---|
| Thin echo ≤ 4 mm: NPV > 99%; TVUS appropriate as initial evaluation | [acog-co734-2018] |
| Abnormal if double thickness > 5 mm; > 5 mm detected 96% of cancers in a meta-analysis of 85 studies; most panelists preferred 5 mm over 4 mm (similar sensitivity, lower specificity at 4 mm) | [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |
| Focal thickening, mass, inhomogeneity or indistinct margins are abnormal regardless of thickness → sampling, sonohysterography or hysteroscopy | [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |
| Meta-analysis (35 studies, 6,302 patients): sensitivity / specificity 94% / 35% at 3 mm, 95% / 45% at 4 mm, 88% / 56% at 5 mm; supports the 4-mm threshold | [chee-endometrium-2025] |
| Risk of cancer with bleeding: about 0.07% if ≤ 5 mm, 7.3% if > 5 mm (model) | [smith-bindman-endometrium-2004] |

### Without bleeding (incidental)

| Statement | Source |
|---|---|
| > 11 mm: cancer risk ≈ 6.7% (similar to bleeding with > 5 mm) → consider biopsy; ≤ 11 mm: risk ≈ 0.002%. A 10-mm threshold (≤ 10 normal) gives ≈ 5.8%, matching bleeding with ≥ 5 mm | [smith-bindman-endometrium-2004] |
| An 8-mm threshold would biopsy ≈ 0.9% of normal women for a 2.1% cancer risk | [smith-bindman-endometrium-2004] |
| > 4 mm without bleeding need not routinely trigger evaluation; individualize by risk factors; TVUS is not a screening test | [acog-co734-2018] |
| The bleeding threshold does not apply to asymptomatic women; no normal maximum established (2001) | [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001] |

## 4. Management

- Bleeding: TVUS or office biopsy are both acceptable first steps; higher clinical risk → biopsy
  first. Thin echo after a non-diagnostic biopsy, with bleeding stopped → no further sampling.
  [acog-co734-2018; sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001]
- Hormone therapy: sequential regimens → scan 4–5 days after the withdrawal bleed; continuous
  regimens → any time. Sensitivity unchanged, more false positives. Tamoxifen with bleeding →
  same 5-mm approach; no routine screening of asymptomatic tamoxifen users.
  [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Axial uterus, obesity, myomas, adenomyosis or prior surgery can prevent a reliable
  measurement; report "not adequately visualized" rather than a number. [acog-co734-2018]
- A thin echo does not reliably exclude type II cancer. [acog-co734-2018]
- Black patients: in a simulation the ≥ 4-mm rule had sensitivity 47.5% in Black vs 87.9% in
  White women (fibroids, non-endometrioid histology) [doll-tvus-simulated-2021]; in a real cohort
  of Black patients undergoing hysterectomy, the false-negative probability at < 5 mm was 11.4%
  (26.1% with partial visibility of the endometrium), and tissue sampling is recommended when
  bleeding is present. [doll-tvus-black-2024]

## 6. What the source does not say

- Smith-Bindman's 11-mm threshold comes from a decision model, not from a cohort with outcomes;
  it excludes hormone users and does not account for individual risk factors.
  [smith-bindman-endometrium-2004]
- SOGC 451 (2024), cited by Digitado-IA for the 11-mm rule, has not been read yet.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- 4 mm (ACOG 2018; Chee 2025) vs 5 mm (SRU 2001) for the bleeding cut-off.
- SRU 2001 is internally inconsistent at exactly 5 mm: the body calls > 5 mm abnormal, the
  conclusions call < 5 mm negative. [sru-postmenopausal-bleeding-2001]
- Smith-Bindman (11 mm) vs ACOG (no routine trigger) for incidental thickening.
