# Routine mid-trimester scan (ISUOG 2022)

> The routine mid-trimester scan, usually at **18–24 weeks**, checks cardiac activity, fetal
> number (and chorionicity), size, basic anatomy (Table 1), placenta and amniotic fluid; cervical
> length is optional by setting. [isuog-midtrimester-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** the routine scan offered to all pregnant women (grade B); detailed or targeted
  examinations follow other guidelines. [isuog-midtrimester-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Vasa previa | Unprotected fetal vessels in the membranes over or within **2 cm** of the internal os (~0.5 per 1000) | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |
| Monochorionic by default | No first-trimester scan, two separate placentas not seen and same fetal sex → treat as monochorionic | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Minimum anatomy (Table 1; * = optional)

| Region | Items | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Head | Intact cranium, normal shape; cavum septi pellucidi, choroid plexus, falx, thalami, lateral ventricles, cerebellum, cisterna magna normal; nuchal fold* | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Face | Both orbits; profile*; nasal bone*; upper lip intact | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Neck | No masses (e.g. cystic hygroma) | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Chest/heart | Normal chest and lungs; heart activity; four-chamber view in normal position; outflow tracts; LVOT and three-vessel (or three-vessel-trachea) view; no diaphragmatic hernia | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Abdomen | Stomach on the left; bowel not dilated or hyperechogenic; gallbladder on the right*; both kidneys, no pyelectasis; bladder normal; abdominal cord insertion normal | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Skeleton | No spinal defects or masses (transverse and sagittal); arms/hands and legs/feet with normal joint position | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |
| Placenta, cord, other | Placental position and relation to the cervix; no masses; three-vessel cord*; placental cord insertion*; genitalia*; cervical length* | [isuog-midtrimester-2022, Table 1] |

### Thresholds mentioned

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Renal pelvis | **≥ 7 mm** → reassess in the third trimester | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |
| Bladder (longitudinal) | 95th centile 14 mm at 18 weeks and 23 mm at 22 weeks; should not reach the cord insertion | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |
| Amniotic fluid (DVP) | ≤ 2.0 cm decreased; > 2 to ≤ 8.0 cm normal; > 8 cm increased | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |
| Placenta–internal os (TVS) | **≤ 15 mm** → follow-up in the third trimester; reaching or overlapping the os → follow-up | [isuog-midtrimester-2022] |

## 4. Management

- Low-lying placenta: "migration" is frequent; cut-offs suggested for likely migration were
  5 mm (anterior) and 15.5 mm (posterior) from the internal os at TVS. Prior uterine surgery
  with a low anterior placenta or previa → look for accreta signs (loss of the hypoechoic
  myometrial line below the placenta, large irregular lacunae, interrupted bladder–serosa line, myometrium
  < 1 mm, placental bulge) and refer for detailed assessment. [isuog-midtrimester-2022]
- Vasa previa risk factors (twins, ART, low-lying or bilobed placenta, succenturiate lobe,
  velamentous insertion) → targeted transvaginal colour Doppler examination (grade B).
  [isuog-midtrimester-2022]
- Routine pulsed Doppler of uterine or umbilical arteries is **not** recommended for low-risk
  screening at this scan. Report marginal or velamentous cord insertion if seen; a single
  umbilical artery without other findings should not cause undue anxiety.
  [isuog-midtrimester-2022]
- Abnormal UA Doppler and/or maternal hypertension or pre-eclampsia → emergency referral.
  AFI may be preferable for polyhydramnios and DVP for oligohydramnios (grade C). Uterine or
  adnexal findings seen should be reported, but their formal assessment is not part of the
  scan. Cervix: TVS CL < 25 mm before 24 weeks is the short-cervix cut-off used in studies;
  screening requires consent, training and audit (see `cervical-length-isuog-2022`).
  [isuog-midtrimester-2022]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Using older femur charts with modern machines: the narrower beam changed lateral
  measurements, and older charts "may lead to an overestimation of the FL"; use recent charts.
  [isuog-midtrimester-2022]
- Calling chorionicity in the mid trimester when it could have been fixed before 14–15 weeks.

## 6. What the source does not say

- Ventricular atrium and cisterna magna are listed as "normal in appearance"; this guideline
  gives no numeric cut-offs for them.
