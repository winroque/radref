# O-RADS MRI (2022)

> ACR lexicon-based score (O-RADS MRI 0–5) for adnexal lesions at MRI, built on the
> O-RADS MRI score validated by Thomassin-Naggara et al (JAMA Netw Open 2020) for
> sonographically indeterminate masses. The key elements are fluid content, the presence of
> **solid tissue**, its T2/DWI signal, and its enhancement curve (DCE) relative to the outer
> myometrium. [orads-mri-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adnexal lesions at MRI, typically those indeterminate or incompletely
  evaluated at US, in average-risk patients **without acute symptoms**. Not for non-ovarian /
  non-adnexal lesions; a dilated tube in an acute setting (pyosalpinx/PID) is not scored.
  [orads-mri-2022]
- **Protocol** (1.5 or 3.0 T): sagittal and axial T2 without fat saturation, T1 in/opposed
  phase, DWI, and dynamic contrast-enhanced (DCE) 3D T1 with fat saturation at **≤ 15-s**
  temporal resolution, starting 30 s before injection, about 4 minutes long, sections ≤ 3 mm.
  DWI with b ≥ 1000 s/mm² and sections ≤ 4 mm; sagittal T2 ≤ 4 mm, axial T2 ≤ 3 mm.
  [orads-mri-2022, Table 2]
- Management recommendations per score are **not** set by this paper (prospective studies
  needed). [orads-mri-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Solid lesion | ≥ 80% enhancing solid tissue | [orads-mri-2022] |
| Solid tissue | **Enhancing** component with one of these morphologies: papillary projection, mural nodule, irregular septation/wall, or larger solid portion | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| Non-solid tissue | Smooth septations or walls (thin or thick), clot, non-enhancing debris, fibrin, fat | [orads-mri-2022] |
| Dark T2/dark DWI solid tissue | Solid tissue homogeneously hypointense on T2 and on high-b DWI | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| Lipid content | Fluid hyperintense on T1 and T2 with signal loss on fat saturation; microscopic fat on opposed-phase images | [orads-mri-2022] |
| Low-risk TIC | Solid tissue enhances **slower** than myometrium, **no shoulder, no plateau** | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 2] |
| Intermediate-risk TIC | Moderate initial rise, slope **≤ myometrium**, with shoulder and plateau | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 2] |
| High-risk TIC | Brisk initial rise, slope **> myometrium**, with shoulder and plateau | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 2] |
| TIC technique | One ROI on the earliest-enhancing solid tissue, one on the outer myometrium (avoid outer myometrial vessels, leiomyomas, adenomyosis) | [orads-mri-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score | Risk (approx. PPV) | Lexicon description (summary) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Incomplete | Lesion incompletely imaged or technically inadequate exam | [orads-mri-2022] |
| **1** | Normal ovaries | No ovarian lesion; follicle (simple cyst ≤ 3 cm), hemorrhagic cyst ≤ 3 cm, or corpus luteum ± hemorrhage ≤ 3 cm — all in a **premenopausal** woman | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| **2** | Almost certainly benign (< 0.5%) | Unilocular cyst, any fluid, no wall enhancement, no solid tissue; unilocular cyst with simple or endometriotic fluid, smooth enhancing wall, no solid tissue; lesion with lipid, no enhancing solid tissue; lesion with **dark T2/dark DWI** solid tissue; dilated tube with simple fluid and thin smooth enhancing wall/folds; para-ovarian cyst, any fluid, thin smooth wall | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| **3** | Low risk (~5%) | Unilocular cyst with proteinaceous, hemorrhagic or mucinous fluid and smooth enhancing wall; multilocular cyst, any fluid, no lipid, smooth enhancing septa/wall, no solid tissue; solid tissue (not dark T2/DWI) with **low-risk TIC**; dilated tube with non-simple fluid and thin wall/folds, or simple fluid with thick smooth wall/folds | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| **4** | Intermediate risk (~50%) | Solid tissue (not dark T2/DWI) with **intermediate-risk TIC** — or, without DCE, enhancing **≤ myometrium** at 30–40 s; lesion with lipid and a **large volume** of enhancing solid tissue | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |
| **5** | High risk (~90%) | Solid tissue (not dark T2/DWI) with **high-risk TIC** — or, without DCE, enhancing **> myometrium** at 30–40 s; peritoneal, mesenteric or omental nodularity or irregular thickening, with or without ascites | [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1] |

Notes (Figure 1 footnotes): PPVs are approximate (from the 2020 validation), apply to the score
overall, and include borderline tumours. Minimal enhancement of a Rokitansky nodule in a
lipid-containing lesion does **not** raise the score to 4. A hemorrhagic cyst ≤ 3 cm in a
premenopausal woman is O-RADS MRI 1 (Fig. 1 footnote, checked on the rendered page; also Fig. 4 and
text p. 5 — but the Fig. 5 legend classifies it as O-RADS MRI 2, an internal inconsistency).
Endometriomas with a multilocular appearance are score 2; multilocular without solid tissue has
PPV < 3%; by TIC, PPV 6.7% (low), 46.6% (intermediate), 85.6% (high). [orads-mri-2022, Fig. 1; pp. 9–10]

## 4. Management

Not defined by the source; see section 1.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Treating smooth thick septa, clot or debris as solid tissue (they are non-solid).
- Using non-DCE MRI and then trying to separate score 3 from 4 (not possible without a TIC).
- No uterus: a low-risk TIC can still be recognized (steady enhancement), but 4 vs 5 cannot be
  separated confidently. [orads-mri-2022]
- Lipid-containing fluid mistaken for hemorrhagic/endometriotic fluid: check fat-saturated
  images.

## 6. What the source does not say

- No management recommendations per score.
- No US scoring (see `orads-us-2022`).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- **Validation (2020):** prospective study at 15 referral centres (ESUR network) (2013–2016, 2-year follow-up
  for non-operated women) in women referred to MRI for a sonographically indeterminate adnexal
  mass; 1,340 enrolled, 1,194 evaluable, 1,130 with a mass and a reference standard (surgery 67.9%,
  follow-up 32.1%); 18.0% had ≥ 1 malignant adnexal or non-adnexal pelvic mass (borderline
  counted as malignant). When a TIC was not feasible it was classified as type 2. No invasive cancer scored 2. Positive likelihood ratios: score
  2 = 0.01, 3 = 0.27, 4 = 4.42, 5 = 38.81. Experienced readers: AUC 0.961, sensitivity 0.93,
  specificity 0.91 (scores 4–5 as positive); interrater κ 0.784. [thomassin-orads-mri-2020]
- The approximate PPVs in the 2022 figure come from that cohort; the 2020 paper itself reports
  likelihood ratios per score rather than PPVs. [thomassin-orads-mri-2020]
