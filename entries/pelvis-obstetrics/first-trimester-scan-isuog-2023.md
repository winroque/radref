# 11–14-week scan (ISUOG 2023)

> Performed at **11 + 0 to 14 + 0 weeks** (CRL **45–84 mm**): confirm viability, date by CRL,
> count fetuses and determine chorionicity, survey basic anatomy, measure nuchal translucency
> (NT) when aneuploidy screening is offered, and (where offered) screen for preterm
> pre-eclampsia. [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** routine first-trimester scans; not very early scans. [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CRL | See `pregnancy-dating`; best-quality CRL between 45 and 84 mm; recent international prescriptive charts recommended (INTERGROWTH-21st cited) | [isuog-11-14-2023; ig21-crl-2014] |
| NT technique | Midsagittal section; image magnified to head and thorax only, calipers at 0.1-mm increments; neutral position; fetus separate from the amnion; maximum thickness; cross calipers on the echogenic margins; three measurements, the **largest** used | [isuog-11-14-2023] |
| BPD | Largest symmetrical axial view; outer-to-inner or outer-to-outer — follow the chart's method | [isuog-11-14-2023] |
| Chorionicity | Lambda sign or T-sign; determine early, when most reliable | [isuog-11-14-2023] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Minimum anatomy (Table 1, summary)

Singleton confirmed; skull ossified with normal contour; falx separating two halves;
choroid plexuses almost filling the posterior two-thirds of the lateral ventricles ("butterfly");
NT < 95th percentile confirmed; heart in the chest with regular rhythm (four-chamber view);
stomach visible; abdominal wall intact; bladder visible and not dilated; four limbs, each with
three segments; placenta without cystic structures. [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 1]
The detailed (best-practice) examination adds, among others, a normally filled bladder with
longitudinal diameter **< 7 mm** by 12 weeks, spine continuity and both hands and feet.
[isuog-11-14-2023, Table 2]

### Screening strategies (Table 3, summary)

| Strategy | Note | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Combined (maternal age + GA, NT, free β-hCG, PAPP-A) | Trisomy 21 detection ~92% at ~4.6% false positives (cut-off 1 in 100 in the cited study) | [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 3] |
| cfDNA with prior anomaly scan and NT | CVS if **NT > 3.5 mm** or anomalies at ultrasound; otherwise cfDNA | [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 3] |
| Additional markers | Nasal bone, DV and tricuspid flow in the 1 in 50 to 1 in 1000 group: DR 93–96%, FPR 2.5% | [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 3] |
| Contingent cfDNA | cfDNA in the 1 in 10 to 1 in 1000 group: DR 98.4%, FPR 0.7% | [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 3] |
| cfDNA (first line) row | DR 100%, FPR 0.1% plus 2.5% additional if NT > 3.5 mm or anomaly; cfDNA alone (T21 99.7% / 0.04%) is not recommended without the 11–14-week scan | [isuog-11-14-2023, Table 3; p. 136] |

- Use the CRL itself (not the calculated GA) as the reference for NT, UtA-PI and serum
  markers. [isuog-11-14-2023]
- Markers: tricuspid regurgitation = flow > 60 cm/s for > 50% of the cardiac cycle (2–4-mm gate across the tricuspid valve, apex at 12 or 6 o'clock); absent/hypoplastic
  nasal bone in 50–60% of trisomy 21. Mean FHR 171 bpm at 10 weeks, 156 at 14. Doppler with
  TI ≤ 1.0 and short exposure (5–10 min). [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 4. Management

- Placenta: note masses, cysts or a large subchorionic collection (> 5 cm); **do not report
  placenta previa** at this stage; look for Cesarean scar pregnancy and accreta signs after
  prior Cesarean. [isuog-11-14-2023]
- Preterm pre-eclampsia: best combined test = maternal factors + UtA-PI + PlGF + MAP, screen
  positive at **≥ 1 in 100**; high risk → aspirin 150 mg nightly from 11–15 + 6 weeks until 36
  weeks, delivery or diagnosis of pre-eclampsia (not for all women). Without UtA-PI/PlGF, use
  maternal factors + MAP (not maternal factors alone); calcium supplementation if intake
  < 800 mg/day. [isuog-11-14-2023]
- CRL technique: transabdominal or transvaginal, fetus horizontal with the crown–rump line at
  ~90° to the beam, neutral position (neither flexed nor hyperextended), image magnified to
  fill most of the screen. The amnion is often seen as a sac around the fetus, not yet fused
  with the chorion. [isuog-11-14-2023]
- CRL dates the pregnancy in all cases except IVF; first-trimester cervical length is not yet
  recommended routinely. [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 5. Pitfalls

- "Normalizing" a small CRL by redating when anomalies are present (trisomy 18, triploidy).
- Measuring NT with the amnion mistaken for skin, or with the neck flexed/extended.

## 6. What the source does not say

- No universal NT cut-off for "abnormal" other than the < 95th percentile check and the
  > 3.5 mm trigger in the cfDNA strategy; risk algorithms (e.g. FMF) handle the rest.
