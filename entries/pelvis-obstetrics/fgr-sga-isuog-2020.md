# FGR and SGA — definition and management

> **SGA** is a size label (EFW or AC < 10th centile); **FGR** means failure to reach growth
> potential. The 2016 Delphi consensus defines early (< 32 weeks) and late (≥ 32 weeks) FGR
> with **solitary** criteria (AC/EFW < 3rd centile; absent UA end-diastolic flow before 32
> weeks) and **contributory** criteria combining size with Doppler or growth velocity. ISUOG
> 2020 adopts these definitions and gives monitoring and delivery criteria.
> [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016; isuog-sga-fgr-2020]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** singleton, correctly dated fetuses without aneuploidy, malformation or infection
  [isuog-sga-fgr-2020, Introduction]; the Delphi FGR definition assumes no congenital anomalies
  (placental FGR) [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016, Table 6].
- Growth centiles in the definition are **non-customized**. [isuog-biometry-2019, Table 2]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SGA | EFW or AC < 10th centile | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| Early FGR | Detected **< 32 weeks** | [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016] |
| Late FGR | Detected **≥ 32 weeks** | [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016] |
| CPR | MCA-PI / UA-PI | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Delphi consensus (Table 2 of ISUOG 2019, reproduced from Gordijn 2016)

| | Early FGR (< 32 weeks) | Late FGR (≥ 32 weeks) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solitary | AC or EFW < 3rd centile, **or** UA absent end-diastolic flow | AC or EFW < 3rd centile | [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016; isuog-biometry-2019, Table 2] |
| Contributory | AC or EFW < 10th centile **plus** UtA-PI > 95th and/or UA-PI > 95th centile | At least **two of three**: AC/EFW < 10th centile; AC/EFW crossing centiles by > 2 quartiles; CPR < 5th or UA-PI > 95th centile | [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016; isuog-biometry-2019, Table 2] |

ISUOG 2020: fetal size alone is not sufficient to identify FGR unless AC or EFW is **< 3rd
percentile**; a drop in AC or EFW centiles should alert to possible FGR.
[isuog-sga-fgr-2020]

## 4. Management

### Early FGR (TRUFFLE-based, ISUOG 2020)

| GA | Deliver if (after steroids when applicable) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Maternal indication or obstetric emergency | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 24 + 0 to 25 + 6 wk | Personalized management | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| ≥ 26 + 0 wk | Spontaneous repeated persistent unprovoked decelerations, or BPP ≤ 4 | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 26 + 0 to 28 + 6 wk | DV a-wave at or below baseline, or cCTG STV < 2.6 ms | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 29 + 0 to 31 + 6 wk | DV a-wave at or below baseline, or STV < 3.0 ms | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 32 + 0 to 33 + 6 wk (permitted after 30 + 0) | Reversed UA-EDF, or STV < 3.5 ms | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| ≥ 34 + 0 wk (permitted after 32 + 0) | Absent UA-EDF, or STV < 4.5 ms | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |

If no delivery criterion is met: surveillance at least every 2 days (per the flowchart);
corticosteroids if delivery before 34 + 0 weeks; elective Cesarean if abnormal STV, DV
alteration, absent/reversed UA-EDF, altered BPP or maternal indication.
[isuog-sga-fgr-2020, Fig. 1]

### Late FGR

| GA | Deliver if | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Repeated unprovoked decelerations; BPP ≤ 4; maternal indication; STV < 3.5 ms at 32 + 0 to 33 + 6 or < 4.5 ms from 34 + 0; absent or reversed UA-EDF | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 36 + 0 to 37 + 6 wk | UA-PI > 95th percentile **or** AC/EFW < 3rd percentile | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |
| 38 + 0 to 39 + 0 wk | Cerebral blood-flow redistribution or any other feature of FGR | [isuog-sga-fgr-2020] |

- Monitoring: MCA-PI and its ratios with UA-PI are the key Doppler parameters in late FGR;
  with UA-PI > 95th, monitor at least once or twice a week. An abnormal MCA-PI, CPR or UCR
  should be **confirmed within 24 h**. [isuog-sga-fgr-2020]
- Induction of labour is indicated in the absence of contraindications, with continuous fetal
  heart rate monitoring. [isuog-sga-fgr-2020]
- Absent or reversed UA-EDF (Fig. 2): monitor every 2–3 days unless delivery is indicated.
  [isuog-sga-fgr-2020, Fig. 2]

### SGA (not FGR)

- Doppler at diagnosis and follow-up; late SGA: growth every 2 weeks and UA/MCA-PI, CPR and UCR
  weekly; plan delivery from 38 + 0 weeks, not beyond 39 + 0 (grade A); continuous monitoring
  in labour. [isuog-sga-fgr-2020, SGA section]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Calling every fetus < 10th centile "FGR" without a contributory criterion.
- Using customized centiles in the Delphi definition (it uses non-customized centiles).
- Basing delivery on a single unconfirmed low MCA-PI (moderate interobserver agreement).

## 6. What the source does not say

- No Doppler reference chart is mandated; the percentiles must come from a stated chart
  (see `obstetric-doppler-isuog-2021`). Charts vary widely (up to 50% in the MCA-PI 5th-centile
  cut-off at term), and there is no strong evidence favouring CPR or UCR. [isuog-sga-fgr-2020]
- The Delphi consensus rejected customized centiles, isolated MCA or DV Doppler, AFI and
  biomarkers as criteria. [gordijn-fgr-delphi-2016, Table 4]
- Antenatal corticosteroids between 34 and 36 weeks: guidelines disagree (RCOG up to 35 + 6).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Magnesium sulfate neuroprotection thresholds vary (< 32–33, < 32, < 30 or < 29 weeks);
  ISUOG defers to local protocols. [isuog-sga-fgr-2020]
