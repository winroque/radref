# Cervical length (ISUOG 2022)

> Transvaginal cervical length (CL) is the reference method to predict spontaneous preterm
> birth (PTB). Measure the **shortest technically correct** of at least three measurements. A
> CL **≤ 25 mm before 24 weeks** is the pragmatic cut-off for preventive measures in
> asymptomatic singleton pregnancies. [isuog-cervix-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** singleton and twin pregnancies, asymptomatic or with threatened preterm
  labour. [isuog-cervix-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Technique (Table 1) | Empty bladder; semi-recumbent; probe in anterior fornix, sagittal; see bladder border, external os, endocervical canal and mucosa, internal os; both lips of equal width; cervix filling 50–75% of the screen; minimal pressure; straight line between functional internal os and external os; three measurements, record the shortest correct one; allow time for dynamic change | [isuog-cervix-2022, Table 1] |
| Funneling | Protrusion of the amniotic sac into the upper canal (T → Y → V → U); its length is **not** included in CL | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Sludge | Hyperechoic debris near the internal os; associated with infection and PTB, no consensus on further tests or treatment | [isuog-cervix-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Screening window | **18–24 weeks** (with the anomaly scan); earlier measurements overestimate CL | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Short cervix (asymptomatic singleton) | **≤ 25 mm** | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Population centiles (2nd trimester) | 10th ≈ 26 mm; 5th 22–23 mm; 1st 11–13 mm; median falls from ~43 mm at 16 weeks to ~31 mm at 36 weeks | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Transabdominal vs TVS | TVS recommended (more precise and reproducible); one prospective study needed a TAS cut-off of ≤ 36 mm to match TVS ≤ 25 mm | [isuog-cervix-2022] |

## 4. Management

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Asymptomatic singleton, no prior PTB | Universal TVS CL at 18–24 weeks where feasible; CL ≤ 25 mm → nightly vaginal progesterone until 36 weeks (grade A) | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Prior spontaneous PTB | Progesterone 16–36 weeks, or surveillance and treatment if CL ≤ 25 mm; CL every 2 weeks from 14–16 to 24 weeks if ≥ 30 mm, weekly if 26–29 mm; CL ≤ 25 mm on progesterone before 24 weeks → ultrasound-indicated cerclage may be recommended | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Threatened preterm labour, 22 + 0 to 33 + 6 weeks | TVS CL recommended; ≥ 30 mm → usually discharge; < 15 or < 20 mm → admission/steroids per local protocol; between → laboratory triage test if available | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| Twins | CL preferred screening method; 25 mm pragmatic cut-off at 18–24 weeks; vaginal progesterone may be considered if ≤ 25 mm; not for unselected twins | [isuog-cervix-2022] |
| After cerclage | Further CL measurement not recommended | [isuog-cervix-2022] |

## 5. Pitfalls

- Full bladder or probe pressure lengthens the cervix; a thick lower segment or contraction can
  mimic funneling. [isuog-cervix-2022, Table 1]
- Measuring before 16–18 weeks (internal os hard to identify).
- Adding funnel length to the CL.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It gives no single cut-off for symptomatic women (15 or 20 mm, local protocol).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- A 20-mm cut-off had sensitivity 22.1% (screen-positive 1.8%) vs 33.1% (4.1%) at 25 mm for
  PTB < 35 weeks in a meta-analysis; ISUOG favours ≤ 25 mm. [isuog-cervix-2022]
