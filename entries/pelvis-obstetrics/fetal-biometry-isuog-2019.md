# Fetal biometry and growth (ISUOG 2019)

> From 14 weeks, measure **BPD, HC, AC and FL**; plot them on charts built with the same
> caliper method; describe the fetus as **AGA, SGA, LGA or FGR**. Size is one time point; growth
> needs at least two scans, usually **≥ 3 weeks apart**. ISUOG favours **prescriptive**,
> prospectively built, population-based charts with the lowest methodological bias.
> [isuog-biometry-2019]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** singleton pregnancies with known GA and normal anatomy. Management of FGR is
  outside this guideline (see `fgr-sga-isuog-2020`). [isuog-biometry-2019]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AGA | Size within the normal range: biometry and/or EFW between the **10th and 90th** centiles | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| SGA | Size below a set threshold: EFW or AC **< 10th** centile (5th, 3rd, −2 SD and Z-scores also used in the literature) | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| LGA | EFW or AC **> 90th** centile (95th, 97th, +2 SD also used) | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| Macrosomia | At term, weight above a fixed cut-off (**4000 or 4500 g**) | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| FGR | Fetus that has not achieved its growth potential; early-onset **< 32 weeks**, late-onset **≥ 32 weeks** at detection | [isuog-biometry-2019] |
| Centile ↔ Z-score | 5th centile = −1.64; 10th centile = −1.28 (normal distribution) | [isuog-biometry-2019] |

"Symmetrical/asymmetrical FGR" should no longer be used. [isuog-biometry-2019]

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Planes and quality score (Table 1: one point per criterion)

| Image | Criteria | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Head (BPD, HC) | Symmetrical plane; thalami; cavum septi pellucidi; **cerebellum not visible**; head > half the image; calipers/ellipse correct | [isuog-biometry-2019, Table 1] |
| Abdomen (AC) | Symmetrical plane; stomach bubble; portal sinus; **kidneys not visible**; abdomen > half the image; calipers/ellipse correct | [isuog-biometry-2019, Table 1] |
| Femur (FL) | Both ends clearly visible; **< 45°** to horizontal; femur > half the image; calipers correct | [isuog-biometry-2019, Table 1] |

- HC and AC: ellipse tool, calipers on the **outer** edge of the soft-tissue circumference
  (ellipse and two-diameter methods are equally reproducible; use the method of the chart).
  [isuog-biometry-2019]
- Quality control: review images; re-measure ≥ 10% of stored images; check Z-score
  distributions; retrain operators whose measurements fall persistently outside the 95% limits
  of agreement. [isuog-biometry-2019]

### Main biometry charts

| Chart | Type and population | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Snijders & Nicolaides 1994 | Cross-sectional, 1,040 normal singletons at 14–40 weeks (40 per week), London; also ventricles, cerebellum, cisterna magna; HC calculated as 3.14 × (BPD + OFD)/2 from outer borders; FL from greater trochanter to lateral condyle; AC from two diameters; published P5/P50/P95 (not P3/P10) | [snijders-biometry-1994] |
| INTERGROWTH-21st 2014 | **Prescriptive**, longitudinal; 4,321 low-risk women in eight countries (including Pelotas, Brazil), scans every 5 weeks from 14 to 42 weeks; HC, BPD, OFD, AC, FL; 3rd–97th centiles | [ig21-fetal-growth-2014] |
| WHO 2017 | Longitudinal, 1,387 low-risk women in ten countries (including Brazil), 7,924 scans at 14–40 weeks, quantile regression; biometry and EFW; growth differed between countries and with fetal sex | [who-fetal-growth-2017] |

## 4. Management

- Interval between growth scans typically **≥ 3 weeks** (computer modelling: AC every 2 weeks
  gives > 10% false positives for FGR). [isuog-biometry-2019]
- Routine mid-trimester scan typically 18–22 weeks; third-trimester scans by local policy; a
  36-week scan detected FGR better than a 32-week scan. [isuog-biometry-2019]
- A fall in centile or Z-score should prompt more monitoring; > 2 quartiles (> 50 centiles) is a
  Delphi criterion for FGR. [isuog-biometry-2019]
- Abnormal biometry → detailed assessment, including confirmation of dating. With new maternal
  hypertension and/or absent or reversed end-diastolic flow in the UA → urgent referral to a
  specialist. [isuog-biometry-2019]
- Report at least: chart/standard and EFW formula used, and centile or Z-score.
  [isuog-biometry-2019]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Mixing caliper conventions (outer-to-outer vs outer-to-inner) with a chart built the other
  way.
- Plotting EFW on **birth-weight** charts (they include many growth-restricted preterm babies).
  [isuog-biometry-2019]
- Using an older FL chart with modern machines (narrower beam shortens lateral measurements)
  — noted in the 2022 mid-trimester guideline. [isuog-midtrimester-2022]

## 6. What the source does not say

- ISUOG does not mandate one specific chart; it asks for prescriptive, low-bias charts and
  awareness of national/local mandates.
- Chart centile tables are not reproduced here.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Customized charts (maternal height/weight, ethnicity, parity, sex) classify a different
  proportion of fetuses as SGA; benefit over population charts was not shown in a prospective
  study cited by ISUOG. [isuog-biometry-2019]
- WHO authors suggest their charts may need local adjustment; INTERGROWTH-21st presents one
  international standard.
