# Polycystic ovarian morphology (PCOM) — 2023 guideline

> Ultrasound thresholds for polycystic ovarian morphology in adults (follicle number per
> ovary, follicle number per section, ovarian volume) and the minimum report content, from the
> 2023 International Evidence-based Guideline for PCOS; plus the 2026 global consensus that
> renamed the syndrome. [teede-pcos-guideline-2023; teede-pmos-name-2026]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adult women, PCOM as one of the diagnostic criteria for the syndrome.
  [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]
- **Does not apply to adolescents:** there are no definitive criteria for PCOM on ultrasound in
  adolescents, so it is not recommended in them (1.4.6). Adolescents need both ovulatory
  dysfunction and hyperandrogenism; those with features but not meeting criteria are "at
  increased risk" and reassessed at or before full reproductive maturity, **8 years after
  menarche** (1.1.4). [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FNPO | Follicle number per ovary: follicles **2–9 mm** in the whole ovary | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023] |
| FNPS | Follicle number per single cross-section | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023] |
| Ovarian volume | Excludes dominant follicle (≥ 10 mm), corpus luteum and cysts; with a dominant follicle, use the contralateral ovary's FNPO | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Situation | PCOM threshold (in at least one ovary) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Adult, transvaginal, current equipment | **FNPO ≥ 20** — primary criterion | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023, rec. 1.4.x] |
| Older equipment or images insufficient to count the whole ovary | Volume **≥ 10 mL** or **FNPS ≥ 10** | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023] |
| Transabdominal | Report mainly volume ≥ 10 mL or FNPS ≥ 10 | [teede-pcos-guideline-2023] |

## 4. Management

- PCOM is **not** a diagnosis of the syndrome; diagnosis is clinical-laboratory (2 of 3:
  ovulatory dysfunction, hyperandrogenism, PCOM **or** elevated AMH). With irregular cycles
  and hyperandrogenism, ultrasound is not needed (1.4.9). In adults AMH can replace ultrasound;
  they are not added together (1.5.5). [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]
- Minimum report content (1.4.11): LMP or cycle phase, transducer frequency, route, total
  2–9 mm follicles per ovary, three diameters or volume of each ovary, other ovarian findings
  (cysts, corpus luteum, dominant follicle), uterus/endometrium (thickness and pattern).
  [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Using the Rotterdam 2003 threshold (≥ 12 follicles), which overdiagnoses with current
  transducers. [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]
- Including a dominant follicle or cyst in the volume.

## 6. What the source does not say

- The 2026 Lancet paper is about the **name** of the syndrome; it does not set or change
  ultrasound thresholds, and it does not say what the ultrasound finding should be called.
  Using "PCOM" for the imaging finding is the 2023 guideline's term, not a 2026 decision.
- No age-specific PCOM cut-offs yet: the guideline asks for them to be defined (1.4.10).
  [teede-pcos-guideline-2023]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- **Renaming (2026):** from 12 May 2026 the polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is called
  **polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS)** by global consensus, because there are
  no pathological cysts and the condition is endocrine and metabolic. A **3-year transition**
  (to ~2029) applies; the international guideline will be updated in 2028.
  [teede-pmos-name-2026]
- Any Portuguese rendering of the new name (e.g. "síndrome ovariana metabólica poliendócrina",
  SOMP) is a usage choice; the source gives only the English name.
