# Early first-trimester pregnancy (SRU 2012 consensus)

> Ultrasound criteria that are **diagnostic** of pregnancy failure (designed for 100%
> specificity) versus only **suspicious**, plus normal milestones, poor-prognosis signs,
> subchorionic haematoma and pregnancy of unknown location (PUL). [doubilet-nonviable-2013;
> rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** intrauterine pregnancy early in the first trimester (up to ~8 weeks),
  transvaginal US. [doubilet-nonviable-2013]
- **Not covered in detail:** sonographic signs of ectopic pregnancy (the review does not detail
  them); gestational-age formulas. [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| CRL | Crown–rump length; the most accurate gestational-age measure up to 12 weeks | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| MSD | Mean sac diameter; grows ~1.13 mm/day | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Intradecidual sign | Eccentric sac within decidua, collapsed cavity seen as echogenic line — highly suggestive of IUP | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Double sac sign | Two concentric echogenic rings — confirms IUP; both signs absent in ≥ 35% of sacs | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| PUL | Positive pregnancy test, no definite IUP and normal adnexa; transient state; three hypotheses: very early IUP, occult ectopic, completed miscarriage | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Pregnancy failure

| Finding | DIAGNOSTIC | SUSPICIOUS (not diagnostic) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Embryo without cardiac activity | **CRL ≥ 7 mm** | CRL **< 7 mm** | [doubilet-nonviable-2013; rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015, Tables 2–3] |
| Sac without embryo | **MSD ≥ 25 mm** | MSD **16–24 mm** | [doubilet-nonviable-2013] |
| Prior scan: sac **without** yolk sac | No embryo with heartbeat **≥ 2 weeks** later | 7–13 days later | [doubilet-nonviable-2013] |
| Prior scan: sac **with** yolk sac | No embryo with heartbeat **≥ 11 days** later | 7–10 days later | [doubilet-nonviable-2013] |
| Dates | — | No embryo **≥ 6 weeks** after LMP | [doubilet-nonviable-2013] |
| Morphology | — | Empty amnion; enlarged yolk sac (**> 7 mm**); small sac for embryo (**MSD − CRL < 5 mm**) | [doubilet-nonviable-2013] |

Time criteria require two scans. Thresholds are applied without rounding up (MSD 24.6 mm is
not ≥ 25 mm). Cardiac activity does not cancel morphologic signs (small sac remains suspicious
with heartbeat). [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

### Normal milestones (menstrual age; each ± 0.5 week)

| Weeks | Milestone | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 4.5–5.0 | Gestational sac visible transvaginally, 2–3 mm | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015, Table 1] |
| 5.0–5.5 | Yolk sac (3–5 mm ring, eccentric; may first appear as two parallel lines) | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| ~6.0 | 1–2 mm embryo next to yolk sac; cardiac activity begins | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| 6.5–7.0 | Amnion visible (easier after 7 weeks) | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| 7–8 | Spine | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| 8 | Head separable from trunk; four limb buds | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| 8.0–8.5 | Embryonic movement | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| 8–10 | Rhombencephalon | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |

- A round, smooth intrauterine fluid collection with a positive test is a gestational sac in
  > 99.5% and should be treated as IUP until proven otherwise.
- The yolk sac is the first structure that definitively confirms IUP.
- An embryo should be visible when MSD ≥ 25 mm.
- Cardiac activity can be seen at 1–2 mm, but its absence below 4 mm can be normal; the
  consensus requires it from CRL 7 mm.
- Heart rate lower limit ~100 bpm up to 6.2 weeks and ~120 bpm at 6.3–7.0 weeks; tachycardia
  (≥ 135 bpm before 6.3 weeks; ≥ 155 bpm at 6.3–7.0) has good prognosis; **bradycardia
  ≤ 85 bpm** is a poor-prognosis sign.
- Between 6.5 and 10 weeks mean amniotic diameter is ~10% larger than CRL; an "expanded
  amnion" is a poor-prognosis sign. [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

### Poor-prognosis signs (follow-up; none diagnostic alone)

| Structure | Sign | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sac | Irregular contour; low implantation | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015, Table 4] |
| Yolk sac | Calcified; > 7 mm | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Amnion | Empty, enlarged or expanded | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Embryo | Amorphous (7–8 weeks) | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Heart | Bradycardia ≤ 85 bpm | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Chorionic villi | Hydropic change | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Subchorionic haematoma | Large, especially ≥ 2/3 of sac circumference | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |

Irregular sac: in one series specificity and PPV 100% for abnormal pregnancy, sensitivity
only 10%. Calcified yolk sac suggests long-standing (≥ 2 weeks) demise.

### Subchorionic haematoma

Present in 18–22% of first-trimester pregnancies with bleeding; risk of loss **doubles** in
large haematomas, especially > 2/3 of the chorionic circumference. "Chorionic bump": uncertain
significance. [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

### PUL sub-groups (review, Fig. 19; round/irregular split checked on the rendered figure)

| US scenario | Hypotheses | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Normal endometrium and adnexa | Early IUP · occult ectopic · completed miscarriage | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Thick/heterogeneous endometrium (blood) + bleeding | PUL favouring miscarriage in progress; focal low-resistance trophoblastic flow may show implantation site | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Indeterminate round collection | Gestational sac vs decidual cyst | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Indeterminate irregular collection | Abnormal IUP vs localized intrauterine fluid | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |

## 4. Management

- Suspicious findings → follow-up US at **7–10 days** is generally appropriate.
  [doubilet-nonviable-2013, Table 2 footnote] To be **diagnostic** by time, the interval must be
  **≥ 11 days** (yolk sac present) or **≥ 14 days** (no yolk sac). [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- Indeterminate intrauterine collection: repeat β-hCG and transvaginal US in **7–10 days**.
  [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- Stable PUL: waiting, repeating β-hCG and US does less harm than presumptive ectopic
  treatment; no methotrexate if an IUP is possible. [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]

### Recommended impression wording (review, Fig. 19, checked on the rendered figure)

| Situation | Wording | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Embryo with cardiac activity | Viable IUP at __ age | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Sac without yolk sac/embryo, or embryo ≤ 4 mm without heartbeat, no suspicious sign | "IUP at __ age of unknown viability" → "Early intrauterine gestational sac at __ age" (probably normal: empty sac, sac with yolk sac but no embryo, or embryo ≤ 4 mm without cardiac activity) | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Any suspicious criterion or poor-prognosis sign | "IUP at __ age of unknown viability" → "IUP at __ age with findings suspicious for pregnancy failure" (the text prefers "IUP of unknown viability" also for suspicious findings) | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Positive test, no IUP or ectopic seen | Pregnancy of unknown location | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |
| Diagnostic criterion met | Nonviable IUP at __ age | [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015] |

## 5. Pitfalls

- Using abandoned criteria: empty sac ≥ 8 mm without yolk sac; CRL ≥ 5 mm without heartbeat
  (8.3% false positives in one series; CRL 6 mm without heartbeat later viable); MSD ≥ 16 mm
  without embryo (4.4% false positives; 17–21 mm sacs later viable). [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- "No embryo ≥ 6 weeks after LMP" is only suspicious: LMP is unreliable.
- Spectral Doppler is discouraged in the first trimester when a normal IUP is possible.
- β-hCG "discriminatory zone": reported between 1,000 and 2,000 mIU/mL but less reliable than
  thought; embryos with heartbeat have followed scans without a sac at β-hCG above
  2,000–3,000; multiple pregnancies have higher β-hCG. Empty uterus with high β-hCG (especially
  > 3,000) greatly raises ectopic likelihood but ~0.5% chance of viable IUP remains. **A single
  β-hCG does not distinguish the three PUL hypotheses.** [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- A minority of ectopics have a small intrauterine collection; complex free fluid represents
  blood (differentials with a possible IUP: ruptured haemorrhagic cyst, heterotopic pregnancy).
  [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- The impact of conservative criteria is small: ~12% of pregnancies formerly labelled
  non-viable become "suspicious" and need follow-up. [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- Minimum exam quality (Doubilet Table 1): transvaginal assessment of uterus and adnexa plus
  transabdominal look for free fluid and a high pelvic mass; trained physician oversight;
  certified operators; equipment adequate for early first-trimester structures.
  [doubilet-nonviable-2013, Table 1]
- Transabdominal US alone may suffice to diagnose failure when CRL ≥ 15 mm without cardiac
  activity (Table 1 footnote); double-sac and intradecidual signs are absent in ≥ 35% of sacs;
  interobserver CRL variation ±15% underlies the 7-mm cut-off. [doubilet-nonviable-2013]
- Heart-rate lower limit: 120 bpm at 6.3–7.0 weeks in the text, 6.5–7.0 weeks in Table 1.
  [rodgers-early-pregnancy-2015]
- Figure 19 says "embryo ≤ 4 mm" and the text "< 4 mm"; either way, a 4 mm embryo without
  heartbeat is suspicious (CRL < 7 mm), never diagnostic.

## 6. What the source does not say

- The review does **not** list "pseudosac", "blighted ovum" or "anembryonic pregnancy" as terms
  to avoid; avoiding them is an inference from its principles, not its text.
- It does not detail the US signs of ectopic pregnancy.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Other bodies (e.g. NICE, ISUOG) publish their own criteria; not summarized here (no entries yet).
