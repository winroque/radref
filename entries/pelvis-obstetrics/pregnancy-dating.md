# Pregnancy dating by ultrasound

> The **first-trimester crown–rump length (CRL)** is the most accurate way to establish or
> confirm gestational age; once dated by an accurate early scan, the due date is not changed by
> later scans. From 14 weeks, dating uses head and limb biometry with progressively wider error.
> The due date is changed when the ultrasound–LMP discrepancy exceeds a gestational-age-specific
> threshold. [acog-co700-2017; isuog-biometry-2019]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** singleton pregnancies dated by last menstrual period (LMP) and/or ultrasound.
- **Assisted reproduction:** use the ART-derived age (e.g. IVF: embryo age and transfer date —
  due date 261 days after transfer of a day-5 embryo, 263 days after a day-3 embryo), not the
  CRL. [acog-co700-2017; isuog-11-14-2023]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Due date by LMP | 280 days after the first day of the LMP (assumes a 28-day cycle, ovulation on day 14) | [acog-co700-2017] |
| CRL technique | True midsagittal plane, fetus neutral (neither flexed nor hyperextended), horizontal on screen with the line at ~90° to the beam, image magnified; longest straight line crown to rump, with the genital tubercle and spine longitudinally in view (ACOG). Mean of three measurements when possible (ACOG); ISUOG 2019 dates by CRL at **8–14 weeks**; best-quality CRL between **45 and 84 mm** when several were taken (ISUOG 2023) | [acog-co700-2017; isuog-biometry-2019; isuog-11-14-2023] |
| Mean sac diameter | **Not** recommended for estimating the due date | [acog-co700-2017] |
| Suboptimally dated | No ultrasound confirming or revising the due date before **22 + 0 weeks** | [acog-co700-2017] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Accuracy and redating thresholds (ACOG/AIUM/SMFM, Table 1)

| GA by LMP | Method | Accuracy of US (text) | Change the due date if US–LMP discrepancy is (Table 1) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 8 + 6 wk | CRL | ±5–7 days (whole first trimester) | **> 5 days** | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |
| 9 + 0 to 13 + 6 wk | CRL | ±5–7 days | **> 7 days** | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |
| 14 + 0 to 15 + 6 wk | BPD, HC, AC, FL | ±7–10 days (14–21 wk) | **> 7 days** | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |
| 16 + 0 to 21 + 6 wk | BPD, HC, AC, FL | ±7–10 days | **> 10 days** | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |
| 22 + 0 to 27 + 6 wk | BPD, HC, AC, FL | ±10–14 days | **> 14 days** | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |
| ≥ 28 + 0 wk | BPD, HC, AC, FL | ±21–30 days | **> 21 days** (only if it is the first scan; risk of redating a growth-restricted fetus) | [acog-co700-2017, Table 1; text pp. e151–e152] |

Smaller discrepancies may justify redating depending on how early the scan was and how
reliable the LMP is. [acog-co700-2017]

### Which CRL chart

| Chart | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| INTERGROWTH-21st (2014) | Prescriptive international standard from 4,265 women in eight countries, CRL measured once at 9 + 0 to 13 + 6 weeks; GA (days) = 40.9041 + 3.21585 × CRL^0.5 + 0.348956 × CRL (CRL in mm), SD of GA = 2.39102 + 0.0193474 × CRL; valid for CRL 15–95 mm. Inverse (size for GA, 58–105 days): mean CRL = −50.6562 + 0.815118 × GA + 0.00535302 × GA², SD = −2.21626 + 0.0984894 × GA. Low-risk naturally conceived singletons with a certain LMP; women with LMP–CRL discrepancy > 7 days excluded | [ig21-crl-2014] |
| Robinson & Fleming (1975) | Original sonar CRL curve, 334 measurements at 6–14 weeks: t (days) = 8.052 × √CRL + 23.73; 95% interval ±4.7 days for one measurement, ±2.7 days with three | [robinson-crl-1975] |
| ISUOG 2023 | Older charts are still widely used, but **recent, international, prescriptive charts** (it cites INTERGROWTH-21st) are recommended | [isuog-11-14-2023] |

### After the first trimester

- CRL loses accuracy beyond **84 mm** (~14 + 0 weeks): use HC (ISUOG) or composite biometry
  (ACOG). HC, with or without FL, can date a pregnancy from the mid-trimester when there was
  no first-trimester scan and the LMP is unreliable. [isuog-biometry-2019; acog-co700-2017]
- Hadlock 1984 (361 fetuses, 14–42 weeks, cross-sectional): HC and FL were the best single
  predictors; combinations (best with all four of BPD, HC, AC, FL) reduced variability, and
  simple averaging of single-parameter ages performed as well as the regression equations.
  [hadlock-ga-1984]

## 4. Management

- Document GA and due date as soon as LMP and/or the first accurate scan are available; later
  changes only in rare circumstances, discussed and documented. [acog-co700-2017]
- After an accurate early scan, later scans assess **interval growth**, not age.
  [isuog-biometry-2019; acog-co700-2017]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Redating a small fetus in the third trimester (may hide growth restriction).
- Hadlock 1984: over- or underestimates age in symmetrically large or small fetuses; exclude BPD
  if the cephalic index is < 70 or > 86; FL/BPD 79 ± 8 and FL/AC 22 ± 2 flag a disproportionate
  parameter; with all four parameters SD 1.02 weeks and maximum error 3.2 weeks (BPD alone 1.36
  and 5.1). [hadlock-ga-1984]
- ACOG: if LMP is uncertain, date by the earliest CRL; GA at delivery = "best obstetric estimate"
  for certificates and research; in the second trimester, smaller discrepancies (10–14 days) may
  justify redating. [acog-co700-2017]
- "Normalizing" a small CRL by changing dates when the fetus has structural anomalies (CRL is
  reduced in trisomy 18 and triploidy). [isuog-11-14-2023]
- Using a calculated GA instead of the CRL itself as the reference for NT, uterine-artery PI
  and serum markers. [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 6. What the source does not say

- ACOG 700 does not name a CRL chart.
- Robinson & Fleming do not print the unit of t next to the equation; the values match days
  (CRL 45 mm → about 78 days). They also warn that their Table I must be re-corrected for the
  systematic errors of each machine. [robinson-crl-1975]
- The Hadlock 1984 regression coefficients are not reproduced here (editorial choice; Tables
  I–III are legible in the PDF).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Upper CRL limit for dating: 84 mm (ACOG, ISUOG); the INTERGROWTH-21st equations were built
  for CRL 15–95 mm.
- ACOG 700 replaced Committee Opinion 611 (2014).

## 8. How to cite

- Redating thresholds: `acog-co700-2017`
- CRL chart: `ig21-crl-2014` (or `robinson-crl-1975` if that chart is the one used)
