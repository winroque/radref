# Obstetric Doppler (ISUOG 2021) and reference ranges

> How to obtain reproducible uterine artery (UtA), umbilical artery (UA), middle cerebral artery
> (MCA) and ductus venosus (DV) waveforms, which index to use (**PI**), and which published
> ranges turn a value into a percentile. Reference ranges depend on technique and route; use the
> same technique as the chart. [isuog-doppler-2021]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** spectral, colour and power Doppler of the maternal–fetal circulation; not
  clinical indications, timing or fetal echocardiography. [isuog-doppler-2021]
- Safety: thermal index **≤ 1.0**; exposure usually no longer than 5–10 min, with special care in
  early pregnancy.
  [isuog-doppler-2021]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PI | (S − D) / TAMX — the recommended index for arteries (linear with resistance; defined with absent/reversed diastole) | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| PIV | (S − a) / TAMX — the recommended index for veins (DV); PVIV is less used | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| RI; S/D | (S − D)/S; S/D | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| CPR; UCR | MCA-PI / UA-PI; its inverse UA-PI / MCA-PI — interpret with GA-specific ranges, not a single cut-off | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| Notch | Reduced early-diastolic velocity before the maximum diastolic velocity (UtA) | [isuog-doppler-2021] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### General acquisition

- No fetal breathing or movement; angle as close to 0° as possible (10° ≈ 2% velocity error,
  20° ≈ 6%); wall filter ≤ 50–60 Hz; 4–6 (no more than 8–10) cycles on screen; waveform filling
  ≥ 75% of the display; avoid triplex mode; repeat and report the best recording.
  [isuog-doppler-2021]

### Vessel technique

| Vessel | Technique | Source |
|---|---|---|
| UtA, first trimester | Transabdominal or transvaginal; sagittal section, move laterally to the paracervical plexus; sample at the internal-os level **before branching** into arcuate arteries; PSV < 60 cm/s (5th centile) → check gate position [isuog-doppler-2021]. 11–14 weeks: **transabdominal**, at the same scan as the NT (CRL 45–84 mm), 2-mm gate, angle < 30°, three similar waveforms, automatic tracing, **mean of right and left PI**, by trained/certified operators (e.g. FMF) [isuog-11-14-2023] | [isuog-doppler-2021; isuog-11-14-2023] |
| UtA, 2nd–3rd trimester | Transabdominal: gate **1 cm downstream** of the apparent crossing with the external iliac artery (or before an early bifurcation); report right and left and the presence of notching; use ranges for the same route; unreliable in uterine anomalies | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| UA | By convention in a **free cord loop** (impedance is highest at the fetal end); in multiples, near the abdominal insertion with matching charts; single UA → interpret with caution | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| MCA | Axial plane with thalami and sphenoid wings; gate on the **proximal third** near the internal carotid origin; angle ~0°; no pressure on the head; ≥ 3 and < 10 waveforms; PSV = highest point. MCA-PI interobserver agreement only moderate — repeat | [isuog-doppler-2021] |
| DV | Sagittal or oblique upper-abdominal plane; gate at the isthmic inlet (colour aliasing); small gate in early or compromised pregnancy; usually triphasic, velocities 55–90 cm/s in the second half of pregnancy | [isuog-doppler-2021] |

### Reference ranges

| Range | Population and method | Key values | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean UtA-PI, 11–41 weeks (Gómez 2008) | 620 women (20 per week), Barcelona; transvaginal at 11–14 weeks, transabdominal at 15–41 weeks | Mean / 95th centile: 1.79 / **2.70** at 11 weeks; 0.70 / **0.99** at 34 weeks; 0.65 / **0.89** at 41 weeks. ln(mean PI) = 1.39 − 0.012 × GA + 0.0000198 × GA² (GA in days) | [gomez-uta-pi-2008] |
| UA-PI, MCA-PI, CPR (FMF, Ciobanu 2019) | 72,387 routine pregnancies at 20–22, 31–33, 35–36 and 41 weeks (FMF-certified sonographers), not restricted to normal pregnancies | MCA-PI and CPR peak around 32 and 34 weeks, then fall; UA-PI falls linearly from 20 to 42 weeks; values shift with maternal characteristics (equations in the paper's methods/supplement) | [fmf-ua-mca-cpr-2019] |
| MCA-PSV for fetal anaemia (Mari 2000) | 111 fetuses at risk from red-cell alloimmunization vs 265 normal fetuses | PSV expressed as **multiples of the median (MoM)** for GA; above **1.50 MoM** detected all moderate or severe anaemia (sensitivity 100%), false-positive rate 12%. Thresholds: 1.29 MoM mild, 1.50 moderate, 1.55 severe; expected median MCA-PSV = e^(2.31 + 0.046 × GA) | [mari-mca-psv-2000] |

## 4. Management

- Clinical use of UA-PI, CPR, DV and UtA-PI in growth restriction: see `fgr-sga-isuog-2020`.
- First-trimester UtA-PI enters combined pre-eclampsia screening (maternal factors, MAP, PlGF);
  risk ≥ 1 in 100 → aspirin 150 mg nightly from 11–15 + 6 weeks to 36 weeks.
  [isuog-11-14-2023]

## 5. Pitfalls

- High wall filter mimicking absent end-diastolic flow. [isuog-doppler-2021]
- MCA sampled distally (PSV falls with distance from the origin) or with head compression.
- Comparing a transvaginal UtA-PI with a transabdominal range.
- Using an MCA-PSV chart without the MoM approach and angle near 0°. Angle correction is allowed
  when 0° cannot be obtained, with the angle recorded in the report; the far-field MCA is
  acceptable if 0° is easier (grade C). [isuog-doppler-2021]

## 6. What the source does not say

- ISUOG 2021 does not impose one chart, but it cites Ciobanu 2019 (FMF) for CPR/UCR and Gómez 2008
  (transabdominal) and Jurkovic (transvaginal) for UtA.
- The Ciobanu median/SD equations and the Mari MoM table are not reproduced here.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- No high-level evidence yet on how CPR or UCR should drive management. [isuog-doppler-2021]
