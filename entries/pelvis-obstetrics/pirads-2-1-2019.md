# PI-RADS v2.1 (2019)

> Consensus update of PI-RADS v2 by the PI-RADS Steering Committee. It keeps the v2 framework
> (a score per sequence, combined into an overall assessment category 1–5 for clinically
> significant prostate cancer) and changes acquisition parameters, the scoring of the
> **transition zone (TZ)**, the definitions of **DWI scores 2 and 3**, the definition of a
> **negative DCE**, the role of biparametric MRI and how to measure **prostate volume**.
> [pirads-2-1-2019]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** multiparametric prostate MRI for detection of clinically significant cancer in
  treatment-naïve glands. [pirads-2-1-2019]
- **Does not apply to:** local recurrence after treatment (current PI-RADS criteria do not
  apply; DCE remains essential there). [pirads-2-1-2019]

### Acquisition changes [pirads-2-1-2019, §2]

| Item | v2.1 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| T2W | Always axial (straight or oblique along the prostate long axis) **plus at least one** orthogonal plane (sagittal and/or coronal) | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| DWI for ADC | One low b value **0–100 s/mm²** (preferably 50–100) and one intermediate **800–1000 s/mm²**; highest b used for ADC = 1000 (kurtosis effects above it) | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| High b value | **≥ 1400 s/mm²**, mandatory; preferably a separate acquisition, or calculated | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| DCE | Temporal resolution **≤ 15 s** (v2: ≤ 10 s, < 7 s preferred; faster allowed if spatial resolution and quality are kept); **3D T1 GRE preferred** | [pirads-2-1-2019] |

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Typical TZ nodule | Round, completely encapsulated nodule (BPH) — a normal variant, **T2W score 1**; need not be reported | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| Atypical TZ nodule | Mostly encapsulated nodule, or homogeneous circumscribed nodule without encapsulation; also a homogeneous mildly hypointense area between nodules — **T2W score 2** | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| "Marked" (DWI) | A more pronounced signal change than any other focus in the same zone | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| Normal central zone | Symmetric low T2/ADC signal around the ejaculatory ducts from base to verumontanum, symmetric mildly high on high-b DWI, no early enhancement | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| Normal AFMS | Symmetric crescentic low signal (like pelvic muscles) on T2W, ADC and high-b DWI, no early enhancement | [pirads-2-1-2019] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Transition zone — T2W score (v2.1)

| T2W | Appearance | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal TZ (rare) or round, completely encapsulated nodule ("typical") | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 2 | Mostly encapsulated or homogeneous circumscribed non-encapsulated nodule ("atypical"), or homogeneous mildly hypointense area between nodules | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 3 | Heterogeneous signal with obscured margins, and others not qualifying as 2, 4 or 5 (unchanged) | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 4 | Lenticular or non-circumscribed, homogeneous, moderately hypointense, **< 1.5 cm** (unchanged) | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 5 | As 4 but **≥ 1.5 cm**, or definite extraprostatic extension/invasive behaviour (unchanged) | [pirads-2-1-2019] |

Assess shape and margins in **at least two planes**. [pirads-2-1-2019]

### Transition zone — overall category (Table 1)

| T2W | DWI | DCE | Overall | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any | Any | 1 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 2 | ≤ 3 | Any | 2 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 2 | ≥ 4 | Any | 3 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 3 | ≤ 4 | Any | 3 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 3 | 5 | Any | 4 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 4 | Any | Any | 4 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |
| 5 | Any | Any | 5 | [pirads-2-1-2019, Table 1] |

DWI 3 does **not** upgrade a TZ T2W 1 or 2 finding (mild/moderate restriction is common in
stromal hyperplasia). [pirads-2-1-2019]

### What to score in the TZ

- Score: findings that differ from the background — a lesion between nodules, or a nodule, with
  clearly more restricted diffusion than the background; or a lesion with obscured margins,
  lenticular shape or invasive behaviour on T2W even without different diffusion.
- Do not score: restricted diffusion shared by many similar nodules (it is a feature of the
  background). [pirads-2-1-2019]

### DWI score (all zones), v2.1

| DWI | Appearance | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unchanged from v2 | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 2 | **Linear/wedge-shaped** hypointense on ADC and/or linear/wedge-shaped hyperintense on high-b DWI | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 3 | **Focal** (discrete, different from background) hypointense on ADC and/or hyperintense on high-b DWI; may be markedly so on ADC **or** high-b DWI, **but not both** | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| 4–5 | Unchanged from v2 | [pirads-2-1-2019] |

### DCE

| Result | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Positive (unchanged) | Focal; earlier than or simultaneous with adjacent normal tissue; corresponds to a suspicious T2W and/or DWI finding | [pirads-2-1-2019] |
| Negative (revised) | No early or contemporaneous enhancement; **or diffuse multifocal enhancement not corresponding to a focal T2W/DWI finding** | [pirads-2-1-2019] |

### Central zone and anterior fibromuscular stroma

- CZ: focal early enhancement and/or right–left asymmetry on T2W, ADC or high-b DWI may indicate
  cancer; asymmetry in **size alone** may be normal (e.g. distorted by BPH). [pirads-2-1-2019]
- AFMS: cancer does not originate there; score a lesion involving it with **PZ or TZ criteria**,
  according to the zone it most likely comes from. [pirads-2-1-2019]

## 4. Management

### Biparametric MRI (no DCE)

- Supported for research and select indications. **mpMRI is preferred** for: prior negative
  biopsy with unexplained raised PSA; active surveillance with fast PSA doubling or changing
  status; repeat imaging after a non-suspicious bpMRI with persistent suspicion; prior prostate
  interventions (TRUS/TURP/BPH therapy, radiotherapy, focal therapy, embolization) or
  drug/hormonal therapy changing morphology (e.g. testosterone, 5-alpha reductase); high-risk
  biopsy-naïve men (family history, genetic predisposition, high risk scores); expected degraded
  DWI (e.g. hip implant). [pirads-2-1-2019]
- Without DCE: TZ assessment is unchanged; in the PZ, DWI-3 lesions **will not be upgraded**, so
  more category 3 and fewer category 4 assessments are expected. [pirads-2-1-2019]

### Prostate volume

- Ellipsoid formula: AP × transverse × longitudinal × **0.52**; **AP and longitudinal on the
  midsagittal T2W**, transverse on the axial T2W. PSA density = serum PSA / prostate volume (ng/mL²).
  [pirads-2-1-2019, Fig. 9]

### Sector map

- 41 sectors: 38 for the prostate (two new sectors at the base: right and left posterior medial PZ), two for the seminal vesicles,
  one for the membranous urethra. [pirads-2-1-2019, Fig. 10]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Scoring typical BPH nodules as 2 (v2 habit): in v2.1 they are T2W 1, and an exam with nothing
  above 1 is reported as **PI-RADS 1**.
- Upgrading a TZ atypical nodule for DWI 3 (only DWI ≥ 4 upgrades it to 3).
- Calling DWI 4 when only one of ADC or high-b DWI is markedly abnormal (that is DWI 3).
- Calling DCE positive for diffuse multifocal enhancement without a focal T2W/DWI correlate.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not reprint the full peripheral-zone scoring table from v2 (DWI-dominant; positive
  DCE upgrades DWI 3, as §5 confirms; §4 of the source describes the v2 DCE role as upgrading PZ
  lesions "with a T2W score of 3–4", an apparent slip). The ACR PI-RADS v2.1 document has the complete tables.
- It gives no management or biopsy thresholds per category.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- v2.1 replaces v2 (2015/2016). bpMRI was left open pending multicentre multi-reader data.
- Letters after publication (Park; reply by Rosenkrantz et al, Eur Urol 2019) discussed the TZ
  changes; they are not summarized here.
