# Kidney transplant Doppler

> Duplex Doppler of the transplant is the usually appropriate first test for graft dysfunction;
> when it suggests an arterial cause, MRA or CTA follows. Transplant renal artery stenosis (TRAS)
> criteria are less settled than for native arteries: velocities at the anastomosis are high in
> many normal grafts, and a three-criterion risk score (PSV ≥ 300 cm/s, spectral broadening,
> intrarenal acceleration time ≥ 0.1 s) has been validated in one centre.
> [acr-ac-transplant-2024] [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] [fananapazir-tras-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adult kidney transplants after surgery; arterial, venous and parenchymal
  Doppler findings.
- **Does not apply to:** native kidneys (see `renal-artery-stenosis-duplex`); the immediate
  post-operative days for the risk score (Fananapazir excluded studies < 28 days).
  [fananapazir-tras-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Anastomotic PSV (RAA PSV) | Angle-corrected PSV at the renal artery anastomosis | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |
| RAA:EIA ratio | Anastomotic PSV divided by external iliac artery PSV | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |
| Spectral broadening | Complete filling of the spectral window in the transplant renal artery | [fananapazir-tras-2022] |
| Parvus–tardus (risk-score definition) | Acceleration time ≥ 0.1 s in segmental or interlobar arteries | [fananapazir-tras-2022] |
| Iliac inflow | Check the iliac artery; damped external iliac flow suggests proximal disease | [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### 3.1 Velocities in grafts without stenosis (Robinson; 844 recipients, single anastomosis)

| Time after transplantation | Mean RAA PSV | PSV > 250 cm/s | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 days (n = 377) | 195 cm/s | 26% | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |
| About 1 month (n = 820) | 206 cm/s | 27% | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |
| About 4 months (n = 785) | 203 cm/s | 26% | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |
| ≥ 1 year (n = 766) | 189 cm/s | 18% | [robinson-tras-velocities-2017] |

At each time point 24–34% of normal grafts had RAA:EIA > 1.8. [robinson-tras-velocities-2017]

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 of 3 | Low | 370 (70%) | 7 (2%) | 0/7 | [fananapazir-tras-2022, Table 1] |

| 2 of 3 | High | 46 (9%) | 23 (50%) | 14/23 (61%) | [fananapazir-tras-2022, Table 1] |

### 3.3 Other thresholds in the sources

| Criterion | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| PSV and ratio | PSV ≥ 2.5 m/s or PSV ratio ≥ 2.5 indicate stenosis | [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] |
| PSV range in studies | 200–300 cm/s proposed; the lower limit has low specificity | [acr-ac-transplant-2024] |
| Renal artery : iliac ratio | Cut-off 1.8 reported with sensitivity 90%, specificity 96.7% | [acr-ac-transplant-2024] |
| Continuity method (Radermacher) | Stenosis ≥ 50% diameter only when the velocity at the aliasing site is ≥ 4 times the velocity just distal to it | [radermacher-ri-transplant-2003] |
| Normal intrarenal waveform | PI usually 0.8–1.5 (RI 0.6–0.75); some well-functioning grafts have higher values; change between visits matters more than one value | [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] |
| Renal vein thrombosis | No venous signal; arterial "to-and-fro" flow (sharp systolic peaks, reversed diastole) | [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] |
| Arteriovenous fistula | Focal high-velocity disturbed flow, low-resistance feeding artery, pulsatile draining vein | [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] |

## 4. Management

- **Initial imaging for graft dysfunction:** duplex Doppler of the transplant is usually
  appropriate (variant 1). [acr-ac-transplant-2024]
- **US suspicious but not conclusive for an arterial cause:** MRA (abdomen and pelvis or pelvis,
  with and without contrast) and CTA are usually appropriate next tests (variant 2).
  [acr-ac-transplant-2024]
- **Risk score:** in the low category no angiogram (of 7) showed TRAS; the authors note that
  most low-risk patients had no angiography, so false negatives are unknown.
  [fananapazir-tras-2022]
- **RI:** prognostic (see `renal-resistive-index`); not diagnostic of rejection.
  [radermacher-ri-transplant-2003] [tublin-ri-2003]

## 5. Pitfalls

- **High normal velocities:** in Robinson's series about a quarter of grafts without stenosis
  exceeded 250 cm/s in the first months, so PSV > 250 cm/s or RAA:EIA > 1.8 alone gives many
  false positives. [robinson-tras-velocities-2017]
- **Tight curvature** at the anastomosis can raise velocities locally. [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024]
- **Intrarenal waveforms** are less helpful than in native kidneys, but a severely damped
  waveform suggests upstream stenosis and should prompt careful study of the anastomosis.
  [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] [tublin-ri-2003]
- **Low prevalence:** recent series put TRAS at about 1–3%. [robinson-tras-velocities-2017]

## 6. What the source does not say

- The ACR AC gives no velocity threshold of its own; it lists the ranges and studies above.
  [acr-ac-transplant-2024]
- Fananapazir validated the score against MRA (DSA when MRA was non-diagnostic) only in
  patients sent to angiography, not in all 530 studies. [fananapazir-tras-2022]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- PSV threshold: 250 cm/s (older studies; EFSUMB) vs 300 cm/s (risk score).
  [efsumb-renal-vessels-2024] [fananapazir-tras-2022] [robinson-tras-velocities-2017]
- The ACR AC attributes to Radermacher 2003 a 47% vs 9% rate of chronic allograft nephropathy;
  in the article those figures are the rates of dialysis (see `renal-resistive-index`).
  [acr-ac-transplant-2024] [radermacher-ri-transplant-2003]
