# Carotid and vertebral Doppler — Brazilian update 2023 (DIC/CBR/SBACV)

> Brazilian recommendations for duplex ultrasound of the carotid and vertebral arteries:
> plaque definition and morphology, multiparametric grading of internal carotid stenosis in
> deciles, near-occlusion and occlusion, CCA/ECA, post-endarterectomy and post-stent criteria,
> vertebral stenosis and subclavian steal, and essentials of transcranial Doppler. Where it
> overlaps the 2019 statement, the 2023 update is the newer document. Recommendation classes
> (I, IIa, IIb, III) and levels (A, B, C) follow the SBC convention. [doppler-carotid-br-2023]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** symptomatic or asymptomatic carotid disease and extracranial vertebral
  disease, adults. Duplex US is the first-choice method (I, B). [doppler-carotid-br-2023,
  Box 3]
- Evaluate CCA, ICA and ECA bilaterally along their whole length, plus the brachiocephalic
  trunk (I, B). CT or MR angiography in symptomatic patients when US cannot grade or is
  non-diagnostic (I, B in Box 4; C in text §5.2). [doppler-carotid-br-2023]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Carotid plaque | Focal structure that (1) protrudes **≥ 0.5 mm** into the lumen; and/or (2) measures **> 50% of adjacent IMT**; and/or (3) has IMT **> 1.5 mm** | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §4.1, Box 4] |
| ASE 2020 plaque grading (I, B) | Emphasis on plaque height and focal vs diffuse: **< 1.5 mm; 1.5–2.4 mm; ≥ 2.5 mm**. Type I = protrusion < 1.5 mm (equated with the first two Mannheim definitions); diffuse IMT ≥ 1.5 mm = type II | [doppler-carotid-br-2023] |
| Plaque echogenicity (types I–V) | I uniformly echolucent; II predominantly echolucent; III predominantly echogenic; IV uniformly echogenic; V calcified. Types II–III higher stroke risk; IV–V more stable | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 6] |
| Plaque surface | Regular < 0.4 mm depth; irregular 0.4–2.0 mm; **ulceration**: De Bray — concavity > 2.0 mm with well-defined base and reversed flow on colour; Muraki — clear concavity with base less echogenic than the adjacent wall; report the size of any mobile component | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 6] |
| Echogenicity relative to neighbours | Hypoechoic (≈ blood), isoechoic (≈ muscle), hyperechoic (brighter than muscle), calcified (≈ bone, with shadow) | [doppler-br-2019, §2.3] |
| Intraplaque haemorrhage | Anechoic area near the surface with intact fibrous cap; vulnerability marker | [doppler-carotid-br-2023] |

Plaque morphology should be in the report (I, B); hypoechoic, heterogeneous and irregular
plaques are stroke/TIA risk markers. 3D plaque burden/volume: IIa, B. Contrast (microbubbles)
for plaque neovascularization: I, B; occlusion vs near-occlusion: IIa, B; dissection: IIa, B;
inflammation: IIb, B. [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Boxes 4, 7]

- **Sequence** (Fig. 3): plaque → PSV < 140 cm/s = < 50% → grade by local lumen reduction
  (anatomic criterion, preferably transverse); PSV > 140 cm/s = > 50% → local measurement +
  velocities (haemodynamic criterion). [doppler-carotid-br-2023]
- **Report stenosis in deciles** (10% bands), both < 50% and > 50% (I, B).
  [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §5.1]
- Do not measure plaques < 20%. [doppler-br-2019, §2.4.1.2]
- ICA criteria do **not** apply to CCA or ECA (I, B).
- Technique: CCA PSV/EDV ~2 cm below the bifurcation in a straight plaque-free segment
  (denominator of ratios); ICA proximal and mid; angle ≤ 60°; spectra at, before and after the
  stenosis. [doppler-br-2019, §2.4.1]

### Residual lumen and NASCET/ECST

- On **CTA**, residual lumen **1.4–2.2 mm** suggests 50–69% (S 75%, E 93.8%); **≤ 1.3 mm** suggests > 70%
  (S 88.2%, E 92.4%, NPV 98%). Lumen ≤ 1.5 mm with haemodynamic change suggests severe stenosis;
  on B-flow < 1.5 mm ≈ 70–99% NASCET. [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §5.2, Table 1]
- NASCET (distal) ≈ ECST (local): < 20 ≈ < 50; 20–29 ≈ 50–55; 30–39 ≈ 58–63; 40–49 ≈ 64–69;
  50–59 ≈ 70–75; 60–69 ≈ 76–81; 70–79 ≈ 82–87; 80–89 ≈ 88–93; 90–99 ≈ 94–99%.
  [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 1]

### Other cut-offs cited in the text (context; grading follows Table 2)

- Consensus 2003 / Joint UK: PSV > 230 for > 70%; 125–230 for 50–69%; EDV > 100 additional for
  > 70%. AbuRahma (asymptomatic ≥ 70%): multiparametric or PSV > 280. PSV ≥ 137 (AbuRahma) and
  ≥ 141 (Petisco) more specific than 125 for ≥ 50%. EDV > 140 for > 80% (specificity > 90%).
  PSV ICA/EDV CCA (St Mary's ratio) less studied — use when other parameters disagree.
  DEGUM and EQUALIS: PSV > 200 and > 230 cm/s for ≥ 50%, > 300 and > 320 cm/s for ≥ 70%.
  Gornik (167 patients, 299 carotids): PSV ≥ 180 cm/s best for ≥ 50% (S 93.3%, E 81.6%), or
  PSV ≥ 125 with PSV ratio ≥ 2.
  [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §5.5.2]
- Correlation with angiography (r): PSV has the best correlation or ties with it (Petisco: PSV
  ratio 0.81 = PSV; Braum: PSV ratio 0.766 > EDV 0.762); the EDV ICA/EDV CCA ratio correlates
  worst (AbuRahma 2011, Petisco 2015, Braum 2008). [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 3]
- Post-stenotic flow (very severe stenosis, shadowing plaque): turbulence, PSV < 30 cm/s,
  prolonged acceleration time; compare contralateral side.

### Near-occlusion and occlusion

- **Near-occlusion:** narrowed lumen with string/trickle flow; velocities high, low or
  undetectable. Not quantified numerically; reported as near-occlusion **with full collapse**
  (marked distal calibre reduction) or **with partial collapse**. CTA criteria for partial
  collapse (I, B): lumen < 1.3 mm; distal ICA < 3.5 mm; ICA/contralateral ICA < 0.87;
  ICA/ipsilateral ECA < 1.27; less opacification than the other side. Near-occlusion vs
  occlusion: observer's opinion on images, not velocities; complement with CTA, MRA or
  angiography; microbubbles IIa, B. [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §5.5.3]
- **Occlusion:** no patent lumen on B-mode and no flow on colour, power and spectral (and
  contrast); high-resistance CCA flow; "staccato" flow at or before the occlusion; possible
  reversed ophthalmic flow (also in > 70% stenosis and near-occlusion). In CCA occlusion the
  ICA may stay patent via the ECA. [doppler-carotid-br-2023]

### CCA and ECA (§5.6, Table 4)

- **CCA > 50%:** PSV at stenosis / PSV pre-stenosis **> 2**, plus lumen reduction on colour/
  power and B-mode; ostial lesions (especially left) poorly assessed.
- **ECA:** Acer 1996 (< 50% PSV < 150; > 60% PSV > 250); Päivänsalo 1996 (≥ 50%: PSV ECA/CCA
  ≥ 2); Kronick 2019 (> 50%: PSV ≥ 200 with aliasing); Shmelev 2019 (≥ 50% without ICA ≥ 50%:
  PSV > 148, ratio ≥ 1.45; with ICA ≥ 50%: PSV > 179, ratio ≥ 1.89). [doppler-carotid-br-2023,
  Table 4]

### Post-endarterectomy and post-stent (§6, Box 5)

| Situation | Criterion | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Post-CEA restenosis > 70% | PSV > 300, EDV > 125, PSV ICA/CCA > 5 | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 5] |
| Post-stent restenosis > 50% | PSV ≥ 220 and ICA/CCA ≥ 2.7 | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 5] |
| Post-stent restenosis > 80% | PSV ≥ 340 and ICA/CCA ≥ 4.15 | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 5] |
| Inadequate stent expansion (0–24 months) | Residual stenosis > 30% | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 5] |
| In-stent restenosis types | I focal at the edge (< 10 mm); II focal intra-stent (< 10 mm); III diffuse (> 10 mm); IV diffuse proliferative (> 10 mm) beyond the edges; V occlusion | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Box 5] |

Post-CEA surveillance at **1, 3 and 12 months** (Bandyk). Restenosis 3–24 months = neointimal
hyperplasia; > 24 months = atherosclerosis (Box 5; the text of §6.3 says 6–12 months and after
24–36 months — internal inconsistency). Expected findings: sutures (regularly spaced bright
echoes), venous patch (may be indistinguishable), Dacron (thick very echogenic), PTFE (bright
double line). US is not the method of choice for stent fracture/kinking. [doppler-carotid-br-2023]

### Vertebral and subclavian

- Normal: diameter 2–4 mm; asymmetry frequent (73%; left larger in 50%); laminar antegrade
  low-resistance flow; **PSV 20–60 cm/s in V2**, 30–100 cm/s at the origin. [doppler-br-2019,
  §2.6.1]
- **Hypoplasia** (Table 5): diameter **≤ 2 mm in V2**; reduced diastolic component; **RI > 0.75**;
  contralateral vertebral enlarged (**> 4 mm**) with normal velocities (2019 adds: calibre
  asymmetry > 1:1.7). [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 5; doppler-br-2019]

| Proximal (V0–V1) stenosis (Hua) | < 50% | 50–69% | 70–99% | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vmax | ≥ 85 cm/s | ≥ 140 cm/s | ≥ 210 cm/s | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 6] |
| Vmax at stenosis / V2 | ≥ 1.3 | ≥ 2.1 | ≥ 4 | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 6] |
| EDV | ≥ 27 cm/s | ≥ 35 cm/s | ≥ 55 cm/s | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 6] |

- V2–V4: no table; multiparametric (turbulence, focal velocity increase, distal damping). V4
  lesion **pre-PICA** gives low-velocity high-resistance V1–V2 curves; **post-PICA** does not
  alter cervical flow (TCD needed). [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §9.5.2]
- Occlusion level (Box 8): proximal — possible distal reconstitution by collaterals; distal
  pre-PICA — low-amplitude, high-resistance, no diastole; V4 post-PICA — normal pattern;
  basilar — distal-occlusion curves in both vertebrals.
- **Subclavian steal** (Box 9), in the ipsilateral vertebral: type 1 latent — systolic
  deceleration (hyperaemia may enhance); type 2 intermittent/partial — systolic reversal;
  type 3 complete — systolic-diastolic reversal. Assess at rest and after reactive hyperaemia.
  [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §9.6]

### Transcranial Doppler — essentials (§10)

- Transducer ≤ 2 MHz; orbital, temporal and foraminal windows; low resistance in all trunk
  arteries except the ophthalmic. [doppler-carotid-br-2023]
- Recommendations (all I, B): silent microembolism — blinded TCD with headframe, continuous MCA
  or basilar monitoring for **≥ 4 h** (the text of §10.4.2 says ≥ 1 h in symptomatic and 4 h in
  asymptomatic patients); vasomotor reserve before endarterectomy; peri-operative
  monitoring and **≥ 90 min** after endarterectomy; include V4 and basilar in carotid/vertebral
  exams of symptomatic patients without an explaining extracranial lesion.
- HITS: transient (< 300 ms) high-intensity signals; distal microembolism = **7.5×** risk of
  recurrent stroke/TIA; ACES: annual ipsilateral stroke/TIA 7.1% with HITS vs 3.0% without.
  Vasomotor reserve (CO₂): flow increase **< 10%** = impaired. [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §10.4]

## 4. Management

- Report content (2019 §2.4.3): transducer, technical quality/limitations; plaques (site, extent,
  morphology, stenosis in deciles); other findings. [doppler-br-2019, §2.4.3] Also useful, from
  other sections: velocities and ratios (Table 2), near-occlusion with full/partial collapse
  (2023 §5.2), IMT vs P75 when measured (2019 §2.2.4), the size of any mobile component (2023 Box 6).
  [doppler-carotid-br-2023, Table 2; Box 6; §5.2; doppler-br-2019, §2.2.4]
- Dissection: CTA and MRA are the methods of choice (I, C); US less accurate; microbubbles
  IIa, B. [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §7.2.1, §8.8]

## 5. Pitfalls

Conditions altering velocities (cardiac effects are systemic and bilateral):

| Condition | Effect | Alternative | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proximal CCA or brachiocephalic stenosis | ↓ PSV and EDV | Ratios + anatomic criterion | [doppler-br-2019, Table 7] |
| Significant contralateral stenosis/occlusion | ↑ velocities (compensatory) | Ratios + anatomic criterion | [doppler-br-2019, Table 7] |
| Arrhythmia (AF) | Variable peaks | Regular period or mean of 5 beats + anatomic | [doppler-br-2019, Table 7] |
| Aortic stenosis | ↓ PSV and EDV | Ratios + anatomic | [doppler-br-2019, Table 7] |
| Aortic regurgitation | ↑ PSV; possible diastolic reversal | Anatomic or ratio without EDV | [doppler-br-2019, Table 7] |

Calcified shadowing plaque and poor machine settings also impair anatomic grading. Surface
analysis is less accurate peri-operatively and with calcium or stenotic plaques.

## 6. What the source does not say

- The ICA table is not to be applied to CCA or ECA.
- No velocity table for V2–V4 vertebral stenosis.
- 3D plaque volume has no cut-offs yet.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- The 2023 update supersedes the carotid/vertebral sections of the 2019 statement where they
  overlap; the 2019 statement remains the source for IMT technique, normative tables, velocity
  confounders and other territories (`doppler-br-2019-territories`, `carotid-imt-reference-values`).
- International criteria (SRU 2003, Joint UK, ESVS) use different bands; cited in the text for
  context.
