# Vascular ultrasound — DIC/SBC 2019, non-carotid territories

> Diagnostic criteria and report content from the 2019 Brazilian vascular ultrasound statement
> for the abdominal aorta and its branches, lower-limb arteries, haemodialysis fistulae, deep
> vein thrombosis and transcranial Doppler applications. [doppler-br-2019]

## 1. Scope

- Abdominal aorta, aortoiliac, mesenteric and renal arteries; lower-limb arteries; AV fistula
  for haemodialysis; DVT; TCD (vasospasm, sickle cell disease, PFO, brain death).
  [doppler-br-2019]
- Carotid and vertebral arteries: see the 2023 update (`carotid-vertebral-doppler-br-2023`).

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AAA | Localized dilatation **≥ 50%** of proximal/normal diameter involving all layers; mean infrarrenal aorta ~2.0 cm, upper normal < 3.0 cm; **AAA = aorta > 3.0 cm** | [doppler-br-2019, §3.1] |
| AAA measurement | AP diameter at maximal systolic expansion, stating **outer-to-outer (OTO)** or **inner-to-inner (ITI)** | [doppler-br-2019, §3.1] |
| Proximal DVT | Popliteal and/or above; distal = infrapopliteal deep veins | [doppler-br-2019, §6] |
| Residual thrombus | Calibre > 2 mm after maximal compression at 6 months, or > 3 mm on two consecutive exams | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |
| AVF flow volume | Flow (mL/min) = mean velocity (cm/s) × r² (cm²) × π × 60; in the draining vein, inner edges, away from turbulence (usually 2–5 cm from anastomosis), mean of 3–5 cycles | [doppler-br-2019, §5.4.2] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Abdominal aorta — screening and follow-up (Table 9)

| Item | Criterion | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | Men 65–75; men 55–75 with family history and/or smoking; women 55–75 with family history and/or smoking | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| 2.6–2.9 cm (subaneurysmal) | Reassess in 5 years | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| 3.0–3.9 cm | 24 months | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| 4.0–4.5 cm | 12 months | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| 4.6–5.0 cm | 6 months | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| > 5.0 cm | 3 months | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |
| Intervention | **≥ 5.5 cm**; symptoms; growth **> 1.0 cm/year** | [doppler-br-2019, Table 9] |

85% infrarenal, 5% involve the suprarenal aorta, 25% have associated iliac aneurysm.

### Aortoiliac and lower-limb arteries (Tables 11, 17)

| Grade | PSV ratio V2/V1 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| < 50% | < 2.0 | [doppler-br-2019, Tables 11, 17] |
| ≥ 50% | ≥ 2.0 | [doppler-br-2019] |
| ≥ 70% | ≥ 4.0 | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Occlusion | No flow | [doppler-br-2019] |

V2 at the lesion, V1 1–2 cm proximal (aortoiliac) or 1–4 cm proximal (limbs); angle ≤ 60°.
Aortoiliac lesions > 50%: sensitivity 86%, specificity 97%. Occlusion: pre-occlusive
high-resistance wave, collaterals, monophasic parvus-tardus distally; hypoechoic concave
interface + staccato suggests embolic occlusion. Peripheral aneurysm: maximal **out-out**
diameter in transverse; pseudoaneurysm: neck with **to-and-fro** flow. [doppler-br-2019]

Waveforms (Table 18, summary): triphasic (normal, young); triphasic without elastic component
(normal elderly); hyperaemic biphasic (inflammation, reactive); post-obstructive biphasic
(after moderate obstruction); biphasic with pandiastolic reversal (vessel feeding
collaterals); to-and-fro (pseudoaneurysm, type II endoleak); acute / damped / extremely damped
monophasic (post-stenotic to post-occlusive with reserve); monophasic without diastole
(post-occlusion without reserve); continuous; reversed (retrograde filling). [doppler-br-2019]

### Renal arteries (Table 15; cm/s)

| Grade | Renal PSV | RAR | Renal EDV | Intrarenal | Stent PSV | Stent RAR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | < 200 | < 3.5 | < 150 | AT < 70 ms | < 390 | < 5 | [doppler-br-2019, Table 15] |
| < 60% | ≥ 200 | < 3.5 | < 150 | AT < 70 ms | < 390 | < 5 | [doppler-br-2019, Table 15] |
| ≥ 60% | ≥ 200 | ≥ 3.5 | < 150 | AT < or ≥ 70 ms | > 390 | ≥ 5 | [doppler-br-2019, Table 15] |
| ≥ 80% | ≥ 200 | ≥ 3.5 | ≥ 150 | AT ≥ 70 ms, tardus-parvus | ≥ 390 | ≥ 5 | [doppler-br-2019, Table 15] |
| Occlusion | — | — | — | Possible tardus-parvus | — | — | [doppler-br-2019, Table 15] |

RAR = renal/aortic PSV ratio (aortic PSV near SMA origin). In the literature renal PSV
cut-offs range 180–250 (the original prints "m/s") and RAR 3.2–3.7. Aortic PSV > 100 or
< 40 cm/s, coarctation, severe LV dysfunction, aortic dissection or aneurysm alter ratios.
AT > 70 ms ≈ 60% stenosis. Kidney length difference of **1.5 cm** suggests significant stenosis
or occlusion; occlusion: no flow + ipsilateral kidney **< 8.5 cm**. [doppler-br-2019, §3.4]

### AV fistula for haemodialysis (§5)

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ideal artery/vein diameter (pre-op) | Elbow 4 mm; wrist 3 mm | [doppler-br-2019, §5.4.1] |
| Minimum functional diameter | Elbow 2 mm; wrist 1.8 mm | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Skin-to-vessel depth | Ideal < 5 mm | [doppler-br-2019] |
| High thrombosis risk in 2–6 weeks | Flow **< 450 mL/min** | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Minimum functional flow | Elbow ≥ 200; wrist ≥ 150 mL/min | [doppler-br-2019] |
| High flow | **> 3,000 mL/min** | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Maturation | Diameter **≥ 6 mm**, flow **≥ 600 mL/min**, depth **≤ 6 mm** | [doppler-br-2019] |
| First cannulation | Minimum 14 days; ideal > 30; if possible 60–90 days | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Targets (Table 19) | Diameter > 4 mm; flow > 500 mL/min; maturation > 30 days | [doppler-br-2019, Table 19] |
| High likelihood of failure | Diameter < 3 mm and flow < 400 mL/min | [doppler-br-2019] |
| Stenosis | V2/V1 ≥ 2 = > 50%; **≥ 4 = significant** | [doppler-br-2019] |

Normal flow: radiocephalic 614 ± 242; PTFE 464 ± 199; mean 514 mL/min. One pre- and two
post-creation exams recommended; maturation failure up to 60%. Non-dominant arm; wrist first,
then elbow, then graft. Contraindications to creation: Paget-Schroetter and DVT.
[doppler-br-2019, §5]

### Deep vein thrombosis (§6, Table 22)

Diagnostic criteria: (1) non-compressibility (main criterion); (2) calibre (normal ≈ 2× the
artery; acute distended; chronic reduced, irregular); (3) wall thickening, trabeculae,
synechiae, valve change = prior recanalized DVT; (4) colour filling defect; (5) spectral:
normal spontaneous, phasic, augments with distal compression, stops with Valsalva; continuous
flow = proximal obstruction/compression; reflux = valve damage. Compression US vs CTA
concordance 95.5% (PIOPED II). [doppler-br-2019, §6]

| | Acute | Subacute | Chronic | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time | ≤ 14 days | 14–28 days | > 28 days | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |
| Calibre | Dilated | Still dilated, normal or slightly reduced | Usually reduced | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |
| Non-compressibility | Total or partial | Total or partial | Partial or absent | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |
| Thrombus | Hypoechoic | Isoechoic | Hyperechoic; wall irregularity; fibrotic residue | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |
| Flow | Absent or partial | Channels; possible microfistula | Multiple channels or filled lumen; collaterals; continuous or low amplitude; reflux standing | [doppler-br-2019, Table 22] |

Technique: compress in transverse **every 5 cm** from femoral to ankle, including calf muscle
veins and great/small saphenous; the statement describes the **complete** exam, not 2-point.
Iliac assessment if the CFV thrombus has no visible end or flow is continuous/low amplitude.
Post-thrombotic syndrome in 30–50% at 1–5 years (severe 5–10%); recurrence ~30% at 8 years.
Recurrence criteria: increased residual mass, extension change, hypoechoic slightly
compressible thrombus with tail, vein diameter ≥ 2× contralateral or artery. [doppler-br-2019]

| Application | Criteria | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Vasospasm after SAH — MCA | Vm 120–130 mild; 130–200 moderate (Lindegaard 3–6); > 200 cm/s severe (Lindegaard > 6) | [doppler-br-2019, Table 26] |
| Vasospasm — other | ACA: > 50% Vm rise in 24 h; PCA > 110; vertebral > 80 cm/s; basilar mild 80–95 (Soustiel 2–2.49), moderate "> 85" (sic; Soustiel 2.5–2.99), severe > 115 (Soustiel > 3); critical period days 4–14 | [doppler-br-2019, Table 26] |
| Sickle cell disease, 2–16 years | Vm < 70 low flow (repeat 30 days); < 170 normal (12 months); 170–184 conditional low (3 months); 184–199 conditional high (30 days); 200–220 abnormal (30 days; > 200 transfusion exchange); **> 220 cm/s imminent stroke risk** | [doppler-br-2019, Table 27] |
| PFO (Spencer) | Grade 0 no HITS; 1: 1–10; 2: 11–30; 3: 31–100; 4: 101–300; 5: > 300 (curtain); > grade 2 = significant shunt | [doppler-br-2019, §7.2] |
| Brain death | SBP ≥ 90 mmHg; systolic peak < 50 cm/s without diastole, or oscillating flow; absent flow is not a criterion without a prior TCD with flow in the same admission | [doppler-br-2019, §7.2] |
| Post-endarterectomy | Up to 70% microembolize in the first hour; 50 signals/h (up to 10%) predicts ipsilateral ischaemia; mean velocity drop > 15% from baseline = risk | [doppler-br-2019, Table 25] |

## 4. Management

- AAA report: technical difficulty; maximal diameter; tortuosity; location (supra-, juxta-,
  infrarenal); shape; mural thrombus; lumen diameter; rupture signs; pre-operatively also neck
  diameter at the lowest renal artery and 15 mm below, neck length and angle, bifurcation
  diameter, aneurysm length, iliac diameters. [doppler-br-2019, §3.1.4]
- AVF report: type and site; functional/dysfunctional/non-functional with cause; afferent and
  efferent flow; diameters; depth. [doppler-br-2019, §5.4.5]
- DVT report: presence; phase; extent with distances to landmarks; residual masses and reflux
  in chronic DVT. [doppler-br-2019, §6.4.2]
- TCD report: indication, quality, windows and vessels (justify those not studied), per-vessel
  patency, direction, PSV/EDV/Vm, PI/RI; conclusion without aetiology. A repeat date is given
  only in sickle cell disease; elsewhere it is not to be suggested. [doppler-br-2019, §7.3.1]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Not stating OTO vs ITI for the aorta (2–4 mm difference between methods).
- Applying limb V2/V1 ratios without a proper proximal reference segment.
- Mesenteric velocities without stating fasting vs post-prandial.
- Respiratory phasicity: lower limbs flow ↓ on inspiration, ↑ on expiration; upper limbs and
  subclavian the opposite. [doppler-br-2019, Table 23]

## 6. What the source does not say

- AAA intervals here differ from other guidelines (ACR 2013, SVS 2018, ESVS 2024); this
  statement does not reconcile them.
- Renal PSV unit in the original is printed as "m/s" — evidently cm/s.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- AAA surveillance: see `acr-incidental-vascular-2013`; SVS 2018 and ESVS 2024 entries pending.
