# Carotid intima-media thickness (IMT) — protocol and normative tables

> How to measure common carotid IMT and compare it with age-, sex- and race-specific
> percentiles, as set out in the 2019 Brazilian vascular ultrasound statement (DIC/SBC), which
> reproduces three normative tables: ELSA-Brasil (40–65 years), CAPS (25–45 years) and MESA
> (65–84 years). IMT is **increased when > P75**. [doppler-br-2019]

## 1. Scope

- **Routine IMT measurement is not recommended in the general population** (class I, level B).
  Without plaque, describing IMT is optional or on request. [doppler-carotid-br-2023]
- **When to measure** (DIC/SBC 2019, Table 1): intermediate cardiovascular risk (to reclassify
  to high risk); familial hypercholesterolaemia; autoimmune disease or use of
  immunosuppressants, corticosteroids, antiretrovirals or other drugs raising cholesterol;
  premature cardiovascular disease in a first-degree relative; < 60 years with a severe
  abnormality in one risk factor; women < 60 years with ≥ 2 risk factors. [doppler-br-2019]
- With plaque present, IMT need not be described unless requested; if the plaque lies at the
  measurement site, include it in the measurement. [doppler-br-2019, §2.2.1]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| IMT | B-mode double line (lumen–intima and media–adventitia interfaces); IMT is the distance between them | [doppler-br-2019, §2.2] |
| Protocol | Fundamental B-mode, **no zoom**, transducer > 7 MHz, depth 3.0–4.0 cm; longitudinal CCA and bifurcation; straightest image with clear double line | [doppler-br-2019, Table 2] |
| Site | **Far wall of right and left CCA, 1 cm proximal to the bifurcation**; automatic or semi-automatic | [doppler-br-2019, §2.2.2] |
| Manual point-to-point | Admitted only when the equipment has no automatic measurement, with strict technique; at least **5 measurements per side** for mean IMT | [doppler-br-2019, §2.2.2] |
| Increased IMT | Mean IMT **> P75** for age, sex and race; use the **higher** percentile of the two sides | [doppler-br-2019, §2.2.3] |
| IMT as plaque | IMT > 1.5 mm characterizes plaque (dyslipidaemia guideline 2017); per ASE 2020, IMT ≥ 1.5 mm may be considered plaque-equivalent, especially if diffuse (type II) | [doppler-carotid-br-2023, §4] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

Values are mean CCA IMT in mm. CCA = common carotid artery. (The original footnotes of Tables 3 and 5 say
"CCE: right common carotid" — a typo; CCE is the **left**.) [doppler-br-2019]

### ELSA-Brasil cohort context (Eickemberg 2019)

- ELSA technique (basis of the ELSA table): Aplio XG (Toshiba), 7.5 MHz linear, centralized
  reading with automatic software; study IMT = mean of right and left CCA mean IMT.
  [eickemberg-elsa-cimt-2019]
- "Healthy" sample of 8,449 adults aged 35–74, from 10,943 with valid images of both CCAs
  (excluded self-reported CVD, lipid-lowering drugs, BMI > 40, bariatric surgery, TG > 800 mg/dL,
  Asian and Indigenous self-classification because of small numbers). Median IMT: men 0.59 mm (IQR 0.52–0.69); women 0.56 mm
  (0.50–0.64). Global P75 (all ages pooled): men 0.69 mm; women 0.64 mm. IMT dichotomized at 1.5 mm
  (plaque threshold) was found in only 4% of men and 2% of women (data not shown in the paper). [eickemberg-elsa-cimt-2019]
- Abdominal adiposity was associated with IMT ≥ P75 (the paper's definition of elevated IMT), mainly waist circumference (adjusted OR
  1.47 men, 1.38 women). [eickemberg-elsa-cimt-2019]

### Original cohorts: what the papers themselves say

| Cohort | Method and sample | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ELSA-Brasil | 10,405 civil servants aged 35–74 (self-reported White, Brown, Black); Toshiba Aplio XG, 7.5 MHz; far wall of a 1-cm CCA segment starting 1 cm below the bifurcation, three cardiac cycles, central reading with MIA software. Black participants had higher IMT than Brown and White (Brown intermediate); men higher than women. The article cites per-side nomograms as "Supplementary data" (supplement not read); they are not in the article | [santos-elsa-cimt-2014] |
| CAPS | 5,056 German primary-care members aged 19–90 (mean 50.1), 7.5–10 MHz; far-wall **CCA-IMT measured 20–60 mm proximal to the flow divider** (bifurcation and bulb measured separately), single systolic frame. Mean follow-up 4.2 years. CCA-IMT predicted MI, stroke and death; per 0.1 mm CCA-IMT, the risk ratio for the combined end point was higher under 50 years than from 50 years (abstract 1.34 vs 1.10; fully adjusted in the Results 1.26 [1.06–1.49] vs 1.08 [1.03–1.14]). The article has no age-percentile table | [lorenz-caps-cimt-2006] |

## 4. Management

- Report mean IMT of each side in mm in the body; in the conclusion, whether it is above or
  below P75, with the table used and its reference. [doppler-br-2019, §2.2.4]

## 5. Pitfalls

- The statement assigns ages to tables (CAPS 25–45, ELSA 40–65, MESA 65–84) but does not say
  how to handle ages between columns, overlap at 40–45, ages < 25 or > 84, or races not in a
  table (MESA has no "pardo"); any rule for these is a local convention.
- A value **equal** to P75 is not above it (increased is > P75).
- CAPS measured CCA-IMT 20–60 mm below the flow divider, ELSA in the 1-cm segment starting
  1 cm below the bifurcation; the statement's protocol (1 cm proximal to the bifurcation) is
  closer to ELSA. The tables are not interchangeable to the hundredth of a millimetre.
- The pooled ELSA P75 (0.69/0.64) is not the comparison standard; comparison is by age, sex
  and race.
- Small differences (hundredths of mm) between exams are within measurement variability; Polak
  et al (Framingham Offspring, NEJM 2011, cited by Eickemberg) associated annual change > 0.02 mm
  with more than twice the stroke risk. [eickemberg-elsa-cimt-2019]
  Do not call progression without the same technique.

## 6. What the source does not say

- The CAPS table has columns to 85 years, but the statement indicates it only for 25–45.
- None of the original articles prints the tables as the statement reproduces them: the CAPS percentiles are not in Lorenz 2006; the MESA
  table is not in Folsom 2008 either, which has no percentile table by age, sex, race and side
  and measures **maximum** IMT (mean of the maxima of near and far walls, both sides) with
  z-scores, not mean far-wall CCA IMT. The statement does not give the exact origin of these
  tables. The ELSA per-side nomograms are cited as "Supplementary data" in Santos 2014; the
  supplement itself was not read.
- The MESA table title says 65–84 years but its columns span 45–84; the statement indicates it
  for 65–84. Some MESA cells are not monotonic in the original (e.g. Hispanic women right CCA
  P25 at 75–84 = 0.63); transcribed as published.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- The 2023 carotid update (`doppler-carotid-br-2023`) keeps IMT optional and adds ASE 2020
  plaque grading; it does not replace these tables.
