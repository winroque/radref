# ESC 2024 — aortic normal values, measurement and aortic plaque; renal and mesenteric imaging

> Gives **upper normal limits** of the aorta by segment and sex (Figure 5), the measurement
> convention by method, a five-grade scale of atherosclerotic aortic plaque by thickness
> (Table 14), and brief imaging statements for renal and mesenteric artery disease.
> [esc-paad-2024]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** adults; aortic diameter reporting on echo/duplex, CT and MR; atherosclerotic
  aortic plaque; renal artery stenosis and chronic mesenteric ischaemia at overview level.
- **Does not apply to:** children (not covered by Figure 5), lower limits of normal, iliac artery
  normal values, indexation beyond the aortic root note in Figure 5.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AAA | Focal dilation at least 1.5 times the normal diameter, generally >= 30 mm | [esc-paad-2024, 9.2.3] |
| Leading-to-leading edge | Echocardiographic convention, end-diastole | [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.1, Fig. 6] |
| Inner-to-inner edge | CT and MR convention, end-diastole | [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.1, Rec. Table 4] |
| Outer-to-outer | Used for the abdominal aorta by ultrasound and whenever the wall is thickened or dissected | [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.1, Fig. 6] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### 3.1 Upper normal diameters (Figure 5; segment numbers follow the SVS/STS classification)

| Segment | Men (mm) | Women (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| Sinus of Valsalva (also < 22 mm/m2) | 40 | 34 |
| Sinotubular junction | 38 | 33 |
| Ascending aorta | 40 | 36 |
| Proximal aortic arch | 37 | 34 |
| Mid aortic arch | 34 | 31 |
| Proximal descending aorta | 29 | 26 |
| Distal descending aorta | 23 | 20 |
| **Abdominal aorta** | **23** | **19** |

Z-scores can be calculated for the root and ascending aorta (links given in the caption). [esc-paad-2024, Fig. 5]

### 3.2 How to measure

| Method | Convention | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Echocardiography | Leading-to-leading edge, end-diastole (Rec., I C); aortic root in parasternal long axis | [esc-paad-2024, Rec. Table 4] |
| CT or MR | **Inner-to-inner** edge, end-diastole (Rec., I C); double-oblique images, not axial (I C) | [esc-paad-2024, Rec. Table 4] |
| Abdominal aorta (US/duplex) | **Outer-to-outer** preferred "given the high incidence of atherosclerotic plaques/thrombi"; best agreement with CT and MR | [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.5, Fig. 6B-C] |
| Thickened wall or dissection | Also report the outer-to-outer diameter | [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.1 and 5.4.5] |

The text adds that the best convention for the abdominal aorta "is under debate". Aortic growth
with age is about 0.9 mm per 10 years in men and 0.7 mm in women. [esc-paad-2024, 5.4.2]

### 3.3 Atherosclerotic aortic plaque grading (Table 14, by atheroma thickness)

| Grade | Severity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal | Intimal thickness < 2 mm |
| 2 | Mild | Intimal thickening 2 to < 3 mm |
| 3 | Moderate | Atheroma >= 3 to < 4 mm, no mobile or ulcerated components |
| 4 | Severe | Atheroma >= 4 mm, no mobile or ulcerated components |
| 5 | Complex | Grade 2, 3 or 4 atheroma plus mobile or ulcerated components |

Severe or complex plaques in the arch or ascending aorta are strongly linked to cerebrovascular
events (odds ratio 4-9.1 for plaques >= 4 mm). [esc-paad-2024, Table 14, 9.1]

### 3.4 Renal artery stenosis (RAS)

- More than 90% of RAS is atherosclerotic, typically ostial; prevalence of >= 60% RAS above
  65 years is 6.8%. [esc-paad-2024, 8.3.2.1.1]
- Duplex: renal artery PSV > 200 cm/s diagnoses > 50% RAS (sensitivity 95%, specificity 90%);
  renal-aortic velocity ratio > 3.5 detects >= 60% RAS (sensitivity 84-91%, specificity 95-97%).
  A side-to-side difference of the intrarenal resistance index is given as an additional criterion; the ESC text prints the threshold as "≥ 0.5" while ESVS 2025 prints "> 0.05" (a resistance index runs from 0 to 1, so the ESC figure may be a misprint, but the source does not say so; the two guidelines disagree and this entry does not choose). [esc-paad-2024, 8.3.2.1.3] [esvs-mesenteric-renal-2025, 8.3.1]
- CTA: sensitivity 64-100%, specificity 92-98%; measures diameter, shows calcification and
  plaque, limited for haemodynamics. Contrast MRA: sensitivity 88%, specificity 100%, overestimates
  the degree by 26-32%. Catheter angiography is the gold standard but should follow DUS and CTA/MRA.
  [esc-paad-2024, 8.3.2.1.3]

### 3.5 Chronic mesenteric ischaemia

- Occlusive CMI is mostly atherosclerotic and affects women more often (65-72%); symptoms
  usually need at least two mesenteric vessels involved. [esc-paad-2024, 8.3.3.2]
- Imaging: duplex (low cost, operator dependent, no agreed criteria); CTA or MRA for anatomical
  mapping; DSA reserved for therapy. [esc-paad-2024, 8.3.3.2]

## 4. Management

Only the points relevant to imaging reports are summarised: asymptomatic non-severe, non-complex
aortic plaques do not mandate antiplatelet therapy; statins are indicated in severe or complex
plaques; anticoagulation or dual antiplatelet therapy are not recommended for aortic plaques
(class III, level C). Prophylactic revascularisation in asymptomatic mesenteric disease is not
recommended. [esc-paad-2024, 9.1, 8.3.3]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Figure 5 gives **upper** limits for a general adult population; there are no lower limits, no
  age bands and no iliac values.
- Convention matters by method: US outer-to-outer versus CT/MR inner-to-inner can differ by
  several millimetres; compare like with like.
- The plaque scale is by thickness (mm) and complexity, not by calcification.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not give a normal abdominal aortic diameter by body size.
- It does not say how to convert between outer-to-outer and inner-to-inner measurements.
- It does not grade RAS as mild, moderate or severe (see ESVS 2025 for the 50-70% and > 70% labels).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Measurement convention: ESC 2024 prefers outer-to-outer for the abdominal aorta by ultrasound
  and inner-to-inner at CT/MR; the SVS 2018 guideline recommends outer-to-outer at CT; ESVS 2024
  recommends no particular caliper method (entry `aaa-surveillance-svs-esvs`).
- RAS CTA specificity: ESC 92-98% versus ESVS 2025 62-98% [esvs-mesenteric-renal-2025].
- The Brazilian Doppler consensus and the ESC upper normal limits may differ for the abdominal
  aorta (entry `doppler-br-2019-territories`).
