# Fleischner Society 2017 guidelines for incidental pulmonary nodules on CT

> Follow-up recommendations for pulmonary nodules found **incidentally** on CT in adults,
> stratified by nodule type (solid, pure ground-glass, part-solid), number (single, multiple),
> size and the patient's risk of lung cancer. Output: no routine follow-up, CT at a given
> interval, or further work-up (PET-CT, tissue sampling). [fleischner-2017]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** incidental pulmonary nodules on CT in adults **≥ 35 years**. [fleischner-2017]
- **Does not apply to:** lung cancer screening (use Lung-RADS), patients with known primary
  cancer, immunosuppressed patients. [fleischner-2017]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nodule size | Average of long and short axes, rounded to the nearest millimetre (table footnote); measured on the transverse, coronal or sagittal image that shows the greatest dimensions | [fleischner-2017] |
| Larger nodules | For nodules and masses larger than 10 mm it is generally appropriate to record both long- and short-axis dimensions (long axis for T staging and response) | [fleischner-2017] |
| Volume equivalents | < 6 mm ≈ < 100 mm³; 6–8 mm ≈ 100–250 mm³; > 8 mm ≈ > 250 mm³ | [fleischner-2017, Table] |
| Low risk | ACCP low risk: estimated cancer risk **< 5%** — young age, less smoking, smaller nodule, regular margins, non–upper-lobe location | [fleischner-2017] |
| High risk | ACCP intermediate (5–65%) and high (> 65%) combined — older age, heavy smoking, larger nodule, irregular or spiculated margins, upper-lobe location | [fleischner-2017] |
| Subsolid | Pure ground-glass or part-solid | [fleischner-2017] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Solid nodules

| Nodule | Low risk | High risk | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single < 6 mm | No routine follow-up | Optional CT at 12 months | [fleischner-2017] |
| Single 6–8 mm | CT at 6–12 months; then consider CT at 18–24 months | CT at 6–12 months, then CT at 18–24 months | [fleischner-2017] |
| Single > 8 mm | Consider CT at 3 months, PET-CT or tissue sampling | Same | [fleischner-2017] |
| Multiple < 6 mm | No routine follow-up | Optional CT at 12 months | [fleischner-2017] |
| Multiple 6–8 mm | CT at 3–6 months; then consider CT at 18–24 months | CT at 3–6 months, then CT at 18–24 months | [fleischner-2017] |
| Multiple > 8 mm | CT at 3–6 months; then consider CT at 18–24 months | CT at 3–6 months, then CT at 18–24 months | [fleischner-2017] |

### Subsolid nodules (no low/high-risk split)

| Nodule | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Single pure ground-glass < 6 mm | No routine follow-up | [fleischner-2017] |
| Single pure ground-glass ≥ 6 mm | CT at 6–12 months to confirm persistence, then every 2 years until 5 years | [fleischner-2017] |
| Single part-solid < 6 mm | No routine follow-up | [fleischner-2017] |
| Single part-solid ≥ 6 mm | CT at 3–6 months; if persistent and solid component < 6 mm, annual CT for 5 years; solid component ≥ 6 mm is highly suspicious | [fleischner-2017] |
| Multiple subsolid < 6 mm | CT at 3–6 months; if stable, consider CT at 2 and 4 years | [fleischner-2017] |
| Multiple subsolid ≥ 6 mm | CT at 3–6 months; subsequent management based on the most suspicious nodule | [fleischner-2017] |

## 4. Management

The table above is the management. Recommendations are minimum intervals for follow-up of a
nodule judged incidental; clinical judgment and patient preference apply. [fleischner-2017]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Applying the table to screening CT or to oncologic patients (out of scope).
- Using the long axis alone instead of the average diameter for nodules < 10 mm.
- Assuming low risk when risk factors are unknown: when risk is not stated, the recommendation
  for both risk levels can be given.
- Rounding: thresholds are applied to the average diameter rounded to the nearest millimetre.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It gives no recommendations for patients < 35 years (out of scope), not "no follow-up".
- It does not define Lung-RADS categories and should not be cited for screening intervals.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- **Lung-RADS** (ACR) governs lung cancer **screening** CT; different size thresholds and
  categories. See `lungrads-2022` (entry pending).
- The 2017 version updates the 2005 guidelines for solid nodules and the separate 2013
  guidelines for subsolid nodules. [fleischner-2017]
