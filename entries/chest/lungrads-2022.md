# ACR Lung-RADS v2022

> Lung-RADS standardizes reporting and management of lung cancer screening (LCS) low-dose CT.
> v2022 (released November 2022) adds classification for atypical pulmonary cysts, extends
> the benign perifissural rule to all juxtapleural nodules, adds guidance for potentially
> infectious/inflammatory findings and airway nodules, redefines growth (with a 12-month
> interval) and slow growth, clarifies the S modifier, and introduces **stepped management**.
> No newer version is described in the 2026 review. [lungrads-2022; petranovic-lungrads-2026]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** lung cancer screening CT only — not incidental nodules on other CT (see
  `fleischner-2017`). [lungrads-2022]
- The "risk of malignancy" column was removed from the category table in v2022.
  [lungrads-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Thin-walled cyst | Unilocular, wall **< 2 mm** — benign, not classified in Lung-RADS | [lungrads-2022] |
| Thick-walled cyst | Unilocular, wall **≥ 2 mm** (uniform, asymmetric or focal nodularity) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Multilocular cyst | Internal septations; may have ground glass or solid components | [lungrads-2022] |
| Benign juxtapleural nodule | Solid, smooth margins, oval/lentiform/triangular, **< 10 mm mean diameter at baseline or new** (a growing juxtapleural nodule does not qualify), perifissural, costal pleural, perimediastinal or peridiaphragmatic | [lungrads-2022, Fig. 5] |
| Large airway | Segmental or more proximal (diameter > 3 mm) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Growth | Increase **> 1.5 mm** in mean diameter **within 12 months** (v1.1: > 1.5 mm with no time interval) | [lungrads-2022; petranovic-lungrads-2026] |
| Size-threshold crossing | A nodule reaching a higher size category is reclassified even without meeting "growth" | [acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 5] |
| Cavitary nodule | Wall thickening dominant → manage as a solid nodule by total mean diameter | [acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 12] |
| Slow-growing | Growth over several screening exams that never reaches > 1.5 mm in any 12-month interval ("≤ 1.5-mm increase in a > 12-month interval") | [lungrads-2022; acr-lungrads-2022-table, notes 7–8] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Assessment categories (ACR table, v2022)

Size = **mean diameter** (long + short axis, one decimal, any plane); volume equivalents in
parentheses. Each exam is coded by the most suspicious finding. [acr-lungrads-2022-table]

| Category | Solid nodule | Part-solid nodule | Non-solid (GGN) | Other | Management |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** Incomplete (~1%) | — | — | — | Prior CT being located; lungs partly unevaluable; findings suggesting infection/inflammation | Comparison; additional LDCT; or 1–3-month LDCT |
| **1** Negative (39%) | No nodules, or benign calcification (complete, central, popcorn, concentric rings) or fat | — | — | — | 12-month LDCT |
| **2** Benign (45%) | Baseline **< 6 mm** (< 113 mm³); new **< 4 mm** (< 34 mm³) | Baseline total **< 6 mm** | **< 30 mm** (< 14,137 mm³) at baseline, new or growing; **≥ 30 mm** stable or slowly growing | Benign juxtapleural nodule; subsegmental airway nodule; category 3 stable/smaller at 6 months; 4B proven benign | 12-month LDCT |
| **3** Probably benign (9%) | Baseline **6 to < 8 mm** (113 to < 268 mm³); new **4 to < 6 mm** | Baseline total **≥ 6 mm** with solid component **< 6 mm**; new total < 6 mm | **≥ 30 mm** at baseline or new | Thick-walled cyst with growing cystic component; category 4A stable/smaller at 3 months (not airway) | 6-month LDCT |
| **4A** Suspicious (4%) | Baseline **8 to < 15 mm** (268 to < 1,767 mm³); growing **< 8 mm**; new **6 to < 8 mm** | Solid component **6 to < 8 mm** at baseline; new or growing solid component **< 4 mm** | — | Segmental or more proximal airway nodule at baseline; thick-walled or multilocular cyst; cyst becoming multilocular | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT may be considered if solid nodule/component ≥ 8 mm |
| **4B** Very suspicious (2%) | Baseline **≥ 15 mm** (≥ 1,767 mm³); new or growing **≥ 8 mm** | Solid component **≥ 8 mm** at baseline; new or growing solid component **≥ 4 mm** | — | Persistent (stable or growing) proximal airway nodule; growing thick-walled or multilocular cyst, or more loculation/new opacity; slow-growing solid or part-solid nodule over several screens | Diagnostic chest CT ± contrast; PET/CT if ≥ 8 mm solid; tissue sampling and/or clinical referral (airway: referral, typically bronchoscopy) |
| **4X** (< 1%) | Category 3 or 4 with extra features raising suspicion (spiculation, lymphadenopathy, metastases, GGN doubling in 1 year, etc.) — a category, not a modifier | | | | Not specified in the table (4X is a distinct category, not a modifier) |
| **S** modifier (10%) | Significant or potentially significant non-lung-cancer findings, added to 0–4 | | | | Per finding (ACR incidental-findings guidance) |

[acr-lungrads-2022-table]

Audit definitions: negative screen = categories 1–2; positive screen = 3–4.
[acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 3]

### Atypical pulmonary cysts (Table 2)

| Lung-RADS | Finding | Management | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Growing cystic component (mean diameter) of a thick-walled cyst | 6-month LDCT | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |
| 4A | Thick-walled cyst, or multilocular cyst at baseline, or thin/thick-walled cyst that becomes multilocular | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT may be considered if solid nodule/component **≥ 8 mm (268 mm³)** | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |
| 4B | Thick-walled cyst with growing wall thickness/nodularity; growing multilocular cyst; multilocular cyst with more loculation or new/increased opacity | Diagnostic chest CT; PET/CT if ≥ 8 mm solid component; tissue sampling and/or clinical referral | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |

A cyst with an associated nodule (endophytic or exophytic) is classified by the most suspicious
finding; when in doubt, choose the higher category. [lungrads-2022]

### Juxtapleural nodules

Benign juxtapleural nodules (definition above) → **Lung-RADS 2**; others are classified by size
and composition. [lungrads-2022, Fig. 5]

### Potentially infectious or inflammatory findings

| Situation | Classification | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Indeterminate infectious/inflammatory process, or findings that obscure lung (segmental/lobar consolidation, > 6 new nodules, large solid nodules ≥ 8 mm appearing in a short interval, new nodules in e.g. immunocompromised patients) | **Lung-RADS 0** with 1- to 3-month LDCT; reclassify at follow-up by the most concerning finding | [lungrads-2022] |
| Malignancy more likely than infection | By size and composition (often 4B) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Clearly infectious findings not needing short-term follow-up (e.g. tree-in-bud, new < 3 cm ground glass) | By size and composition of the most suspicious finding, often Lung-RADS 2 with 12-month LDCT | [lungrads-2022] |

### Airway nodules (Table 3)

| Lung-RADS | Finding | Management | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Subsegmental — baseline, new or stable | 12-month screening LDCT | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |
| 4A | Segmental or more proximal — at baseline | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT if ≥ 8 mm solid nodule/component | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |
| 4B | Segmental or more proximal — stable or growing | Referral for clinical evaluation (typically bronchoscopy); the text also allows the 4B diagnostic work-up, including PET/CT if a solid component ≥ 8 mm; **not** downgraded by stepped management | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |

Features favouring secretions (complex or tubular shape, no soft tissue, air, HU < 21.7) may be
classified Lung-RADS 2; multiple airway opacities favour a non-neoplastic process.
[lungrads-2022, text p. 479] (the Fig. 7 legend says "< 21 Hounsfield units")

## 4. Management

### Stepped management (Table 4)

| Reclassify to | When | Next | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Category 4A lesion stable or decreased on 3-month CT | 6-month LDCT from the current exam | [lungrads-2022, Table 4] |
| 2 | Category 3 lesion stable or decreased on 6-month CT | 12-month screening LDCT from the current exam | [lungrads-2022, Table 4] |

Resolved or proven-benign findings are reclassified; follow-up is timed from the **current**
examination. [lungrads-2022]

### Growth and slow growth

- Compare with the **oldest** available chest CT.
- Slow-growing nodules may be classified as stable until they reach a new size threshold.
- Slow-growing non-solid (ground glass) nodules may stay Lung-RADS 2 until they meet another
  category (e.g. develop a solid component). [lungrads-2022]

### S modifier and interval diagnostic CT

- S modifier (categories 0–4) for significant or potentially significant findings unrelated to
  lung cancer; management per ACR incidental-findings recommendations, in the impression.
  Not needed for findings already known, treated or under evaluation, unless they changed
  unexpectedly. [lungrads-2022]
- A diagnostic CT may substitute for an **annual** (not baseline) screening exam if it meets
  LCS technical parameters except dose. [lungrads-2022]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Downgrading a persistent segmental/proximal airway nodule at 3 months (it becomes 4B).
- Classifying simple thin-walled cysts or diffuse cystic lung disease without concerning
  features. Thin-walled cysts (unilocular, uniform wall < 2 mm) are benign and are not
  classified [acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 12a]; fluid-containing cysts (possible infection)
  and multiple cysts (LCH, LAM) are not classified unless other concerning features are present
  [acr-lungrads-2022-table, notes 12g, 12h].
- Using Lung-RADS thresholds for incidental (non-screening) nodules.
- Ignoring a prior diagnostic CT at baseline: it should be used (e.g. 8-mm nodule stable for
  1 year → 2; new → 4B). Patients no longer eligible still receive a category and management
  from the current exam. After a cancer diagnosis the exam is no longer screening.
  [lungrads-2022, p. 486; acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 16]
- Resolved category 3/4A findings and proven-benign 4B go directly to 1 or 2, outside stepped
  management. [lungrads-2022, Table 4]

## 6. What the source does not say

- Volumetry: volume equivalents are given per category, but measurement stays **mean diameter**;
  the ACR table writes growth as "> 1.5 mm (> 2 mm³)" — the 2 mm³ figure is not explained in
  the summary, the 2024 article or the 2026 review and is implausibly small for a volume change;
  do not use it as a volumetric growth threshold. Volumetry is under discussion by the
  committee (member survey). [acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 6; petranovic-lungrads-2026]
- Juxtapleural size: the ACR table says **< 10 mm** (524 mm³) mean diameter; the ACR summary
  and the 2026 review say **≤ 10 mm**. [acr-lungrads-2022-table; petranovic-lungrads-2026]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- v2022 replaces v1.1 (2019); the risk-of-malignancy column was removed. Ongoing committee
  topics (2026): volumetry vs diameter, subsolid-nodule over-surveillance (NLST data suggest
  GGN early-recall thresholds could be lower), integration of risk models (category 4B
  encourages the Brock/McWilliams calculator), AI, incidental nodules, international
  harmonization. [petranovic-lungrads-2026; acr-lungrads-2022-table, note 13]
- v2022 replaces v1.1 (2019). NCCN recommends 1-month CT for airway nodules; the paper finds
  insufficient data to choose between the approaches. [lungrads-2022]
