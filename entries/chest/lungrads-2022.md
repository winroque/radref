# ACR Lung-RADS v2022 — what changed

> Lung-RADS standardizes reporting and management of lung cancer screening (LCS) low-dose CT.
> v2022 (released November 2022) adds classification for atypical pulmonary cysts, extends
> the benign perifissural rule to all juxtapleural nodules, adds guidance for potentially
> infectious/inflammatory findings and airway nodules, redefines growth (with a 12-month
> interval) and slow growth, clarifies the S modifier, and introduces **stepped management**.
> This entry covers those changes; the full category table (nodule size thresholds) is in the
> ACR v2022 document and is not reproduced here. [lungrads-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** lung cancer screening CT only — not incidental nodules on other CT (see
  `fleischner-2017`). [lungrads-2022]
- The "risk of malignancy" column was removed from the category table in v2022.
  [lungrads-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Thin-walled cyst | Unilocular, wall **< 2 mm** — benign, not classified in Lung-RADS | [lungrads-2022] |
| Thick-walled cyst | Unilocular, wall **≥ 2 mm** (uniform, asymmetric or focal nodularity) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Multilocular cyst | Internal septations; may have ground glass or solid components | [lungrads-2022] |
| Benign juxtapleural nodule | Solid, smooth margins, oval/lentiform/triangular, **< 10 mm mean diameter**, perifissural, costal pleural, perimediastinal or peridiaphragmatic | [lungrads-2022, Fig. 5] |
| Large airway | Segmental or more proximal (diameter > 3 mm) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Growth | Increase **> 1.5 mm** in mean diameter **within 12 months** | [lungrads-2022] |
| Slow-growing | Increase of 1.5 mm over **> 12 months** (does not meet growth from one annual screen to the next) | [lungrads-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Atypical pulmonary cysts (Table 2)

| Lung-RADS | Finding | Management | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Growing cystic component (mean diameter) of a thick-walled cyst | 6-month LDCT | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |
| 4A | Thick-walled cyst, or multilocular cyst at baseline, or thin/thick-walled cyst that becomes multilocular | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT may be considered if solid nodule/component **≥ 8 mm (268 mm³)** | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |
| 4B | Thick-walled cyst with growing wall thickness/nodularity; growing multilocular cyst; multilocular cyst with more loculation or new/increased opacity | Diagnostic chest CT; PET/CT if ≥ 8 mm solid component; tissue sampling and/or clinical referral | [lungrads-2022, Table 2] |

A cyst with an associated nodule (endophytic or exophytic) is classified by the most suspicious
finding; when in doubt, choose the higher category. [lungrads-2022]

### Juxtapleural nodules

Benign juxtapleural nodules (definition above) → **Lung-RADS 2**; others are classified by size
and composition. [lungrads-2022, Fig. 5]

### Potentially infectious or inflammatory findings

| Situation | Classification | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Indeterminate infectious/inflammatory process, or findings that obscure lung (segmental/lobar consolidation, > 6 new nodules, large solid nodules ≥ 8 mm appearing in a short interval, new nodules in e.g. immunocompromised patients) | **Lung-RADS 0** with 1- to 3-month LDCT; reclassify at follow-up by the most concerning finding | [lungrads-2022] |
| Malignancy more likely than infection | By size and composition (often 4B) | [lungrads-2022] |
| Clearly infectious findings not needing short-term follow-up (e.g. tree-in-bud, new < 3 cm ground glass) | By size and composition of the most suspicious finding, often Lung-RADS 2 with 12-month LDCT | [lungrads-2022] |

### Airway nodules (Table 3)

| Lung-RADS | Finding | Management | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Subsegmental — baseline, new or stable | 12-month screening LDCT | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |
| 4A | Segmental or more proximal — at baseline | 3-month LDCT; PET/CT if ≥ 8 mm solid nodule/component | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |
| 4B | Segmental or more proximal — stable or growing | Referral for clinical evaluation (typically bronchoscopy); **not** downgraded by stepped management | [lungrads-2022, Table 3] |

Features favouring secretions (complex or tubular shape, no soft tissue, air, HU < 21.7) may be
classified Lung-RADS 2; multiple airway opacities favour a non-neoplastic process.
[lungrads-2022]

## 4. Management

### Stepped management (Table 4)

| Reclassify to | When | Next | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Category 4A lesion stable or decreased on 3-month CT | 6-month LDCT from the current exam | [lungrads-2022, Table 4] |
| 2 | Category 3 lesion stable or decreased on 6-month CT | 12-month screening LDCT from the current exam | [lungrads-2022, Table 4] |

Resolved or proven-benign findings are reclassified; follow-up is timed from the **current**
examination. [lungrads-2022]

### Growth and slow growth

- Compare with the **oldest** available chest CT.
- Slow-growing nodules may be classified as stable until they reach a new size threshold.
- Slow-growing non-solid (ground glass) nodules may stay Lung-RADS 2 until they meet another
  category (e.g. develop a solid component). [lungrads-2022]

### S modifier and interval diagnostic CT

- S modifier (categories 0–4) for significant or potentially significant findings unrelated to
  lung cancer; management per ACR incidental-findings recommendations, in the impression.
  Not needed for findings already known, treated or under evaluation, unless they changed
  unexpectedly. [lungrads-2022]
- A diagnostic CT may substitute for an **annual** (not baseline) screening exam if it meets
  LCS technical parameters except dose. [lungrads-2022]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Downgrading a persistent segmental/proximal airway nodule at 3 months (it becomes 4B).
- Classifying simple thin-walled cysts or diffuse cystic lung disease without concerning
  features.
- Using Lung-RADS thresholds for incidental (non-screening) nodules.

## 6. What the source does not say

- This article does not list the complete size thresholds of categories 1–4X; they are in the
  ACR Lung-RADS v2022 assessment-category document (not yet added as a reference).
- Volumetry: v1.1 gave volume equivalents, but no volumetric growth threshold was adopted.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- v2022 replaces v1.1 (2019). NCCN recommends 1-month CT for airway nodules; the paper finds
  insufficient data to choose between the approaches. [lungrads-2022]
