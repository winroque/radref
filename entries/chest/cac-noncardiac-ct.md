# Coronary artery calcium on non-contrast chest CT (SCCT/STR 2016, CAC-DRS 2018)

> CAC **should be evaluated and reported on every non-contrast chest CT** (Class I), at least as
> none, mild, moderate or severe (Class I); ordinal scoring is reasonable (IIa) and Agatston
> scoring may be reasonable (IIb). CAC-DRS codes the result as A (Agatston) or V (visual),
> category 0–3, plus the number of involved vessels (/N1–N4).
> [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017; cac-drs-2018]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** non-gated, non-contrast chest CT done for any indication (lung screening,
  staging, other) in patients **≥ 40 years** (SCCT/STR Table 5 footnote), and gated calcium scans for the Agatston part of CAC-DRS.
- **Does not cover:** contrast-enhanced CT or coronary CTA (see `cad-rads-2-2022`).

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Visual estimation | Whole coronary tree rated none / mild / moderate / severe | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017] |
| Ordinal score (Shemesh) | LM, LAD, LCx, RCA each 0–3 (mild < 1/3 of vessel length calcified, moderate 1/3–2/3, severe > 2/3); sum 0–12 | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017] |
| Ordinal score (NLST) | 10 segments each 0–3; sum 0–30 | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017] |
| Agatston score | Summed score from calcified plaque area and peak density, for 120 kV, 2.5–3-mm slices | [cac-drs-2018] |
| N modifier | Number of vessels with CAC (1–4: LM, LAD, LCx, RCA); not used when the category is 0 | [cac-drs-2018] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### SCCT/STR risk wording

| Method | Very low | Mildly increased | Moderately increased | Moderately to severely increased | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agatston | 0 | 1–99 | 100–299 | ≥ 300 | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, Table 6] |
| Visual | None | Mild | Moderate | Severe | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, Table 6] |
| Ordinal 0–12 | 0 | 1–3 (one range spanning mild to moderately increased) | ← | 4–12 | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, Table 6B] |
| Ordinal 0–30 | 0 | 1–5 | 6–11 | 12–30 | [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, Table 6] |

- A percentile > 75th for age, sex and ethnicity (MESA) means moderately to severely increased
  relative risk regardless of the score. [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017]

### CAC-DRS (2018)

| Category | Agatston | Visual | Treatment suggestion in the source | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | none | Statin generally not recommended (except familial hypercholesterolaemia) | [cac-drs-2018, Table 1] |
| 1 | 1–99 | mild | Moderate-intensity statin | [cac-drs-2018, Table 1] |
| 2 | 100–299 | moderate | Moderate- to high-intensity statin + aspirin 81 mg | [cac-drs-2018, Table 1] |
| 3 | > 300 | severe | High-intensity statin + aspirin 81 mg | [cac-drs-2018, Table 1] |

- Notation: `CAC-DRS A2/N3` (Agatston 100–299 in three vessels); `CAC-DRS V0` (no N).
  Visual scoring is for non-gated non-contrast CT only, not for gated calcium scans.
  [cac-drs-2018]
- Report also thoracic aortic, aortic valve, mitral annular and pericardial calcification as
  none/mild/moderate/severe (outside the CAC-DRS category). [cac-drs-2018]
- SCCT/STR: evaluating and reporting thoracic aortic calcification on every non-contrast CT may
  be reasonable (Class IIb). [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, Table 5]
- CAC-DRS also asks for the main pulmonary artery and ascending/descending aorta diameters, and
  for non-cardiovascular findings with their specific management. [cac-drs-2018, §6]
- For Agatston on chest CT, reconstruct at 2.5–3 mm (chest CT is usually ≤ 2 mm).
  [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017, §2.2.1]

## 4. Management

- SCCT/STR: treatment should parallel the risk category; formal treatment recommendations are
  left to other documents. [scct-str-cac-nongated-2017]
- CAC-DRS Table 1 pairs each category with a treatment recommendation (from the 2017 SCCT
  consensus). [cac-drs-2018, Table 1]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Not reporting CAC on a chest CT because it was "not requested".
- Applying gated Agatston percentiles to a non-gated score without saying so.
- Using the visual method on a gated calcium scan. [cac-drs-2018]

## 6. What the source does not say

- No Brazilian percentile tables (MESA is US-based; CAC-DRS notes the gap for other ethnicities).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Boundary at 300: SCCT/STR uses ≥ 300 for the top group; the CAC-DRS table lists 100–299 and
  "> 300", leaving 300 itself unassigned (the text merges 300–1000 and > 1000). Treat 300 as
  category 3.
- CAC-DRS Table 1 words category 0 differently for Agatston ("generally not recommended") and
  visual ("not recommended").
- SCCT/STR was co-published in J Thorac Imaging 2017;32:W54–66.
