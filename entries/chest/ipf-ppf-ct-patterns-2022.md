# UIP CT patterns and progressive pulmonary fibrosis (ATS/ERS/JRS/ALAT 2022)

> Four CT categories for the usual interstitial pneumonia (UIP) pattern in suspected IPF, and
> criteria for progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF) in fibrotic ILD other than IPF. Update of
> the 2018 IPF guideline. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]

## 1. Scope

- **UIP categories:** adults with suspected idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. Clinically suspected
  IPF: unexplained bilateral fibrosis, bibasal crackles, age > 60 (rarely 40–60).
  [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]
- **PPF:** ILD of known or unknown cause **other than IPF**, with radiological evidence of
  fibrosis. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fibrosis (reliable signs) | Traction bronchiectasis/bronchiolectasis and/or honeycombing (a continuum in IPF) | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022] |
| Honeycombing | Clustered cysts with thick walls and similar diameters, 3–10 mm (up to 2.5 cm); a single subpleural layer counts | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Fig. 2] |
| Paraseptal emphysema (mimic) | Single layer, larger cysts (usually > 1 cm), upper lobes, no reticulation or traction | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Fig. 3] |
| Airspace enlargement with fibrosis (mimic) | Smoking-related; asymmetric, larger, irregular clustered cysts, no traction bronchiectasis or other signs of fibrosis; not a distinct idiopathic interstitial pneumonia | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Fig. 4] |
| Clues to fibrotic HP | Upper/mid-zone fibrosis, mosaic attenuation or three-density sign, axially diffuse fibrosis, lobular air trapping on expiration | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Fig. 5] |
| Clues to CTD-ILD | Exuberant honeycombing (> 70% of fibrotic area), straight-edge sign (coronal), anterior upper-lobe predominance | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Fig. 6] |
| Pleuroparenchymal fibroelastosis | Dense apical subpleural fibrosis with volume loss; coexists in 6–10% of IPF and worsens prognosis | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| | UIP | Probable UIP | Indeterminate for UIP | Alternative diagnosis | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confidence for histologic UIP | Confident (> 90%) | Provisional high (70–89%) | Provisional low (51–69%) | Low to very low (≤ 50%) | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Table 3] |
| Distribution | Subpleural and basal; often heterogeneous; occasionally diffuse; may be asymmetric | Subpleural and basal; often heterogeneous | Diffuse, without subpleural predominance | Peribronchovascular with subpleural sparing (NSIP); perilymphatic (sarcoidosis); upper/mid lung (fibrotic HP, CTD, sarcoidosis); subpleural sparing (NSIP, smoking-related) | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Table 3] |
| Features | Honeycombing ± traction bronchiectasis/bronchiolectasis; irregular septal thickening; usually reticulation and mild ground-glass; may have pulmonary ossification | Reticulation with traction bronchiectasis/bronchiolectasis; may have mild ground-glass; **no** subpleural sparing; no honeycombing (honeycombing would make it UIP; Fig. 2 legend) | Fibrosis signs not suggesting any specific aetiology | Lung: cysts (LAM, PLCH, LIP, DIP); mosaic attenuation / three-density sign (HP); predominant ground-glass; profuse centrilobular micronodules; nodules; consolidation. Mediastinum: pleural plaques (asbestosis); dilated oesophagus (CTD) | [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Table 3] |

### PPF — at least 2 of 3 in the past year, without alternative explanation [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Table 4]

Excluding alternative explanations matters most when only symptoms worsen or only DLCO falls
(less specific than FVC and CT). [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022, Table 4 note]

1. Worsening respiratory symptoms.
2. Physiological progression: absolute decline in FVC ≥ 5% predicted, or in DLCO (Hb-corrected)
   ≥ 10% predicted, within 1 year.
3. Radiological progression, one or more of: (a) increased extent or severity of traction
   bronchiectasis/bronchiolectasis; (b) new ground-glass **with** traction bronchiectasis;
   (c) new fine reticulation; (d) increased extent or coarseness of reticulation; (e) new or
   increased honeycombing; (f) increased lobar volume loss. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]

## 4. Management

- **Probable UIP is now managed like UIP:** after multidisciplinary discussion, biopsy becomes
  less likely; IPF can be diagnosed without biopsy in the right clinical context; histologic
  UIP is confirmed in 80–85% of these cases. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]
- Assessing progression: visually, by the fraction of lung volume with fibrosis in upper,
  middle and lower thirds, comparing contiguous axial, coronal and sagittal slices side by
  side, adjusting for differences in lung volume. Quantitative CT is not yet standardized for
  routine use. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]

## 5. Pitfalls

- UIP is not synonymous with IPF: it also occurs in fibrotic HP, CTD and occupational
  exposure.
- PPF is a **behaviour**, not a diagnosis; imaging alone can show only criterion 3.
- Honeycombing not mentioned is not the same as honeycombing absent (relevant to choosing
  UIP vs probable UIP).

## 6. What the source does not say

- No acquisition protocol (slice thickness, prone, expiration): it refers to the 2018
  guideline. It only mentions deep-inspiration CT, lobular air trapping on expiration (HP) and
  contiguous-slice comparison. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]
- It cites interstitial lung abnormalities (ILA) — incidental fine reticulation, intralobular
  lines, distortion, ± ground-glass on CT done for other reasons; independent mortality risk;
  ≥ 40% progress in 4–6 years — but the ILA definition comes from another source (Fleischner
  2020 position paper, not yet added). [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Changes from 2018: four categories kept with minor wording changes; merging UIP and probable
  UIP was discussed and rejected (data from expert centres; probable may have better survival,
  slightly lower predictive value, more overlap with fibrotic HP, lower value in mild fibrosis
  and younger patients); the term **"early UIP pattern" was eliminated** (to avoid confusion
  with ILA); CT suggesting an alternative diagnosis with histologic probable UIP became
  "indeterminate for IPF" (was "not IPF") — a clinicopathologic decision. [raghu-ipf-ppf-2022]
