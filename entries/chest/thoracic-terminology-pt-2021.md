# Thoracic imaging terminology in Portuguese (Hochhegger 2021)

> Alphabetical glossary of chest radiograph, CT and MRI terms agreed by Brazilian and
> Portuguese societies, with the Brazilian term, the European Portuguese variant where it
> differs, accepted synonyms, and terms to avoid. Grouped here by theme; definitions condensed.
> The Portuguese terms are the content of the source and are kept as written.
> [hochhegger-terminology-2021]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** wording of chest radiograph, CT and MRI reports in Portuguese.
- **Not:** a classification; using the right term is not citing a criterion.

## 2. Definitions

### Anatomy

| BR term | PT variant / synonym | Short definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ácino | — | Unit distal to the terminal bronchiole (6–10 mm); seen only when filled, as an ill-defined nodular opacity. A secondary lobule has 3–25 acini | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| lóbulo pulmonar secundário | — | Smallest unit bounded by interlobular septa, polyhedral, 1.0–2.5 cm; centre with bronchiole and arteriole; not seen when normal | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| interstício | — | Supporting network: axial (bronchovascular), peripheral (subpleural + septa), intralobular; not seen when normal | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| espaços aéreos | — | Gas-exchanging portion (respiratory bronchioles, ducts, alveoli), excluding conducting airways | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| fissura | cissura (accepted) | Infolding of visceral pleura between lobes; may be incomplete or accessory | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| interface | sinal da interface | Boundary between structures of different density; irregularity suggests interstitial thickening | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| compartimentos do mediastino | — | Prevascular (anterior), visceral (middle), paravertebral (posterior), per ITMIG; defined within the entry "massa", not a separate term | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |

### Opacities and patterns

| BR term | PT variant / synonym | Short definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| opacidade | — | Generic: area denser than surroundings, no implied nature. **Not used in MRI** | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade parenquimatosa | opacificação parenquimatosa | Any increase in parenchymal attenuation, obscuring or not vessels and bronchi | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| consolidação | — | Opacity obscuring vessel and bronchial margins, except air bronchograms (defined under "opacidade parenquimatosa", no own entry) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade em vidro fosco | vidro despolido (PT) | Increased attenuation preserving vessel and bronchial margins; on MRI, high T2 signal. **Avoid on radiographs** | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade linear | — | Thin dense line; generic — prefer the specific term | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| banda parenquimatosa | — | Linear opacity 1–3 mm thick and up to 5 cm long, peripheral, often touching thickened pleura | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| linha curvilínea subpleural | — | 1–3 mm line < 1 cm from and parallel to the pleura; dependent compression, oedema or fibrosis | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade pendente | — | Dependent subpleural atelectasis; disappears with position change | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| espessamento de septos interlobulares | linhas B de Kerley (radiograph) | Peripheral lines perpendicular to pleura, 1.0–2.5 cm apart; smooth, irregular or nodular | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão reticular | — | Interlacing lines from inter/intralobular septal thickening or cyst walls | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| pavimentação em mosaico | crazy paving | Ground-glass with superimposed septal and intralobular lines; CT term | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão perilobular | opacidades/espessamento perilobular | Thicker, ill-defined curvilinear opacities around the lobule (arcade); suggests organizing pneumonia | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão nodular | — | Multiple round opacities < 3 cm; classify distribution: perilymphatic, random, centrilobular | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão nodular centrolobular | — | Nodules in the lobule centre, a few mm from pleura and fissures without touching them | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão miliar | — | Diffuse, random, uniform micronodules (< 3 mm); haematogenous spread | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão de árvore em brotamento | árvore em botão (PT) | Branching centrilobular opacities with nodular tips; filled bronchioles | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| padrão de perfusão em mosaico | — | Interspersed areas of different attenuation from small-airway or occlusive vascular disease; expiration accentuates the airway component | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| oligoemia | — | Focal, regional or diffuse reduction in blood volume | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| distorção arquitetural | — | Disorganized bronchi, vessels, fissures or septa, usually fibrosis with volume loss | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| faveolamento | favo de mel (PT) | Clustered subpleural cystic spaces, usually subcentimetric, thick-walled, with volume loss; two or three cysts within fibrosis suffice | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |

### Focal lesions and abnormal air spaces

| BR term | PT variant / synonym | Short definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| nódulo | — | Rounded focal opacity (soft tissue, fat or calcium) up to 3 cm | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| pequeno nódulo | — | Up to 10 mm mean diameter (mean of two perpendicular diameters, preferably axial); > 10 mm: give long and short diameters | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| micronódulo | — | < 3 mm; no measurement needed | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| nódulo sólido / em vidro fosco puro / parcialmente sólido | subsólido = pure ground-glass or part-solid | Solid obscures vessels; ground-glass does not; part-solid has both | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| massa | — | Expansile lesion > 3 cm (lung, pleura, mediastinum, wall) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| cavidade | — | Gas space > 1 cm within mass, nodule or consolidation, thick wall (> 2 mm) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| pseudocavidade | — | Cystic low-attenuation focus, usually < 1 cm, within nodule/mass/consolidation; associated with adenocarcinoma | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| cisto | quisto (PT) | Round, well-defined space, thin wall ≤ 2 mm, usually air-filled; ≥ 5 cysts justify investigation of cystic disease | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| bolha | — | Air space ≥ 1 cm, wall ≤ 1 mm, usually with emphysema | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| bleb / vesícula | — | Air collection < 1 cm in visceral pleura or subpleural; apical | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| pneumatocele | pneumatocelo (PT) | Cyst with wall < 1 mm that changes size rapidly | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| bola fúngica | aspergiloma (synonym) | Hyphal ball colonizing a prior cavity; mobile, air crescent | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| atelectasia | colapso (accepted) | Volume loss with increased attenuation and displacement of fissures, bronchi, vessels, diaphragm or mediastinum | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| atelectasia redonda | — | Round peripheral collapse adjacent to thickened pleura with curving vessels/bronchi (comet tail) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |

### Emphysema, airways, pleura and signs

| BR term | PT variant / synonym | Short definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| enfisema centrolobular | centroacinar | Small low-attenuation foci in lobule centre, central artery visible; upper lobes | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| enfisema parasseptal | acinar distal | Subpleural/peribronchovascular spaces separated by intact septa | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| enfisema panacinar | panlobular | Diffuse homogeneous low attenuation with vascular pruning, lower lobes | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| enfisema bolhoso | — | Emphysema dominated by a large bulla; **giant** if ≥ 1/3 of the hemithorax | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| aprisionamento aéreo | aprisionamento gasoso (PT) | On expiration, attenuation lower than expected without volume reduction | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| broncocele | broncocelo (PT) | Dilated bronchus filled with secretion ("finger-in-glove") | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| bronquiectasia | cilíndrica / varicosa / cística | Irreversible dilatation: lumen larger than adjacent artery, lack of tapering > 2 cm beyond bifurcation, or bronchus < 1 cm from pleura | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| bronquiolectasia | — | Dilated bronchioles; inflammatory (reversible) or, more often, traction (fibrosis) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| placa pleural | — | Focal dense connective-tissue pleural thickening, may calcify | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| pneumotórax | — | Air in the pleural space; > 2 cm between pleura and lung = large | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| sinal do halo / halo invertido | SHI | Ground-glass around a nodule / central ground-glass with a consolidation ring (organizing pneumonia most common) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| sinal do anel de sinete | anel de pérola | Dilated bronchus beside a smaller artery | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| sinal do septo nodular | em contas, em rosário | Thickened septum with nodularity (lymphangitis or sarcoidosis) | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### Terms the consensus advises against

| Avoid | Use instead | Reason | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| infiltrado | opacidade, or the specific term | Imprecise; means different things to different readers; replace with "opacidade" | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| atenuação em mosaico | "opacidade em vidro fosco não homogênea" (use "padrão de perfusão em mosaico" only when it is truly perfusion) | Confused with mosaic perfusion | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| micetoma (for fungus ball) | bola fúngica (aspergiloma) | Mycetoma is a chronic subcutaneous infection | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade linear (generic) | banda parenquimatosa, atelectasia linear, espessamento septal | Precision recommendation | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| vidro fosco on radiographs | opacidade mal definida / tênue | Superimposition misleads | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |
| opacidade on MRI | describe the signal | X-ray attenuation term | [hochhegger-terminology-2021] |

## 4. Management

Not applicable (terminology).

## 5. Pitfalls

- Internal inconsistency: the "padrão nodular" entry calls nodules < 1 cm "micronódulo",
  while the "micronódulo" entry defines < 3 mm. The specific entry (< 3 mm) is the consistent
  reading. [hochhegger-terminology-2021]
- "Atenuação em mosaico" is still used in other guidelines (e.g. the 2022 IPF guideline uses
  "mosaic attenuation" as a sign of hypersensitivity pneumonitis); replacing it can change the
  meaning.

## 6. What the source does not say

- "Consolidação" has no own entry; it is defined within "opacidade parenquimatosa".
- It does not grade fibrosis or assign CT patterns (see `ipf-ppf-ct-patterns-2022`).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- English-language reference: the source cites the Fleischner glossary of 2008 (Hansell); an
  updated Fleischner glossary was published in 2024 (Bankier et al., Radiology). Neither is in
  radref yet.
