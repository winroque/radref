# ACR TI-RADS (2017)

> Point-based risk stratification of thyroid nodules on ultrasound. Points from five feature
> groups are added to give a level (TR1–TR5); the level and the nodule's maximum diameter
> determine whether fine-needle aspiration (FNA) or follow-up is recommended. [acr-tirads-2017]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** thyroid nodules seen on dedicated thyroid ultrasound. [acr-tirads-2017]
- The system assigns levels to **nodules**; it gives no category for a gland without nodules.
- Guidance for adults; "should not be construed as standards". [acr-tirads-2017]
- Incidental nodules seen on CT/MRI/PET or on non-thyroid ultrasound are triaged first by the
  incidental thyroid nodule white paper (`acr-incidental-thyroid-2015`).

## 2. Definitions — points

Choose **one** option in each of composition, echogenicity, shape and margin; add **all**
that apply in echogenic foci. [acr-tirads-2017]

| Feature | Option | Points | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition | Cystic or almost completely cystic | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Spongiform (predominantly, > 50%, small cystic spaces) | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017, Fig. 1] |
| | Mixed cystic and solid | 1 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Solid or almost completely solid | 2 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| Echogenicity | Anechoic | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Hyperechoic or isoechoic | 1 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Hypoechoic | 2 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Very hypoechoic (more hypoechoic than the strap muscles) | 3 | [acr-tirads-2017, Fig. 1] |
| Shape | Wider-than-tall | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Taller-than-wide | 3 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| Margin | Smooth | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Ill-defined | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Lobulated or irregular | 2 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Extra-thyroidal extension | 3 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| Echogenic foci | None or large comet-tail artefacts | 0 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Macrocalcifications | 1 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Peripheral (rim) calcifications | 2 | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| | Punctate echogenic foci | 3 | [acr-tirads-2017] |

### Scoring rules

- Spongiform nodules receive no additional points from other groups.
- Mixed cystic and solid: score the predominant solid component.
- Composition cannot be determined because of calcification: 2 points.
- Echogenicity cannot be determined: 1 point.
- Margin cannot be determined: 0 points.
- Only TR1 nodules can score 0; any other nodule scores at least 2 (a mixed nodule gets 1 for
  composition plus at least 1 for the echogenicity of its solid component).

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Points | Level | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | TR1 — benign (expected cancer risk ≤ 2% for TR1–TR2) | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| 2 | TR2 — not suspicious | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| 3 | TR3 — mildly suspicious (≈ 5%) | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| 4–6 | TR4 — moderately suspicious (5–20%) | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| ≥ 7 | TR5 — highly suspicious (≥ 20%) | [acr-tirads-2017] |

There is no TR0 and no sub-levels.

## 4. Management

| Level | FNA if maximum diameter | Follow-up if | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TR1 | No FNA | — | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| TR2 | No FNA | — | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| TR3 | ≥ 2.5 cm | ≥ 1.5 cm | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| TR4 | ≥ 1.5 cm | ≥ 1.0 cm | [acr-tirads-2017] |
| TR5 | ≥ 1.0 cm | ≥ 0.5 cm | [acr-tirads-2017] |

- TR5 nodules of 5–9 mm: routine biopsy of nodules < 1 cm is not recommended, but FNA may be
  appropriate in certain circumstances (active surveillance/ablation/lobectomy programmes);
  shared decision between referring physician and patient. The report should say whether the
  nodule can be measured reproducibly and whether it abuts the trachea or lies next to the
  tracheoesophageal groove. [acr-tirads-2017]
- **Follow-up timing** (nodules below the FNA threshold): TR5 yearly up to 5 years; TR4 at 1,
  2, 3 and 5 years; TR3 at 1, 3 and 5 years; intervals < 1 year not warranted (except proven
  cancer under surveillance); stop at 5 years if unchanged; if the TR level increases, next
  scan in 1 year. [acr-tirads-2017]
- **Significant growth:** ≥ 20% increase in at least two dimensions with a minimum increase of
  2 mm, or ≥ 50% increase in volume; compare with earlier scans, not only the last one.
  [acr-tirads-2017]
- **How many nodules:** biopsy no more than **two** nodules with the highest point totals
  that meet FNA criteria (size is not the primary criterion); follow and report no more than
  **four** nodules with the highest scores below the FNA threshold. [acr-tirads-2017]
- Recommendations are guidance, not a standard; the decision to biopsy also weighs the
  referrer's and patient's preferences and patient factors. [acr-tirads-2017]
- **Cervical lymph nodes** are assessed in every exam; FNA of a suspicious node is recommended in
  addition to the (up to two) nodules. Suspicious signs: rounded shape, loss of the fatty hilum,
  peripheral flow, cystic component, punctate echogenic foci. [acr-tirads-2017, p. 593]
- Follow-up probably continues beyond 5 years if a nodule grows significantly but stays below
  the FNA threshold; be cautious calling minimal extrathyroidal extension.
  [acr-tirads-2017]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Confusing "not described" with "cannot be determined": the system gives default points when a
  feature cannot be determined (composition obscured by calcification = 2; echogenicity = 1;
  margin = 0) and aims to classify every nodule. Leaving the level open when a feature was
  simply not described in a report is a reporting convention, not a rule of the source.
  [acr-tirads-2017, Fig. 1]
- Adding spongiform points to other groups.
- Forgetting that echogenic foci points are cumulative.
- Taller-than-wide measured on a longitudinal image: assess on a **transverse** image (height
  parallel, width perpendicular to the beam). [acr-tirads-2017, Fig. 1]
- Large comet-tail artefacts (V-shaped, > 1 mm, in cystic components) score 0; small
  comet-tails may accompany punctate echogenic foci (3). [acr-tirads-2017, Fig. 1]

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not aim to detect every thyroid cancer: the size thresholds deliberately balance
  detection of clinically important cancers against biopsy of benign nodules and indolent
  cancers (overdiagnosis is a stated concern). [acr-tirads-2017]
- It does not give a category to a gland without nodules.
- The committee did not address follow-up of nodules that have already had FNA.
  [acr-tirads-2017]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Other systems (e.g. ATA 2015, EU-TIRADS, K-TIRADS) use pattern-based categories and different
  FNA thresholds; not summarized here (no entries yet).
