# Incidental thyroid nodules on imaging (ACR white paper, 2015)

> Which thyroid nodules found incidentally on CT, MRI, nuclear medicine or non-thyroid
> ultrasound should go to dedicated thyroid ultrasound, based on suspicious features, age and
> size. Guidance, not a standard of care. [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** nodules not detected or suspected clinically, found on CT, MRI, nuclear
  medicine (FDG-PET, MIBI, octreotide) or ultrasound of extrathyroidal structures (carotids,
  salivary glands, cervical nodes, parathyroids, neck masses). [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]
- **Does not apply to:** patients at increased risk of thyroid cancer, patients with symptoms
  of thyroid disease, **children**. Dedicated thyroid US done for symptoms or a palpable nodule
  is not incidental. [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]
- Severe comorbidity or limited life expectancy without suspicious features: no work-up unless
  requested or clinically indicated. [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Size | Measured in the **axial plane** | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| Suspicious features (CT/MRI) | Invasion of local tissues and/or abnormal lymph nodes (calcification, cystic component, increased enhancement). Size is non-specific, but work-up may be considered with ipsilateral node **> 1.5 cm** short axis (jugulodigastric) or **> 1 cm** (other levels); levels IV and VI most suspicious | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| Suspicious features (extrathyroidal US) | Microcalcifications, marked hypoechogenicity, lobulated/irregular margins, taller-than-wide on transverse | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

### CT and MRI

| Situation | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Suspicious features (any size) | Thyroid US (consider FNA) | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| No suspicious features, **< 35 years**, nodule **< 1 cm** | Nothing | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| No suspicious features, **< 35 years**, nodule **≥ 1 cm** | Thyroid US | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| No suspicious features, **≥ 35 years**, nodule **< 1.5 cm** | Nothing | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| No suspicious features, **≥ 35 years**, nodule **≥ 1.5 cm** | Thyroid US | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |

Multiple nodules: apply to the **largest**. Incidental enlarged heterogeneous gland: US if no
serious comorbidity. CT/MRI have no reliable features of benignity or malignancy in the nodule
itself. [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]

### Nuclear medicine

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Focal** FDG uptake (with or without CT nodule) | US and FNA of the focus, **regardless** of US appearance (malignancy ~1 in 3) | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| Diffuse uptake without mass | Not focal; this branch does not apply | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| Uptake on MIBI or octreotide | Thyroid US; FNA according to US | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |

### Ultrasound of extrathyroidal structures

| Finding | Recommendation | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Suspicious features | Dedicated thyroid US | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |
| No suspicious features | Same age/size cut-offs as CT (< 35 y ≥ 1 cm; ≥ 35 y ≥ 1.5 cm) | [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015] |

## 4. Management

After thyroid US, FNA and follow-up follow the US findings (e.g. ACR TI-RADS).

## 5. Pitfalls

- Applying the CT cut-offs to a focal FDG-avid nodule (FDG focus goes to US/FNA regardless).
- Applying the paper to children.

## 6. What the source does not say

- The paper recommends **not** mentioning in the impression, nor recommending work-up for, a
  nodule that does not meet criteria; whether to mention it in the body is left to the
  radiologist. [acr-thyroid-incidental-2015]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Dedicated thyroid US is scored with `acr-tirads-2017` (published after this paper).
