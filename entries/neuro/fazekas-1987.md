# Fazekas scale (1987)

> Two **separate** 0–3 grades for T2 white matter hyperintensities: periventricular (PVH) and
> deep white matter (DWMH). [fazekas-1987]

## 1. Scope

- **Original:** T2-weighted MRI at 1.5 T in Alzheimer's dementia and normal ageing.
  [fazekas-1987] (Current practice uses T2/FLAIR.)

## 2. Definitions

- **PVH:** periventricular hyperintensity. **DWMH:** deep white matter hyperintensity.

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Grade | Periventricular (PVH) | Deep white matter (DWMH) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Absent | Absent | [fazekas-1987] |
| 1 | "Caps" or pencil-thin lining | Punctate foci | [fazekas-1987] |
| 2 | Smooth "halo" | Beginning confluence of foci | [fazekas-1987] |
| 3 | Irregular PVH extending into deep white matter | Large confluent areas | [fazekas-1987] |

## 4. Management

Not applicable. In the article, punctate or early-confluent DWMH occurred in ~60% of both
Alzheimer patients and controls, unrelated to hypertension or other vascular risk factors.
[fazekas-1987]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Reporting "Fazekas N" without saying which of the two scales. In common usage a single number
  usually refers to deep white matter, but this is a convention, not the article.

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not define a single combined Fazekas grade.
- It does not attribute the lesions to small-vessel disease ("presumed vascular origin" is
  later terminology, e.g. STRIVE).

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- STRIVE standards for small-vessel disease terminology — not yet added.
