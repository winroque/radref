# ASPECTS (Barber 2000)

> The middle cerebral artery (MCA) territory is divided into **10 regions** on two axial
> non-contrast CT levels. Start at 10 and subtract 1 for each region with early ischaemic change.
> 10 = normal; 0 = diffuse ischaemia of the whole MCA territory. In the original cohort a score
> **≤ 7** sharply separated independence from dependence or death at 3 months. [aspects-2000]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** non-contrast CT in hyperacute anterior-circulation (MCA) ischaemic stroke.
- **Does not apply to:** posterior circulation or anterior cerebral artery territory.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Levels | (1) thalamus and basal ganglia; (2) just rostral to the ganglionic structures | [aspects-2000] |
| Regions (10 points) | Subcortical 3: caudate (C), lentiform (L), internal capsule (IC). Cortical 7: insular ribbon (I), M1 anterior MCA cortex, M2 cortex lateral to the insular ribbon, M3 posterior MCA cortex (ganglionic level); M4, M5, M6 anterior, lateral and posterior MCA territories immediately above M1–M3 (supraganglionic level) | [aspects-2000, Fig. 1] |
| Early ischaemic change | Focal swelling (focal narrowing of CSF spaces — sulcal effacement or ventricular compression) or parenchymal hypoattenuation relative to the same structure elsewhere or the contralateral hemisphere | [aspects-2000] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score | Meaning in the source | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Normal CT | [aspects-2000] |
| ≤ 7 | Predicted dependence or death (sensitivity 0.78, specificity 0.96) and symptomatic intracerebral haemorrhage (0.90 / 0.62) after IV alteplase; odds of symptomatic ICH about 14 times that of scores > 7 (OR 14, CI 1.8–117); sICH 1% with scores > 7 | [aspects-2000, Table 1; Discussion] |
| 0 | Diffuse ischaemia throughout the MCA territory | [aspects-2000] |

- Cohort: 156 patients treated with IV alteplase within 3 h; CT with 10-mm slices; median
  baseline ASPECTS 8. Readers knew the affected side but were blind to NIHSS and outcome.
  Between-observer agreement for ASPECTS ≤ 7 vs > 7 (side known) κ 0.71–0.89; within-observer
  κ 0.67–0.82. Model modifiers: age > 78 years (functional outcome) and glucose > 10 mmol/L
  (sICH). [aspects-2000, Table 2; Results]
- Source inconsistency: in Table 1 the n labels look swapped (≤ 7 shown with n = 89, > 7 with
  n = 65); the text says 65 had ≤ 7 and 89 had > 7.

## 4. Management

- The authors explicitly warn that ASPECTS ≤ 7 should **not** be used to exclude patients from
  thrombolysis on the basis of this study (no untreated comparison). [aspects-2000]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Scoring without knowing the symptomatic side lowers reliability (the study scored with the side
  known).
- Counting old infarcts or chronic small-vessel changes as early ischaemia (not addressed in the
  source; describe separately).

## 6. What the source does not say

- No thresholds for thrombectomy selection (later trials, not in this paper).
- No CT-angiography or MRI (DWI-ASPECTS) version.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Later trials used ASPECTS cut-offs (for example ≥ 6) for thrombectomy eligibility; not covered
  here.
