# STRIVE-2 (2023) — small vessel disease on MRI

> STRIVE-2 keeps the STRIVE-1 terms, describes what is seen rather than presumed mechanisms, and
> adds **cortical cerebral microinfarct** and the **summary SVD score**. It is a research standard
> (the focus is neuroimaging features and research use, not clinical management); the authors
> hope it helps bring the features into clinical practice. Read it together with STRIVE-1. [strive-2-2023]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** MRI description of cerebral small vessel disease (SVD) in research and clinical
  practice.
- **Does not give:** severity cut-offs for clinical decisions (it points to validated visual
  scales such as Fazekas and ARWMC for WMH; Table 2). [strive-2-2023, Table 2]

## 2. Definitions (panel and Fig. 3, summarized)

| Term | Definition | Usual size | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recent small subcortical infarct | Recent infarct (within about 3 weeks) in the territory of one perforating arteriole, with consistent imaging and symptoms; best seen on DWI | ≤ 20 mm axial | [strive-2-2023] |
| Lacune (of presumed vascular origin) | Round/ovoid subcortical cavity with CSF-like signal, likely end-stage of a small infarct, small haemorrhage, incidental DWI lesion or cavitated WMH | 3–15 mm (cavities < 3 mm can also follow recent infarcts) | [strive-2-2023] |
| White matter hyperintensity (of presumed vascular origin) | T2/FLAIR-hyperintense white matter signal without cavitation; if deep grey matter and brainstem lesions are included, call them "subcortical hyperintensities" | Variable | [strive-2-2023] |
| Perivascular space | CSF-like signal following a penetrating vessel; round, ovoid or linear | Commonly ≤ 2 mm (usually < 3 mm) | [strive-2-2023] |
| Cerebral microbleed | Small signal void with blooming on T2*/SWI | Usually 2–5 mm, up to 10 mm | [strive-2-2023] |
| Cortical superficial siderosis | Thin T2*/SWI hypointensity in or over the superficial cortex | Variable | [strive-2-2023] |
| Cortical cerebral microinfarct (new) | Strictly cortical lesion, typically wedge-shaped, T1-hypo, T2/FLAIR-hyper, isointense on T2* | < 4 mm (detectable 0.5–4.0 mm) | [strive-2-2023] |
| Brain atrophy | Volume loss not due to a focal macroscopic injury | — | [strive-2-2023] |
| Incidental DWI-positive lesion (emerging term) | DWI-hyperintense lesion found incidentally; not yet an established marker | ≤ 20 mm axial | [strive-2-2023] |
| Summary SVD score (new) | Grouping of accepted SVD markers into one index | — | [strive-2-2023] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

- A lacune at least doubles the future risk of stroke in people without previous stroke.
  [strive-2-2023]
- Lacunes and perivascular spaces cannot be separated by size or rim alone; use shape, location,
  colocated perivascular spaces and surrounding signal. [strive-2-2023]
- Minimum field strength 1.5 T; 3 T preferred for research protocols. [strive-2-2023, Table 1 footnote]
- About 15% of recent small subcortical infarcts have no intrinsic small-vessel cause (embolism or
  parent-artery atheroma). [strive-2-2023]

## 4. Management

- Not a management guideline.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Calling a recent small subcortical infarct a "lacune" before it cavitates.
- Using "infarct" for a lacune without evidence of a vascular insult (the panel advises against
  it); "of presumed vascular origin" is for when a vascular insult is reasonably certain.
- Counting enlarged perivascular spaces as lacunes.

## 6. What the source does not say

- No numeric burden thresholds for reporting (e.g. how many microbleeds are "many"). It states
  that variation between populations prevents age-stratified normative WMH data.
- The appendix (unusual appearances, boundaries between features) was not read for this entry.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Update of STRIVE-1 (Wardlaw 2013), which should be used alongside it.
