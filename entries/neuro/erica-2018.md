# ERICA score (Enkirch 2018)

> Visual 0–3 rating of entorhinal cortex atrophy on coronal 3D T1 MRI, centred on the
> "tentorial cleft sign" (detachment of the entorhinal cortex from the cerebellar tentorium).
> [enkirch-erica-2018]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** coronal 3D T1 (1 mm) aligned with the brainstem; each hemisphere separately.
  [enkirch-erica-2018]

## 2. Definitions

- **Tentorial cleft sign:** a cleft between the entorhinal cortex and the cerebellar tentorium.
  [enkirch-erica-2018]

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Normal volume of entorhinal cortex and parahippocampal gyrus | [enkirch-erica-2018] |
| 1 | Mild atrophy, widened collateral sulcus | [enkirch-erica-2018] |
| 2 | Moderate atrophy, entorhinal cortex detached from the tentorium (tentorial cleft sign) | [enkirch-erica-2018] |
| 3 | Severe atrophy of the parahippocampal gyrus, wide cleft between entorhinal cortex and tentorium | [enkirch-erica-2018] |

## 4. Management

Not applicable. Pathologic cut-off: **≥ 2** indicates probable Alzheimer's disease — in the
study, accuracy 91% (sensitivity 83%, specificity 98%) against subjective cognitive decline,
higher than MTA (74%). [enkirch-erica-2018]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Applying the cut-off outside the study's comparison (AD vs subjective cognitive decline).

## 6. What the source does not say

- No age-specific normal values.
