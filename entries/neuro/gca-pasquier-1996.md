# Cerebral atrophy rating (Pasquier 1996) and the "GCA 0–3" simplification

> Reproducibility study of visual cerebral atrophy assessment on axial T2 MRI in patients with
> hemispheric infarcts. Thirteen regions are rated 0–3 and summed (0–39). The single global
> 0–3 grade used in practice ("GCA") is a simplification of this scale, conventionally cited
> to this article. [pasquier-gca-1996]

## 1. Scope

- **Original:** axial spin-echo T2 MRI at 0.5 T (6-mm sections, 1-mm gap), 2–15 days after a
  hemispheric ischaemic stroke; n = 50, median age 50 years (19–81). [pasquier-gca-1996]
  (The PDF is image-only; content was checked by OCR.)

## 2. Definitions

Thirteen regions: sulcal dilatation — frontal, parieto-occipital, temporal (right and left);
ventricular dilatation — frontal, occipital and temporal horns (right and left) and third
ventricle. [pasquier-gca-1996]

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score (per region) | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Absent | [pasquier-gca-1996] |
| 1 | Mild | [pasquier-gca-1996] |
| 2 | Moderate | [pasquier-gca-1996] |
| 3 | Severe | [pasquier-gca-1996] |

Sum of 13 regions = cerebral atrophy score, 0–39. [pasquier-gca-1996]

## 4. Management

Not applicable.

## 5. Pitfalls

- Saying the article defined a single global 0–3 score: it defined regional scores and a sum.
- Changing readers between exams: interobserver agreement was moderate (overall κ 0.48 in the
  first session, 0.67 in the second; intraobserver κ 0.65); the authors recommend the same
  reader for follow-up. [pasquier-gca-1996]
- Temporal horns are poorly assessed on the axial plane used. [pasquier-gca-1996, Discussion]

## 6. What the source does not say

- It does not define the global "GCA 0–3" grade as such, nor age-specific normal values.
- It was not developed in a dementia population.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- Later dementia literature uses the simplified global 0–3 grade. Koedam 2011 cites for it
  Scheltens P, Pasquier F, Weerts JG, et al. Eur Neurol 1997;37:95–99 (dementia and normal
  ageing); not yet read or added. The 1996 article itself mentions a companion article in the
  general population.
