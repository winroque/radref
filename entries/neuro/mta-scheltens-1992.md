# MTA scale (Scheltens 1992)

> Visual 0–4 rating of medial temporal lobe atrophy on coronal T1 MRI, from the width of the
> choroid fissure, the width of the temporal horn and the height of the hippocampal formation.
> Rated separately for each side. [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** coronal T1 MRI, oblique plane parallel to the long axis of the brainstem, on
  the slice that best shows both hippocampal formations. [scheltens-mta-1992]
- Original population: probable Alzheimer's disease and normal ageing.

## 2. Definitions

↑ = widening; ↓ = decrease. Right and left rated separately. [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score | Choroid fissure | Temporal horn | Hippocampal height | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Normal | Normal | Normal | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 1 | ↑ | Normal | Normal | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 2 | ↑↑ | ↑ | ↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 3 | ↑↑↑ | ↑↑ | ↓↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 4 | ↑↑↑ | ↑↑↑ | ↓↓↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |

## 4. Management

Not applicable. In the study, 81% of Alzheimer patients scored 2–4 and 67% of controls scored
0–1. [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Score 2 already includes reduced hippocampal height; describing hippocampi as "normal" with
  MTA 2 is internally inconsistent.

## 6. What the source does not say

- **Age-specific cut-offs** (e.g. abnormal ≥ 2 below 75 years, ≥ 3 from 75) are **not in this
  article**; they come from later work (not yet added). Do not cite Scheltens 1992 for them.

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- ERICA (`erica-2018`) rates entorhinal cortex atrophy and was more accurate than MTA in its
  validation study.
