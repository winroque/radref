# MTA scale (Scheltens 1992)

> Visual 0–4 rating of medial temporal lobe atrophy on coronal T1 MRI, from the width of the
> choroid fissure, the width of the temporal horn and the height of the hippocampal formation
> (hippocampus and parahippocampal gyrus). [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** T1 MRI in an oblique coronal plane parallel to the brainstem axis. In the
  study, six oblique slices (5 mm, 1-mm gap) were planned from a midsagittal scout, the first
  directly adjacent to the brainstem, at 0.5–0.6 T; atrophy was judged on all six slices.
  [scheltens-mta-1992]
- Original population: 21 patients with probable Alzheimer's disease and 21 healthy controls
  (mean age of controls 70.9 years). [scheltens-mta-1992]
- How the scale was built: five blinded readers ranked all 42 scans from absent to severe
  atrophy and pooled them into five groups (0–4); Table 1 was written afterwards to let others
  apply it. [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 2. Definitions

↑ = increase; ↓ = decrease; N = normal (Table 1). Hippocampal formation = dentate gyrus,
hippocampus proper and subiculum together with the parahippocampal gyrus. [scheltens-mta-1992]

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Score | Choroid fissure | Temporal horn | Hippocampal height | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Normal | Normal | Normal | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 1 | ↑ | Normal | Normal | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 2 | ↑↑ | ↑ | ↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 3 | ↑↑↑ | ↑↑ | ↓↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |
| 4 | ↑↑↑ | ↑↑↑ | ↓↓↓ | [scheltens-mta-1992] |

## 4. Management

Not applicable. In the study, 17/21 (81%) Alzheimer patients scored 2–4 and 14/21 (67%) controls
scored 0–1; no control scored 4. In a regression, the score depended on age as well as on
diagnosis; it correlated with age in controls (not in patients), and six of the seven controls
scoring 2 or 3 were aged over 77 — discrimination is poorer in older subjects.
[scheltens-mta-1992, Fig. 5, Table 4]

## 5. Pitfalls

- Score 2 already includes reduced hippocampal height; describing hippocampi as "normal" with
  MTA 2 is internally inconsistent.

## 6. What the source does not say

- **A separate score for each side.** The 1992 score is one score per subject; only the linear
  measurements (A–D) were taken left and right. Rating each hemisphere separately is later
  practice.
- **Age-specific cut-offs** (e.g. abnormal ≥ 2 below 75 years, ≥ 3 from 75) are **not in this
  article**; they come from later work (not yet added). Do not cite Scheltens 1992 for them.
  The article's Discussion does say the rating would be useful "in subjects younger than 75
  years", and reports sensitivity 81% and specificity 67%. [mta-scheltens-1992, Discussion]

## 7. Disagreements and newer versions

- ERICA (`erica-2018`) rates entorhinal cortex atrophy and was more accurate than MTA in its
  validation study.
