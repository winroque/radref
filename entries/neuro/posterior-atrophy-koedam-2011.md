# Posterior atrophy scale (Koedam 2011)

> Four-point (0–3) visual rating of posterior (parietal) atrophy on MRI, scored separately for
> left and right from sagittal, axial and coronal images; the highest score across
> orientations is taken. Developed in a memory clinic to help distinguish Alzheimer's disease
> (AD) — particularly early-onset AD — from controls and other dementias. [koedam-posterior-2011]

## 1. Scope

- **Development population:** 118 memory clinic patients (60 AD — 35 early-onset < 65 years,
  25 late-onset; 20 other dementias — 10 FTLD, 10 DLB; 38 controls with subjective memory
  complaints). Vascular dementia was excluded. [koedam-posterior-2011]
- **Imaging in the study:** 3.0 T; sagittal 3D T1 (1 mm) with sagittal and oblique-coronal
  MPR; 3D FLAIR with axial MPR. Sagittal and coronal ratings on T1 MPR; axial ratings on FLAIR
  MPR. [koedam-posterior-2011]
- Applicability to CT was **not** studied (raised as future work). [koedam-posterior-2011]

## 2. Definitions

Landmarks rated in each orientation: [koedam-posterior-2011]

| Orientation | What is assessed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sagittal (paramedian) | Widening of the posterior cingulate sulcus and the parieto-occipital sulcus; atrophy of the precuneus | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| Axial | Widening of the posterior cingulate sulcus; sulcal dilatation in the parietal lobes | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| Coronal | Widening of the posterior cingulate sulcus; parietal lobes | [koedam-posterior-2011] |

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Grade | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No atrophy: closed posterior cingulate and parieto-occipital sulci; closed sulci of the parietal lobes and precuneus | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| 1 | Mild widening of the posterior cingulate and parieto-occipital sulci, with mild atrophy of the parietal lobes and precuneus (Methods also phrase it as "mild widening of the sulci without evident volume loss of the gyri") | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| 2 | Substantial widening of the posterior cingulate and parieto-occipital sulci, with substantial atrophy (volume loss of the gyri) of the parietal lobes and precuneus | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| 3 | End-stage atrophy: evident widening of both sulci and knife-blade atrophy of the parietal lobes and precuneus | [koedam-posterior-2011] |

- Scored for **left and right separately**. If orientations disagree (e.g. 1 on sagittal, 2 on
  axial), the **highest** score is used. [koedam-posterior-2011]
- In the analysis, left and right were averaged; an **average score > 1** was considered
  abnormal (the same dichotomy was applied to MTA). [koedam-posterior-2011]

## 4. Management

Not applicable. Study results (rater 1 scores): [koedam-posterior-2011]

| Measure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mean PA score | AD 1.6 ± 0.9; controls 0.6 ± 0.7; other dementias 0.8 ± 0.8 (AD vs each, p < 0.01) | [koedam-posterior-2011, Table 1] |
| Intra-rater agreement (weighted κ) | 0.93 and 0.95 | [koedam-posterior-2011, Table 2] |
| Inter-rater agreement (weighted κ) | 0.65–0.84 (mean 0.73) | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| AD vs controls, PA > 1 | Sensitivity 58%, specificity 95% (MTA > 1: 45% / 92%; MTA or PA: 73% / 87%) | [koedam-posterior-2011] |
| PA and age | Not associated (B = 1.1, SE 0.8), unlike MTA (B = 3.1, SE 0.7) | [koedam-posterior-2011, Table 3] |
| Prominent PA without MTA | 28% of AD; in early-onset AD, 34% had prominent PA vs 14% prominent MTA | [koedam-posterior-2011] |

## 5. Pitfalls

- Using a single orientation: the score is the highest across sagittal, axial and coronal.
- Reading normal PA as reassurance against AD: sensitivity was 58% in the development study.
- Treating the > 1 cut-off as validated in other populations or at 1.5 T/CT: it comes from one
  3.0 T memory-clinic cohort. The AD cohort was relatively young; the authors advise caution when
  generalizing to older patients. [koedam-posterior-2011]
- PA was associated with MMSE independently of MTA (B −1.8). [koedam-posterior-2011, Table 3]

## 6. What the source does not say

- No age-specific normal values (PA was not associated with age in this cohort).
- No validation on CT or at lower field strength.
- Vascular dementia was not included.
