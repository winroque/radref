# Posterior atrophy scale (Koedam 2011)

> Visual 0–3 rating of parietal (posterior) atrophy, assessed on sagittal, axial and coronal
> MRI, developed because posterior atrophy is a feature of Alzheimer's disease, notably
> early-onset. [koedam-posterior-2011] [verify]

## 1. Scope

- **Applies to:** MRI in the evaluation of dementia; rating per hemisphere (left/right) using
  three orientations. [verify]

## 2. Definitions

Structures assessed: posterior cingulate sulcus, parieto-occipital sulcus, precuneus, parietal
lobe sulci and gyri. [verify]

## 3. Criteria / categories

| Grade | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Closed sulci; no gyral atrophy | [koedam-posterior-2011] [verify] |
| 1 | Mild widening of posterior cingulate and parieto-occipital sulci; mild parietal and precuneus atrophy | [koedam-posterior-2011] [verify] |
| 2 | Substantial widening of those sulci; substantial parietal and precuneus atrophy | [koedam-posterior-2011] [verify] |
| 3 | End-stage atrophy: wide sulci, "knife-blade" gyri | [koedam-posterior-2011] [verify] |
